# The Spiral Stair Room

Up: [[The Mill Sector]]

**Floor 1, Room 8 — a long corridor on the Vestibule-to-stairs route that bulges at its middle into a shaft: the ceiling opens up into darkness and an iron spiral staircase climbs into it. Both ends of the stair are sealed for now — a vertical route the party can see but can't yet use.**

> [!danger] DM-only
> Spoiler room. On the main path from the [[The Bell Junction|Bell Junction]] (7) toward the Floor-2 down-stairs; the spiral stair is a dormant vertical connection (up to the mill grounds, down to Floor 2).

## Layout
Through the archway from [[The Bell Junction]] (7), a turn to the right opens a **long corridor** that **bulges outward at its middle**. In that middle bay the **ceiling lifts away into darkness**, and an **iron spiral staircase** rises up into it. Past the bulge the corridor continues on toward Room 9 (the Pit Room) and the Floor-2 route.

## The spiral stair — a sealed vertical shaft (discovered, NOT read aloud)
Both ends of the stair are locked, so right now it's a **tease, not a route** — a Stretch-worth of investigation that pays in intel and forward hooks rather than immediate passage.
- **Top hatch — the sealed mill-grounds exit.** Climbing the stair up into the dark reaches a **locked metal hatch** in the ceiling. The **old iron key from the [[The Expedition Post|Expedition Post]] footlocker (room 11) fits the keyhole but will not turn** — it's for a *different but similar* lock. So the way up (to a new, unexplored part of the mill grounds above) is visible and tantalizing but shut, pending the *right* key. (This is the roster's "sealed Mill-grounds exit.")
- **Bottom hatch — down to the next floor, locked from below.** At the **foot** of the stair, a **similar hatch in the floor** appears to drop to a matching spiral stair one floor down (Floor 2). It's **locked from the other side** — these hatches only unlock **from underneath** — and there's **nothing to grip** to haul it up from here anyway. Unusable from this side; a door that can only ever be *opened from below*.

Net: the party learns there's a shaft threading up to the mill grounds and down to Floor 2, that their current key is *almost but not quite* the answer up top, and that the down-hatch is a one-way-from-elsewhere door — three durable hooks, no immediate passage.

## Stretch-worth
Climbing and examining the stair, trying the key in the top hatch, and working the bottom hatch are all Stretch Activities; the payoff for now is knowledge and forward hooks (the *right* key, the shaft, the from-below-only down-hatch) rather than loot or a shortcut. See [[Dungeon Mechanics]].
