# The Spiral Stair Room

Up: [[The Mill Sector]]

**Floor 1, Room 8 — a long corridor on the Vestibule-to-stairs route that bulges at its middle into a shaft: the ceiling opens up into darkness and an iron spiral staircase climbs into it, with catwalks roughly every 20 feet. Home of [[The Siren]] and [[Wesley's Sigils|Wesley's sigil]] through [[Episode 3 — Second Shift]] — both gone as of [[Session 8 — Second Shift|Session 8]]. The top hatch is now the party's one-way exit to [[The Debrief Station|Mercer's cabin]].**

> [!danger] DM-only
> Spoiler room. On the main path from the [[The Bell Junction|Bell Junction]] (7) toward the Floor-2 down-stairs. The spiral stair is a vertical connection: up to the cabin in the woods, down to Floor 2.

## Layout
Through the archway from [[The Bell Junction]] (7), a turn to the right opens a **long corridor** that **bulges outward at its middle**. In that middle bay the **ceiling lifts away into darkness**, and an **iron spiral staircase** rises up into it. Past the bulge the corridor continues north toward Room 9 ([[The Pit Room]]) and the Floor-2 route. *(A route the party has now used: Bell Junction → here → north to the Pit Room.)*

**Vertical geometry, as run in Sessions 7–8:** catwalks and rafters at roughly **20-ft intervals**; going around the stair to the next level costs **15 ft of movement**; the stair is incomplete in places. Falls are **1d6 per 10 ft.** The room was lit **green** by the sigil while it was live.

## The sigil — cleared

[[Wesley's Sigils|Wesley's sigil]] sat on the floor beside the foot of the stair, binding [[The Siren]] and two harpies. **It cannot be defaced** (ink nothing; warhammer a green flash and a bounce). **The harpies died in Session 7; the Siren in Session 8, and the green went out.** What's left is **plain white chalk** — *"like graffiti, or a hopscotch."*

> [!note] What the dead chalk still offers
> Chalk persists through the reset; footprints don't. The circle itself — **compass-steady, ten feet, a human hand** — is still there to be examined. The Ep3 design's tally marks with a fresh stroke can be here too; the boot prints and spent torch could not have survived. **Not yet examined by the party.**

## ⭐ The loose brick — [[The Stone of Quietude]]

In one wall near the stair, **a loose brick over a secret compartment.** [[Gern]] found it in Session 8 by lining the room up against **his Marv's Spot photograph**, which shows a man putting *"a quiet rock"* back into a brick at the edge of this staircase. No check required once the photo was matched. **The Stone is now Gern's.**

## The top hatch — exit to the cabin

A locked manhole-style hatch at the very top of the stair, a **skeleton-key keyhole** (circle over a bell shape). **The old iron key from [[The Expedition Post]] footlocker (Room 11) opens it** — confirmed in play, Session 8; this supersedes the original "fits but won't turn" tease.

Beyond it: **[[The Debrief Station]]**, a one-room cabin in the woods. **One-way:** the cabin face is smooth and flush and auto-locks on close. From below it's an **emergency exit out of the middle of Floor 1** — the three surviving PCs used it to escape the Pit Room's hyenas in Session 8; the hyenas didn't take the stairs.

## The bottom hatch — down to Floor 2

At the **foot** of the stair, a **hatch in the floor** drops to a matching shaft on Floor 2. **Opens to its own key — the Floor-2 brass mill tag** — and lands in [[The Bucket Line]] at its top landing. See [[The Mill Sector]] for the key's candidate locations. Not yet engaged by the party.

## Stretch-worth
The stair climb, the dead sigil (forensic read), the loose brick (looted), the top hatch (open with the key), the bottom hatch (needs the Floor-2 tag). See [[Dungeon Mechanics]].
