# The School Gym

Up: [[Mercer Falls — Setting]]

**The high-school gym, propped open for summer conditioning — the first [[Time Points|Town-Time]] node that sells a *mechanical edge* instead of information, and the room that misses [[Ricky Vance|Ricky]].**

## Description
Bleachers folded flat against one wall, a scuffed hardwood court, floor wax and old sweat. Every footstep comes back off the cinderblock a half-second late. High on the far wall the trophy case: brass and fake walnut, team photos going back forty years — and this year's, the row of boys in white jerseys, third from the left grinning like the place belongs to him. Somebody has set out a folded practice jersey and a water bottle on the first bench, the way you would for a kid who's coming in later.

Open by day (side door propped for conditioning); at night it's trespassing.

## In play
- **Clear your head (1 pt).** Shoot around, run the bleachers, hit the heavy bag. **Advantage on your next WIS check or save.** Once per day, doesn't stack. Plugs directly into the climax — the [[The Hush|Hush]]'s *Thrown Voice* is a DC 12 WIS save, clocking the mark is a WIS (Perception) 13, and Insight is WIS. A day-six gym trip is a real investment, not a throwaway.
- **Rehearse the noise (1–2 pt).** The loudest, most echoing room in town. Test a firecracker string, a boombox, an air horn in an enclosed space and learn what "overwhelming" actually means — **de-risks the blind-it plan** before the lair. [[Bash Whitlock|Bash]]'s playground.
- **The coach (2 pt, DC 12 Insight).** He has swallowed the military-camp story whole and *still* reads Ricky's name off the summer conditioning list every morning and sets out his gear. Quiet dread, no supernatural content — the [[The Reality Filter|Filter]]'s **near-miss.** Deliberately *not* a third Filter-resistant adult; [[Russ]] and [[Wade Vance|Wade]] shouldn't get diluted.
- **Ricky's locker (2 pt, DC 12 Investigation).** A photo of Wade in uniform, a folded letter, a stack of recruiting mail — the pressure he's built himself against. Humanizes him *before* the party decides whether to save him, which makes his disposition arc land far harder. Also the obvious source for a letterman jacket, if the party wants one.

> [!note] Why the boon is earned
> The gym is where **[[Tank]]** knows exactly who he is — and it's also *Ricky's* room. Clearing his head means standing in front of a picture of the kid he pranked into a hole. Competence and guilt in the same room; that's the price of the advantage, and it's why the node isn't a vending machine.

## Design function
Gives the party **something to spend a point on when they're out of leads,** which the Time-Point economy needed — every other node pays in information, so a dry stretch used to feel like wasted currency. It also gives an episode about a missing football player a room that misses him: the trophy case and the set-out gear do more than any NPC could.
