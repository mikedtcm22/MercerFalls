# Jerry's Pawn Shop

Up: [[Mercer Falls — Setting]]

**A pawn shop in [[The Districts|The Flats]] that asks no questions — the PCs' cash channel for dungeon loot, the town's whisper-hub, and its accidental witness, whose private ledger quietly counts the hollowed.**

## Description
A working-class pawn shop in the Flats, run by **[[Jerry]]**. He buys dungeon antiques without provenance and occasionally sells weird items — a steady-state gear/cash channel for the PCs (the money loop; bible §12). Comes online **early, in [[Episode 2 — The Hush]]**: the players took to the idea of a pawn shop in Session 1, so it's available as somewhere to turn dungeon haul into supply money (the trip still costs [[Time Points]]). Jerry's deeper value is as an intel node — see below.

## In play
Two things happen here:
- **Selling loot** — fast cash, low rates, no questions ([[Gear and Items]] / [[Time Points]]).
- **Whispers** — Jerry is the town's human-intel counterpart to [[Russ]]: he sees people at their most desperate and hears what the town won't say. In [[Episode 2 — The Hush]] that's the Track-A investigation — the Vances, who's gone where, who's been acting strange (Wade kitting himself out to *hunt,* the wife calling every shop → the Vance-house lead). A Time-Points beat surfaces gossip (1 pt) or a real, load-bearing whisper (2 pt).
- **The forfeit-ticket ring (a future prize).** If a PC pocketed the mill pawn-ticket in [[Session 1 — The Dare]], Jerry looks it up: a gaudy 1970s–80s pro-football **championship ring**, still in the case. A PC's **ember flickers** on contact — it's *magic,* unidentified (no Identify spell in this setting). Jerry's price is out of reach in Ep2; make it attainable **~level 3+** — a recurring want-it-later money-loop carrot rather than an Ep2 grab. ⚠ pick the ring — recommend a *Ring of Protection*; thematic alt *Ring of Mind Shielding.*
- **Maybe the graphic novel.** Russ may have sold Jerry the comic tie-in [[The Hush-Hunting Graphic Novel|*The Quiet Dark: Below*]] a while back; if Jerry's is the node the party has skipped, it's here to buy — the den-tracking lore (F4) that finds the [[The Hush|Hush]]'s lair.

The accidental-witness thread runs underneath: dozens of locals pawn things on a slow drip to keep up with bills, and **Jerry has noticed the pattern.** In conversation he can hint he knows accurate counts of hollowed townspeople — they all pawn things eventually because they don't notice they're running through their savings. He doesn't use the word "hollowed"; he's just seen it, and kept a **private ledger.** He sees the town accurately because he's its accidental witness — but he has no [[The Reality Filter|Filter]]-permission to *act,* so he only catalogues. The [[Henry Voss|Voss]] thread ([[Episode — The Hungry House]]) deepens it: Voss has pawned things for years to make mortgage payments, and chasing that leads PCs back to Jerry.

> [!danger] DM-only
> Jerry's ledger is a forensic data set — a hollowed-count of [[Hollowed Townspeople]] surfacing as a spending-pattern signature, without Jerry (or the town) understanding what it means. It corroborates the [[The Wrongness Layer|wrongness]] and feeds [[Forensic Delving]] / [[The Pattern Recognition Arc]]. His characterization (mannerisms, the trigger point for the count reveal) is still loose (bible §20 item 21); the ledger mechanic is locked.
>
> **The championship ring (backstory & prize).** The man who pawned it had a close relative on a 1970s/80s championship team and pulled strings to have a ring struck for *him;* then he hollowed, pawned his proudest possession for the cash, and never came back — Jerry even floated an extra 90 days' grace, and it just sat. It's a small, sharp instance of the pawn-drip tragedy *and* a real magic item in disguise: **ember-reactive** (flickers on contact) but **unidentified** — the party has to investigate what it does. Gate it behind price until **~level 3+.** Nature TBD — recommended **Ring of Protection** (+1 AC & saves — universally wanted, "powerful side" without breaking low levels); thematic alt **Ring of Mind Shielding** (shields the mind/soul — on-the-nose for a hollowing campaign).

> [!note] As played — Session 3 ([[Episode 2 — The Hush — Play Log|play log]])
> [[Old Man]] traded the Session-1 **lyre** (flavored as a "Great Uncle Rudolph" probate inheritance — bone nut, bone saddle, abalone inlay) to clear his pawn debt and redeem his **Epiphone Thunderbird**. Purely a re-flavoring; mechanically it's the same recovered instrument. He then spent an extra TP tuning Jerry's guitar stock (a favor, plus honest advice on which one to window) and bought a **miner's helmet with a working lamp** — $20 cash, **"Tommy" scrawled inside** — deliberately at full price as goodwill, and left a **standing request** for flashlights, maglites, and helmet lamps. The helmet is now the party's first **hands-free light source**; see [[Gear and Items]].
>
> **Jerry also volunteered, unprompted, that [[Wade Vance|Wade]]** came in buying an unreasonable quantity of shotgun shells, paid cash, and bolted with a duffel bag — Jerry's own read: *"one of those hush-hush military badass dudes."* This pre-spoils the *category* of Wade's Episode-2 reveal (see [[Episode 2 — The Hush]]); the den scene should now land on *scale* instead. **[[Patricia Vance|Patricia]]** has also been in, asking after both her husband and son — a live thread for a future Jerry beat (see her note).
