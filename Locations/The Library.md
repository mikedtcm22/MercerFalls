# The Library

Up: [[Mercer Falls — Setting]]

**The Mercer Falls public library — the town's memory. A core, daytime investigation node: where the disappearance pattern surfaces, where the Hush can be reverse-engineered without [[Russ]], and where a stern preacher is suspiciously haunting the same records the kids are.**

## Description
A small Carnegie-era public library in town — stacks, a **local-history shelf**, a folklore/legends section, and a back-room **microfilm reader** with the run of the *Mercer Falls Ledger* going back decades. Open daytime hours only (☀ — closed at night). Walk-in; no unlock required. Debuts as a core node in [[Episode 2 — The Hush]].

Dignified for the street it's on: a big stone Carnegie-era building, a short flight of steps up to a formal entrance, the circulation desk straight ahead, the space fairly dim except where the microfilm readers throw their light around in the back.

**Staff: Timothy** — the lone librarian on duty, disinclined to help anyone unprompted and quick to shush. He knows [[Old Man]] by his first name as a regular reader, and as of Session 3 he is **doing him a favor**: pulling everything the library holds on [[The Mill|the mill]]'s founding, ready the next morning. Cultivating him cost 1 TP and a persuasion check; he is now a **standing research proxy** — a kid can pay a point to set him digging and come back later, which is a genuinely different action economy from combing the microfilm yourself. Worth protecting as a resource.

## In play (Episode 2)
- **The disappearance pattern — key connective tissue.** In the old papers, a small *"[name] missing"* note buried in one issue, then ~a week later a *"[name] found dead — suspected animal attack"* headline — and **none of the articles ever connect the two,** nobody notices the rhythm ([[The Reality Filter|the Filter]], in print). Paired with the Hush's behavior (from Russ or the folklore shelf), *this* is what makes the PCs think **the same thing has [[Ricky Vance|Ricky]]** — missing now, on that same ~week clock. It historically corroborates the ripening window and is a prime convergence driver.
- **The kill, the hard way.** The folklore / local-legends shelf (or a run of old "wild animal" sighting reports) is the backup path to the [[The Hush|Hush]]'s weakness (a sound-hunter — **blind it with silence *or* an overwhelming wall of noise, then use the moment;** the source film ends by overloading it and luring it off a cliff) and its ripening window — so a party that skips [[Panels]] can still assemble the solution. (Closes the Nightmare-Engine redundancy gap.)
- **The silence relic (F1).** With the Marv's-Spot photo, the local-geography/history holdings surface the legend of a dungeon relic that (allegedly) *drowns all sound around it.* The useful part is **what it does** — the PCs already assume it's down in the dungeon — which they can intuit is a weapon against a thing that hunts by ear (silence blinds it). Points toward the [[The Stone of Quietude|Stone of Quietude]] delve. (Second reader alongside Russ.)
- **Gateway to the Mansion.** Good work here surfaces the Mercer papers / [[Mercer Falls Historical Society|Historical Society]] and **unlocks [[The Mercer Mansion|the Mansion]]** (the big-picture [[Elias Mercer]] / mill-origin lore, and a cordial [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]]).
- **Maybe the graphic novel.** If Russ has sent them for ***The Quiet Dark: Below*** (the [[The Hush-Hunting Graphic Novel|Hush-hunting comic]]) and the Library is the node they've under-used, it's here — checked out to Russ once, back on the shelf. It tracks a Hush by the *noise it cultivates.*

> [!note] As played — Session 3 ([[Episode 2 — The Hush — Play Log|play log]]), and what's still on the table
> [[Old Man]] spent **2 TP on the microfiche and got only half the pattern:** fatal *"animal attacks"* recurring every couple of years, usually young people. He did **not** surface the *missing-first, found-dead-a-week-later* pairing — which is the half that makes the ~week clock legible and drives the convergence. **The connective tissue is still unspent**, so the node has real value on a return trip; consider gating the pairing behind a better roll or a second point rather than handing it over.
> He also **rolled a nat 1 on Perception** and learned nothing about [[Reverend Hollis|Hollis]] at the reader, and a **nat 1 on Investigation** trying to recall the 1887 ledger names. The folklore shelf and the silence-relic read were never touched. He asked whether a historical society existed, rolled a **5 on History**, and so the Mansion pointer never came up here either — it reached them instead from [[Wendell Pace|Wendell]] at [[Marco's Diner]] (the folklore-and-legends exhibit).

> [!danger] DM-only — Hollis in the microfilm
> **[[Reverend Hollis]]** is here, hunched at the microfilm, actually digging **dirt on [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]]** for their rivalry — something he'd never explain to a pack of kids. So his cover is honest ("a man doing some reading"), he **clams up when they get close,** and he keeps **turning up wherever they turn up.** His interests overlap theirs *precisely* because Wesley is the hidden hinge between the mill, the Mansion, and the disappearances — so the players draw the damning line themselves without him feeding it to them. First time a **second party is shown independently circling Wesley** (a later payoff), and the quiet tragedy: the one adult building a real case against the villain gets filed under "suspect." **Play him shifty and petty, not sinister-competent,** or the table promotes him to mastermind. His church ([[Reverend Hollis|the red-herring node]]) unlocks as a follow-up.
>
> **Fired in Session 3 and completely unread** — Old Man walked up behind him, he killed the machine and left, and the Perception roll whiffed. The beat is fully reusable, and the "keeps turning up wherever they turn up" property means the second sighting should be the one that itches. Note the party has no idea he was reading about Wesley, or that Wesley exists.

## Checks (proposed)
- *Newspapers / microfiche (2 pt, Investigation ~DC 12)* → the disappearance pattern + the Mansion pointer.
- *Folklore shelf (3–4 pt, Investigation ~DC 14)* → the Hush blind-it tactic / window backup; the graphic novel if seated here.
- *Local geography, with the photo* → the silence relic (F1).
- *Set Timothy on a topic (1 pt + Persuasion)* → he does the digging; results available the following day. A cheap way to buy research without spending the hours yourself.
