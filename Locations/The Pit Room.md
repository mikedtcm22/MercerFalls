# The Pit Room

Up: [[The Mill Sector]]

**Floor 1, Room 9 — the floor's exit-guardian: the one room with a persistent, resetting combat encounter, gating the way to the Floor-2 down-stairs. A wolf-pit splits the room; the easy road past is guarded by the sleeping Dogman Chief, the hard road is a fall into the wolves.**

> [!danger] DM-only
> Spoiler room. Floor 1's **Guardian encounter** (see [[Dungeon Mechanics]]) — unlike the floor's wandering fights, this one **resets with the dungeon** and always stands between the party and the down-stairs (room 10 → Floor 2).

## Layout
A large rectangular chamber with a big **pit in the middle**, offset toward the left so the floor around it is uneven:
- **The pit** — ~**8 ft deep** (deep enough to be slow to climb out of, *not* deep enough to hurt a fall). At the bottom, a **pack of wolves sleeps**. Two **rope-ladders** hang on two sides of the pit (the way out for anything that fell — or was thrown — in).
- **Left path — narrow.** Only ~**2 ft** between the pit's edge and the left wall: passable, but a squeeze that wants **Dex checks**; a slip pitches you into the pit, among the wolves.
- **Right path — wide (~12 ft)… but guarded.** Easy footing — except the **Dogman Chief** (~7 ft, the pack alpha; see [[The Dogmen]]) sleeps at its far end, on a **bearskin rug**. Take this road and you **fight him or sneak past** (Stealth vs. his passive Perception).
- Entrance at the near end (from [[The Spiral Stair Room]], 8); the exit to the **down-stairs (room 10)** lies at the **far end**, past the Chief's post.

## The hidden ramp — how the wolves get loose (discovered, NOT read aloud)
Down one wall of the pit hangs a **rope** (not obvious from above). It's tied to a **long board lying the length of the pit floor**; hauling the rope up **tips the board into a ramp** the wolves can climb — the mechanism by which the **Chief looses his pack**. Left alone, the wolves stay penned; but the fight is built to escalate — a woken Chief raises the ramp (or the party trips it by accident), turning a one-guardian fight into pack chaos.

## The encounter (persistent / resetting)
Floor 1's Guardian fight, resetting each dungeon reset: the **Dogman Chief** (topside, on the rug) + **3 Pit Wolves** (penned in the pit until the ramp goes up). Tuned **Hard for a level-2 party of four** (Artificer, Cleric, Barbarian, Bard) — and **Deadly if attempted at level 1**. (Under milestone leveling, the party reaches this at level 2; they level at the end of Episode 2.) Budget: Chief (~CR 2) + 3 wolves ≈ **1,200 adjusted XP = Hard** on the nose.

**The real difficulty dial is the pit, not the wolf count.** The wolves start penned and only reach the fight after the Chief spends an action to **Raise the Ramp** and they climb out (~a turn), funneling up one or two at a time. A smart party keeps it Hard — drop the Chief before he pulls the rope, cut the rope, or fight the wolves at the ramp-mouth one at a time; a careless party (letting the ramp up, getting surrounded, or slipping off the narrow ledge into the pen) spikes it toward Deadly. The **Rampage/Incite synergy** is a snowball amplifier, not an alpha strike: Incite Rampage is an *action* (the Chief forgoes Multiattack) and grants one wolf a single extra bite, and the wolves' Rampage only pays out on a kill — so it escalates a fight already going badly rather than opening with a blowout.

> [!info] Dogman Chief — *Medium fiend (dogman), Floor-1 pack alpha*
> **AC** 15 · **HP** 52 (8d8+16) · **Speed** 35 ft.
> **STR** 14 (+2) · **DEX** 14 (+2) · **CON** 15 (+2) · **INT** 6 (−2) · **WIS** 10 (+0) · **CHA** 7 (−2)
> **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft, PP 10 · **Languages** Gnoll · **CR** ~2 (XP 450)
> **Multiattack.** Two attacks, Bone Whip or Bone Bow in any combination.
> **Bone Whip.** Melee +5, reach **10 ft**, *Hit* 5 (1d6+2) piercing.
> **Bone Bow.** Ranged +3, range 150/600 ft, *Hit* 6 (1d10+1) piercing.
> **Incite Rampage (Recharge 5–6, Action).** One creature it can see within 60 ft that has the Rampage bonus action may use its reaction to make one melee attack. *(Used instead of Multiattack.)*
> **Bonus Action — Rampage (1/Day).** After dealing damage to a Bloodied creature, move up to half speed and make one Bone Whip attack.
> **Raise the Ramp (Action).** Haul the pit-rope to drop the board into a ramp; the pit-wolves can begin climbing out on their next turn.
> *(= the [[The Buzzsaw Room|Buzzsaw]] Lieutenant chassis + Incite Rampage from the Pack Lord, HP bumped 40→52 so he survives to loose and incite the pack. Wears the Chief regalia — see [[The Dogmen]].)*

> [!info] Pit Wolf (×3) — *Medium beast (Wolf, Rampage variant)*
> **AC** 13 · **HP** 11 (2d8+2) · **Speed** 40 ft.
> **STR** 12 (+1) · **DEX** 15 (+2) · **CON** 12 (+1) · **INT** 3 (−4) · **WIS** 12 (+1) · **CHA** 6 (−2)
> **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +4 · **PP** 13 · **CR** 1/4 (XP 50)
> **Bite.** Melee +4, *Hit* 7 (2d4+2) piercing; target DC 11 STR save or knocked **prone**.
> **Bonus Action — Rampage.** Immediately after reducing a creature to 0 HP with its bite, move up to half speed and make one bite. *(Replaces Pack Tactics — and satisfies the Chief's Incite Rampage prerequisite.)*

The room is the fight: the pit as pen and hazard, the narrow ledge as a shove-target (into the pen), the ramp as the escalation lever, the two paths as a stealth-or-brawl choice.

## Stretch-worth
The **crossing choice** (risky narrow squeeze vs. wide-but-guarded), **sneaking past or fighting the Chief**, spotting and controlling the **ramp-rope**, and the fact that this is the **gate to Floor 2** — the room demands engagement every trip until the party owns it.
