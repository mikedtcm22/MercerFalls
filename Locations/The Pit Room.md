# The Pit Room

Up: [[The Mill Sector]]

**Floor 1, Room 9 — the floor's exit-guardian: the one room with a persistent, resetting combat encounter, gating the way to the Floor-2 down-stairs. A wolf-pit splits the room; the easy road past is guarded by the sleeping Dogman Chief, the hard road is a fall into the wolves.**

> [!danger] DM-only
> Spoiler room. Floor 1's **Guardian encounter** (see [[Dungeon Mechanics]]) — unlike the floor's wandering fights, this one **resets with the dungeon** and always stands between the party and the down-stairs (room 10 → Floor 2).

## Layout
A large rectangular chamber with a big **pit in the middle**, offset toward the left so the floor around it is uneven:
- **The pit** — ~**8 ft deep** (deep enough to be slow to climb out of, *not* deep enough to hurt a fall). At the bottom, a **pack of wolves sleeps**. Two **rope-ladders** hang on two sides of the pit (the way out for anything that fell — or was thrown — in).
- **Left path — narrow.** Only ~**2 ft** between the pit's edge and the left wall: passable, but a squeeze that wants **Dex checks**; a slip pitches you into the pit, among the wolves.
- **Right path — wide (~12 ft)… but guarded.** Easy footing — except the **Dogman Chief** (~7 ft, the pack alpha; see [[The Dogmen]]) sleeps at its far end, on a **bearskin rug**. Take this road and you **fight him or sneak past** (Stealth vs. his passive Perception).
- Entrance at the near end (from [[The Spiral Stair Room]], 8); the exit to the **down-stairs (room 10)** lies at the **far end**, past the Chief's post.

## The hidden ramp — how the wolves get loose (discovered, NOT read aloud)
Down one wall of the pit hangs a **rope** (not obvious from above). It's tied to a **long board lying the length of the pit floor**; hauling the rope up **tips the board into a ramp** the wolves can climb — the mechanism by which the **Chief looses his pack**. Left alone, the wolves stay penned; but the fight is built to escalate — a woken Chief raises the ramp (or the party trips it by accident), turning a one-guardian fight into pack chaos.

## The encounter (persistent / resetting) — stats TBD
Floor 1's Guardian fight, resetting each dungeon reset. Composition: the **Dogman Chief** (topside, on the rug) + a **pit pack of wolves** (penned until the ramp goes up). *Chief stat block and wolf count: to be designed (next).* The room is the fight: the pit as pen and hazard, the narrow ledge as a shove-target, the ramp as an escalation lever, the two paths as a stealth-or-brawl choice.

## Stretch-worth
The **crossing choice** (risky narrow squeeze vs. wide-but-guarded), **sneaking past or fighting the Chief**, spotting and controlling the **ramp-rope**, and the fact that this is the **gate to Floor 2** — the room demands engagement every trip until the party owns it.
