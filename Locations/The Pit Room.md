# The Pit Room

Up: [[The Mill Sector]]

**Floor 1, Room 9 — the floor's exit-guardian: the one room with a persistent, resetting combat encounter, gating the way to the Floor-2 down-stairs. A beast-pit splits the room; the easy road past is guarded by the sleeping Dogman Chief, the hard road is a fall into the pit.**

> [!danger] DM-only
> Spoiler room. Floor 1's **Guardian encounter** (see [[Dungeon Mechanics]]) — unlike the floor's wandering fights, this one **resets with the dungeon** and always stands between the party and the down-stairs (room 10 → Floor 2).

> [!note] First contact — [[Session 8 — Second Shift|Session 8]]
> Played twice in one night: a disguised scouting pass, then a poisoned-stew gambit that turned into a fight. **[[Old Man]] was hollowed here**; the other three escaped up the spiral stair and out the top hatch. **As played, the pit held two giant hyenas, not three wolves** — see *As played* below, which supersedes the encounter block for future runs.

## Layout
A large rectangular chamber with a big **pit in the middle**, offset toward the left so the floor around it is uneven:
- **The pit** — ~**8 ft deep** (deep enough to be slow to climb out of, *not* deep enough to hurt a fall). At the bottom, **the pack sleeps** curled together. Two **rope-ladders** hang on two sides of the pit (the way out for anything that fell — or was thrown — in).
- **Left path — narrow.** Only ~**2 ft** between the pit's edge and the left wall: passable, but a squeeze that wants **Dex checks**; a slip pitches you into the pit.
- **Right path — wide (~12 ft)… but guarded.** Easy footing — except the **Dogman Chief** sleeps at its far end, on a **bearskin rug**, in a **crude circlet of rusted iron and bone**, a mantle of matted pelts, and cords of teeth and claws across his chest. Take this road and you **fight him, sneak past, or talk.**
- Entrance at the near end (from [[The Spiral Stair Room]], 8); the exit — **a stairway leading down (room 10)** — at the **far end**, past the Chief's post.

## The ramp — how the pack gets loose
**As played:** a **crude board ramp, currently pulled up**, lying beside the Chief at the foot of his rug. **Lowering it into the pit is an action**, and the beasts climb out on their next turn. *(Replaces the design's hidden rope tipping a board on the pit floor.)* Left alone, the pack stays penned.

## The Chief's people — established in play
Woken by a "dogman" (Old Man in Disguise Self), the Chief spoke like a sergeant: *"What are you doing here? Get back to your post."* He accepted without question that there's **a mess** serving **stew**, called Old Man **"grunt,"** and on being poisoned declared **"a mutiny."** **Floor 1's dogmen have posts, rank, and a mess hall.** Worth carrying into [[The Dogmen]].

He **can be talked to and fooled**: Deception against his Wisdom, and Disguise Self holds unless he takes the Study action and beats the caster's DC on Intelligence (he rolled 11 vs. DC 13).

## As played — Session 8

1. **Scouting.** Old Man, disguised as the Chief's twin, Stealth 12 (with Guidance) to reach the ramp — the Chief woke. Gern signalled the others back into the corridor (Survival 18) so only "a dogman" was seen. Old Man improvised **kobold stew in the mess**; the Chief sent him for some, with grog.
2. **The stew.** A Tinker's Magic dog bowl (Performance 13), a Capri Sun, Dunkaroo frosting, and condensed Poison Spray. **Deception 16 vs. Wis 2** — he ate half, gagged, **took 11 poison** (1d12). Disguise held; he knew he'd been poisoned anyway.
3. **The fight.** Unarmed shove at Old Man (missed); **lowered the ramp**; stepped back to shoot with the **bone bow**. **Incite Rampage** gave a hyena a reaction bite that **hollowed Old Man.** Hyena bites landed for **11, 13, 13, 7, 10.** Tank went to 1 on Relentless Endurance.
4. **The retreat.** Bash laid **grease** and then **ball bearings**; both hyenas went prone in the grease. Gern hit the Chief with **Infestation**. **The survivors dashed up the spiral stair and out the top hatch**; the hyenas didn't take the stairs and the Chief let them handle it.

**Old Man's faint nullprint is in this room.** It fades as he recovers.

> [!note] What the party now believes — and what's true
> They're planning a return with **"ten times more delicious and ten times more poisonous stew."** Floor 1 resets **static** ([[Floor Reset and the Intelligence Gradient]]): **the Chief comes back each day at full HP with no memory of the mutinous dogman.** The trick meets a fresh Chief every time — a fresh contested roll, not a wary one.
>
> They also now know the **top hatch is one room away** and opens with Tank's key. **Fleeing this fight costs them nothing** (no notch, no lost day). Tune with that in mind.

## The encounter (persistent / resetting) — retuned to as-played

**Current canon:** the **Dogman Chief** (on the rug) + **2 giant hyenas** (penned until the ramp goes down). Appropriate for the **level-3** party arriving in [[Episode 4 — The Crossing|Ep4]] — and proven lethal enough to hollow a PC at level 2.

> [!info] Dogman Chief — *Medium fiend (dogman), Floor-1 pack alpha*
> **AC** 15 · **HP** 52 (8d8+16) · **Speed** 35 ft.
> **STR** 14 (+2) · **DEX** 14 (+2) · **CON** 15 (+2) · **INT** 6 (−2) · **WIS** 10 (+0) · **CHA** 7 (−2)
> **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft, PP 10 · **Languages** Gnoll *(understood the disguised Old Man without issue)* · **CR** ~2 (XP 450)
> **Multiattack.** Two attacks, Bone Whip or Bone Bow in any combination.
> **Bone Whip.** Melee +5, reach **10 ft**, *Hit* 5 (1d6+2) piercing.
> **Bone Bow.** Ranged +3, range 150/600 ft, *Hit* 6 (1d10+1) piercing.
> **Incite Rampage (Recharge 5–6, Action).** One creature it can see within 60 ft that has the Rampage bonus action may use its reaction to make one melee attack. *(Used instead of Multiattack.)*
> **Bonus Action — Rampage (1/Day).** After dealing damage to a Bloodied creature, move up to half speed and make one Bone Whip attack.
> **Lower the Ramp (Action).** Drop the board into the pit; the beasts can begin climbing out on their next turn.
> *(= the [[The Buzzsaw Room|Buzzsaw]] Lieutenant chassis + Incite Rampage from the Pack Lord, HP bumped 40→52 so he survives to loose and incite the pack. Wears the Chief regalia — see [[The Dogmen]].)*

> [!info] Pit beasts — *giant hyenas (×2), as played*
> Run off the SRD/MM **Giant Hyena** (CR 1): Large beast, ~45 HP, bite ~2d6+3, **Rampage** bonus action — which satisfies the Chief's Incite Rampage prerequisite. Fast enough (50 ft) to close a 90-ft room in a round.
>
> *(The original design — **3 Pit Wolves**, Wolf/Rampage variant, AC 13, HP 11, bite +4 for 2d4+2 with a DC 11 Str prone rider — is retired for this room. Keep it for a wandering pack.)*

**The real difficulty dial is still the pit.** The beasts start penned and only reach the fight after the Chief spends an action on the ramp. A smart party drops the Chief before he lowers it, holds the ramp, or fights at the ramp-mouth; a careless one gets bitten apart in the open. **Bash's grease proved the counter:** two prone hyenas in a 10-ft slick bought the retreat.

## Stretch-worth
The **crossing choice** (risky narrow squeeze vs. wide-but-guarded), **sneaking past, fighting, or talking to the Chief**, controlling the **ramp**, and the fact that this is the **gate to Floor 2** — the room demands engagement every trip until the party owns it.
