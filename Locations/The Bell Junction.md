# The Bell Junction

Up: [[The Mill Sector]]

**Floor 1, Room 7 — a four-way junction on the main route from the Vestibule to the Floor-2 stairs, ruled by a big out-of-reach bell. Ring the bell and a false door becomes a portal to a random room the party has already visited — a setback early, a gambler's shortcut once they've been deep.**

> [!danger] DM-only
> Spoiler room. The hinge between the Vestibule-to-stairs main path and the keystone side (secretly reachable from room 6).

## Layout — a rectangle with four ways and a bell
Four exits, one per wall, plus the bell overhead:
- **Right wall — door to the [[The Octagonal Vestibule|Octagonal Vestibule]] (3).** The main approach: one of the Vestibule's real spokes leads here.
- **Left wall — a broad archway to Room 8** (the standard path on toward the Floor-2 down-stairs). An open archway, not a door.
- **Top wall — a hidden panel: the far side of the one-way secret passage from Room 6.** From this side it reads as blank wall and is hard to find; from the room-6 side it's the passage out. (Supersedes the old "one-way painted door 6→7.")
- **Bottom wall — the bell-portal door** (below).
- **Overhead, center — a large fixed bell**, hung high from the ceiling, **out of reach** of 5-ft melee/reach.

## The bell and the portal door
The bottom-wall door is inert until the bell is rung:
- **Ringing the bell.** It hangs above any normal 5-ft reach. To ring it: a **10-ft reach weapon** (whip, polearm — automatic), or **a thrown/fired projectile** (javelin, arrow, sling stone, hurled object — a trivial ranged attack against a big fixed target). A purely melee, 5-ft-reach party has to improvise a throw.
- **Sigil lights → portal active.** When the bell rings, the **bell-shaped sigil carved above the door illuminates**; while lit, opening the door reveals a **shimmering portal**.
- **Unlit = false door.** Open the door with the sigil dark and it's a **false door** — solid stonework behind it, no effect.
- *Proposed defaults (confirm):* the sigil stays lit ~1 minute after a ring (long enough to decide and step through), then fades — re-ring to reactivate; **one ring sets one destination**, so everyone who crosses during that lit window arrives **together** in the same room; re-ringing rerolls.

## Where the portal goes — random, among rooms already visited
Stepping through drops you in a **random room the party has already visited** — *any* floor they've reached, not just Floor 1 — chosen when the portal activates and **not revealed until you step through** (that's the gamble). Exclude the current room.
- **Early = hazard/setback.** With only Floor-1 rooms on the visited list, it mostly flings the party backward across Floor 1 (e.g., dumped at the [[The Buzzsaw Room|Buzzsaw]]), costing progress toward the stairs.
- **Later = a gambler's shortcut.** Once the party has explored deep (say Floor 3), the visited pool includes those deep rooms — so a ring-and-step *might* leapfrog them straight to a Floor-3 room they know, saving a long descent… or might just spit them out on Floor 1. High-risk, high-reward travel.
- **DM implementation:** keep a running list of visited rooms; on activation, roll one at random from it (excluding room 7). The pool grows as they explore, so the door's value shifts from trap to tool over the campaign.

## Stretch-worth
Several: solving the **bell** (how do we reach it?) and discovering the **ring → sigil → portal** relationship; the gamble of **using the portal** (setback or shortcut); and **searching the blank top wall** for the hidden panel (the secret route to/from room 6). See [[Dungeon Mechanics]].
