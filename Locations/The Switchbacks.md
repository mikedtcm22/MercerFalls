# The Switchbacks

Up: [[The Mill Sector]]

**Floor 1, Room 4 — a blind-cornered switchback corridor linking the Vestibule (3) to the Counting Room (5). Its whole job is to make the party move like it's being hunted; a hidden mechanism here opens the secret door to the Stutter Stairs (15).**

> [!danger] DM-only
> Spoiler room. Off the Session-1 delve path (reached on later delves via a different Vestibule door).

## What it is
A narrow corridor that doubles back on itself over and over — short legs, blind corners, no sightline past the next turn, and the party's light dying at each bend. Two abreast at most. It carries no scripted encounter; its recurring character is **terrain that rewards caution**.

## The recurring beat — move careful or pay for it
Built to make **careful movement / stealth the obvious Stretch Activity** without narrating dread. It reports only neutral, physical cues and lets the players supply the caution:
- short sightlines (you see one leg, no farther; light stops at the corner);
- a low, wet animal smell drifting on the draft, stronger at some bends (ambient [[The Nightmare Engine|pack-floor]] flavor — **not** a promise of a fight);
- a single scrape/click from around a bend that doesn't repeat;
- the party's own light throwing shadows that round each corner a beat ahead of them.

Mechanically the geometry backs the instinct: on any delve a **wandering monster** is actually present, the blind corners mean careless movement rounds a corner into it with no line of sight, while a careful/stealthy approach spots it first or slips past. The room teaches caution and pays it off, every delve — no scripted monster required.

## The hidden shortcut to the Stutter Stairs (15)
About halfway along, tucked in a switchback nook, a **simple concealed mechanism** (default: a **rusted lever behind a wall bracket**) operates the secret door to the [[Stutter Stairs]] (15).
- **Deliberately not in the read-aloud** — a reward for a player who *searches*, not something the narration hands over.
- **Hard from this side:** a Search Stretch with no seam to tip you off and the mechanism tucked out of sight (high DC). From the **15 side the door is plainly a door** — the "hard to find from here, easy coming back" asymmetry.
- **Payoff:** a real shortcut that skips a chunk of the loop toward the Compass-Keystone side (16).

## Stretch-worth
Two things earn a Stretch here: the **movement choice itself** (ambush terrain makes careful/stealth matter every time through), and the **search-and-shortcut** (the hidden mechanism → the 15 door) for whoever pokes around. See [[Dungeon Mechanics]].
