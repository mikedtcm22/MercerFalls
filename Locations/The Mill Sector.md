# The Mill Sector

Up: [[Mercer Falls — Setting]]

**The Act-1 dungeon: floors 1-5 of [[The Dungeon]] reached through [[The Mill]]'s lower works — the campaign's heaviest dungeon-design investment, dozens of sessions of grinding, glyph-decoding, and cat-and-mouse with [[Wesley Crane]].**

> [!danger] DM-only
> This entire note is a spoiler. The Mill sector is the only entrance the PCs can use through Act 1 (via trespass into the sealed mill grounds).

## Where it sits
One of three fragmented-arc sectors of the dungeon's upper floors (see [[The Dungeon's Surface Footprint]]). The Mill, Mercer, and Lake sectors' floors 1-5 **do not connect**; the system unifies only at floor 6. PCs entering from the mill descend through industrial space, then the bluff's bedrock, then dungeon proper. **Floors 1-5 are heavy content by design** — this is where the Act-1 investment goes. (By contrast the **Mercer sector** is a low-detail transit corridor used in Act 2, and the **Lake entrance** drops straight to a deep Tier-2 floor — both noted here only for orientation; see [[The Dungeon's Surface Footprint]].)

## Design lens for the build-out
Every room is built to the **[[Dungeon Mechanics|Stretch-Worth Principle]]**: it offers at least one thing worth spending a Stretch on (loot, inspect/forensic-read, disarm, decode, fight, trigger) — an *opportunity, not an obligation*. Combat-likely rooms additionally get the **[[Combatscape Layers]]** treatment (elevation, cover, chokepoints, interactables) so a wandering monster turns the room into part of the fight. Monsters follow the **[[The Nightmare Engine|reskin pattern]]** (D&D stat blocks, Mercer Falls flavor) and each floor carries a **lair theme** — Floor 1 is **"the pack floor"** (Dogmen/gnolls organized core, feral beasts around, all beneath [[The Hush]]). No pure pass-through rooms.

## Floor 1 — the sixteen-room map (canonical layout)
**Sixteen numbered rooms in a donut footprint** around a central dark void — the unmapped Mercer/Lake sectors' "bite" out of the ring (see [[The Dungeon's Surface Footprint]]). Entry is at the east; the ring loops up and around to the Floor-2 stairs at the northwest. The **[[The Ember Chamber|Awakening Room]]** (map-marked **S**) sits adjacent to the ring, not in it — the one-time pocket for the Session-1 awakening only.

Connective structure: the **[[The Keystone Tablet|two keystones]]** sit at opposite ends of the loop (the **Map-Keystone in room 6**, gated by the [[The Counting Room|room-5]] puzzle; the **Compass-Keystone in a hidden enclave, room 16**). A **one-way secret passage** runs from room 6 to the **[[The Bell Junction]] (7)** — a hidden panel on the 7 side. The wrapping **[[The Stutter Stairs]]** (15) are reached from the **[[The Switchbacks]] (4)** via a secret passage, and their far-side landing (once its mirror-loop is broken) holds the door to the **Compass enclave (16)**. The other two sectors link into Floor 1 through **secret entrances that stay dormant until Act 2**: the **[[The Mercer Gate|Mercer sector]]** at the **Down-Stairs (10)**, and the **Lake sector** at the **Buzzsaw Room (14)**.

**The rooms** *(the full Session-1 delve path — 2, 3, 11, 12, 13, 14 — plus the off-trail rooms 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 15 are now designed; 1, 6, 10, 16 carry hooks awaiting build):*
1. **Dungeon Entrance** — the boiler-room hatch down from [[The Mill]]'s lower works. In Session 1 the way onward reads **caved-in** until the PCs engage the Awakening Room. *(Interaction hook TBD beyond the entrance itself.)*
2. **[[The Cataloguing Office]]** — long hidden office where [[Elias Mercer]]'s 1880s operation catalogued finds; century-old yet mid-shift. The players' first room; loot (real, leaves the dungeon), forensic reads, the Mercer-papers seed. *(Fully designed.)*
3. **[[The Octagonal Vestibule]]** — the hub; a door on each of eight walls. Open a real exit and the other doors collapse to blank wall, locking the hub to that spoke until reset. Three real paths, two fake-door maw traps, two closets (loot / sleeping dogmen), one entrance. S1: pre-collapsed to Wesley's door, with two lootable dead dogmen on the floor. *(Fully designed.)*
4. **[[The Switchbacks]]** — a blind-cornered switchback corridor (Vestibule → Counting Room) built to make careful movement/stealth the obvious play, cued by neutral physical details (no narrated dread); a hidden midpoint mechanism (a rusted lever) opens the secret passage to the Stutter Stairs (15) — hard from this side, plain from the 15 side. *(Fully designed.)*
5. **[[The Counting Room]]** — the **map-keystone puzzle**: a rotatable "map of the room" pedestal + an 8×8 checkerboard floor + a right-wall pit; align the pedestal's door-mark to the real door (left) to read three floor-tiles that open the door to room 6; misalignments give treasure (far wall), nothing (entrance), or spike traps (pit wall). *(Fully designed.)*
6. **Map-Keystone Niche** — past the room-5 puzzle door; **holds the Map-Keystone**, and a **one-way secret passage** onward to the Bell Junction (7). *(Its old glyph-lock is superseded by the room-5 puzzle; small standalone identity still TBD.)*
7. **[[The Bell Junction]]** — a rectangular four-way: right door → Vestibule (3); left archway → Room 8; top hidden panel ← the one-way secret passage from 6; bottom = a **false door that becomes a portal when the ceiling bell is rung** (10-ft reach or a projectile lights the bell-sigil) — the portal drops the party in a **random already-visited room on any floor** (setback early, gambit shortcut later). *(Fully designed.)*
8. **[[The Spiral Stair Room]]** — a corridor bulging at its middle into a shaft where an **iron spiral staircase** climbs into darkness. **Top hatch** = the sealed Mill-grounds exit (the room-11 key fits but won't turn — wrong-but-similar lock); **bottom hatch** = down toward a Floor-2 spiral stair, locked from underneath / no grip this side. Both sealed for now — forward hooks. *(Fully designed.)*
9. **[[The Pit Room]]** — Floor 1's **exit-guardian** (the floor's one **persistent, resetting** encounter), gating the Floor-2 down-stairs. A central ~8-ft **wolf-pit** (penned wolves, two rope-ladders, a hidden rope-and-board ramp to loose them); a **narrow left crossing** (Dex, or fall in) vs. a **wide right crossing** guarded by the sleeping **Dogman Chief** on a bearskin rug — fight or stealth. Encounter locked: Chief (Lieutenant chassis + Incite Rampage, HP 52) + 3 Rampage-variant Pit Wolves; Hard @ level 2 / Deadly @ level 1. *(Fully designed.)*
10. **Down-Stairs** — stairs down to **Floor 2**. *(Also the dormant secret entrance the **[[The Mercer Gate|Mercer sector]]** links into in Act 2.)*
11. **[[The Expedition Post]]** — [[Elias Mercer]]'s forward expedition outpost; a **dynamic [[Combatscape Layers|Combatscape]] battle map** (a tall timber platform on posts crossing the room, open floor beneath, high ground optional via two corner ladders; floor-level barrels for cover; a far-corner freight shaft + hoisted crate) hosting the floor's guaranteed first wandering fight, plus finds: a failed-spectator specimen in the crate, Elias's "Third Descent" desk page, footlocker loot (incl. the old key for room 8). *(Fully designed.)*
12. **[[The Spiked Corridor]]** — a **traveling-wave** gauntlet of **low** spikes (painful, survivable; a fixed **diagonal** of dead tiles is the safe read). Four ways across — run it, walk the diagonal, pull the disguised torch-release, or lasso-swing the ceiling fixture. S1: the wave is live; a **blood streak across the far end** (Wendell Pace, dragged across) leads to the exit as the telegraph. *(Fully designed.)*
13. **[[The Pillared Reach]]** — the largest room on the floor; the young [[The Hush]]'s nest. The party arrives on a fight in progress (young Hush killing Wendell Pace) with a rush / watch / slip choice; a **sound-as-terrain** fight; the first witnessed hollowing; the spore-mark → Session 2 hook. *(Fully designed.)*
14. **[[The Buzzsaw Room]]** — the **Session-1 climax** ([[Wesley Crane|Wesley]]'s first real sigil/portal). Physically the **Millrace**: a wide fast-water chasm crossed by a winding single-file catwalk — an anti-surround stage for the fight vs. a modified Pack Lord + wolf. Permanent identity: a deadly-terrain room plus a **major magic weapon/armor in the far corner** as the come-back reward. Also the dormant **Lake-sector** entrance. *(Fully designed.)*
15. **[[The Stutter Stairs]]** — the fake-out "stairs down": a V-shaped stair looped by an **invisible mirror** at the bottom landing that sends the party back where they started without their turning around. Diagnosed via a recurring landmark + the Stretch/torch cost; perceived by Arcana/Detect Magic; broken by lifting the **hollow last-steps** to a switch (one per side). The far-side landing holds the door to the **Compass-Keystone enclave (16)**. Reached from the Switchbacks (4). *(Fully designed.)*
16. **Compass-Keystone Enclave** — hidden; holds the **Compass-Keystone**, reached via the Stutter Stairs' far-side door (once the mirror-loop is broken); guarded by a **deep guardian** (TBD). *(Awaiting build.)*
- **S — [[The Ember Chamber|Awakening Room]]** — the one-time, entity-made pocket adjacent to the ring; the Session-1 awakening only, then it closes forever.

See [[The Keystone Tablet]] for what the two Floor-1 keystones do; [[The Nightmare Engine]] for the Floor-1 "pack floor" bestiary theme.

## Floor 1 in Session 1
The floor is **partially pre-cleared by Wesley** on a recent solo delve; the party follows his footprints, spent torches, drag marks, a cloth scrap (seen again on Wesley), stacked corpses, tally marks, and chalk-struck glyphs — their first encounter with the glyph-language. On the map his trail runs from the Entrance (1) through the Cataloguing Office (2) and Octagonal Vestibule (3), out through the Expedition Post (11), the Spiked Corridor (12), and the Pillared Reach (13), ending at the **Buzzsaw Room (14)** — where Wesley is finalizing **his first real sigil** ([[Wesley's Sigils]]); he says one line (*"You poor things"*), triggers the sigil, and vanishes. The engineered wave that follows is tuned for a likely TPK with a narrow sigil-escape window. See [[Session 1 — The Dare]].

The Spiked Corridor's spike-wave is **live** in S1 (Wesley threaded the diagonal; the low spikes only gash and bruise — a painful, survivable crossing). A **streak of Wendell Pace's blood across the far end leads to the exit door** — the telegraph. The party's first *combat* is a real **wandering roll** in the Expedition Post (11); their first *witnessed hollowing* is Wendell in the Pillared Reach (13) — or, if a fast, hot-rolling party pulls him off the young Hush alive, moments later in the Buzzsaw Room (14).

## Floors 2-5 and steady-state mechanics
Floors 2-5 await detailed design (built one at a time as each comes online). Steady-state: daily intrinsic **floor reset** (chalk and glow persist; footprints and blood are gone), Stretch-based resource attrition, the glyph-language as a persistent knowledge resource, and the **sigil cat-and-mouse** with Wesley (real vs. decoy sigils). Forensic delving — residue-reading, sigil-reading, tracking Wesley's status via the gradient-shuffle — is the signature mechanic here. See [[Dungeon Mechanics]], [[Floor Reset and the Intelligence Gradient]], [[Forensic Delving]].

The Act-1 climax is partial access to **floor 6**, repulsed before significant exploration. After [[The Mercer Gate]] opens at the equinox, the Mill sector's floors 1-5 stop being campaign-active (explored, mapped, drained).
