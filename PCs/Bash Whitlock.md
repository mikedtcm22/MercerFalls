# Bash Whitlock

Rock Gnome Artificer (**Artillerist**, from level 3) · 13 (skipped a grade) · smooth through a screen, lost face-to-face. Ideal: *it's all readable — you just need the right instrument.* Flaw: *anything glowing beats anyone talking.* Bond: his cousin [[Dominique Ferraro]].

## The read — Order/Chaos in his epistemology
He can't be conned by words (skeptical to the bone, "assumes every system has a backdoor") — so [[The Entity]] cons him with **data**: a working instrument, a confirmed prediction, a readout that's *right*. His precognition "window" and his gear picking up the mill's electrical hash are, unknowingly, an early antenna for the wrongness — **the window is the Entity.** Chasing it is, unknowingly, serving the thing under the town.
- **[[Order and Chaos|Order]] tempts his worldview:** total readability = a determined, agency-dead clockwork (the sealed box).
- **Chaos tempts his appetite:** the lit-up chase; "the people slowing you down are the cage."
- **Redemption:** the unreadable — a person, a free choice, a changeable future — isn't a gap in his instrument; it's the *signature* of the agency the whole cosmology is about.

## Awakening test — "The Basement: the Box and the Rig" (locked)
His station is his own basement, recreated. Two devices on opposite walls; touch one, the other dissolves (mote-fade). **The black box:** sealed, impenetrable, *works* — shows the near future, one slider, no way in (the one system he can't open). **The open rig:** wires bare, alive, throwing static — and a voice (maybe [[Dominique Ferraro|Dom]]) surfacing and dropping. Post-it (survives the choice): *"one of these works. / the other just wants to talk. / you already know which one you've been chasing — go."*
- **Rig branch:** ember wedged in the machinery (he must *work* it free); the static clears to Dom's voice — *"…you were right, man, you were always—"* (cut off).
- **Box branch:** the screen shows him finding the ember behind an **air vent** (the next few seconds); he enacts it; the box goes inert (miracle spent).
Aligned = the rig (the unreadable, the person). The tell: it nudged him (post-it) toward a *sealed system he could never open* and asked him to dissolve a voice that might be Dom's. See [[The Ember Chamber]], [[The Embers]].

## Battle plan
- **Through-line — The Corrupted Instrument.** His gear is the Entity's antenna, feeding true-seeming readouts that nudge him and (since he's the party's research engine) the whole party. The recurring shape: **sealed answers vs. open, human paths** — trusting the sealed one serves the thing under the town.
- **Early/mid lean — Glowing Beats Talking.** Reward the machine-over-person reflex, then make it cost something human (heads-down when the team needed him; a clue followed off a cliff).
- **Crisis & turn — Dom on the Screen.** The rig opens a window on [[Dominique Ferraro]] in danger. If the future is fixed/readable (his creed), she's doomed — and the Awakening fragment detonates (being *right* about a determined world is what dooms her). His turn: change a future a screen showed him → assert agency, refute his own creed for love.
- **Reserve:** the precognition window (DM-controlled — true warning, manipulation, or red herring); the long-game reveal that his materialist certainty was the Entity's bait.
- **⚠ [[The Reading Glass]] went to [[Gern]], not Bash** (Session 8). The designed "this is the same thing that happens to me" payoff now routes through Gern — see that note for the reroute options (Bash recognising the stillness on someone *else's* face is the leading one).

## Relationships
Knows [[Gern]] via mother-arranged playdates (assigned, not chosen). Connected to [[Tank]] through [[Dominique Ferraro]] — Bash recruits Tank as a protector and to **phreak him a free second phone line**. At the mill on a **dare** — really to ingratiate himself with Tank. Deliberate foil to [[Old Man]]: the skeptic who distrusts the beautiful line beside the face who fools everyone with it. See [[The Party]].

## Household & Time Points
**Unmodified (confirmed, do not apply a modifier).** Bash's household is narratively lax and unsupervised — his mother works nights, and he has real freedom — but he has **not** taken the Lax Parents backstory mechanic, so his Time Points run standard baseline (per [[Time Points]]), not +1 Night. Session 3 played through several evening beats without his player invoking the extra point; keep it that way unless he explicitly picks it up later. **Session 8:** out from ~2 a.m. to ~5 a.m.; heat check pending at the start of Ep4. **[[Dominique Ferraro|Dominique]] wasn't told** they were going back down.

## Mechanics of note
- **Replicated magic items (ruled Session 8):** capped by the Magic Items column for his level; **making a new one destroys the oldest.** He swapped the **Bag of Holding → Rope of Climbing**, handing out the contents first (Tank got the Room-11 key; Old Man got his record back, Mended). **Tank still has the +1 warhammer.**
- **Tinker's Magic** as a utility engine: a dog bowl on demand (Performance 13 for quality), **ball bearings** dropped behind him in a retreat.
- Signature kit in play: **caltrops** (earned the party a surprise round), **Grease**, **Poison Spray** (his favourite; persistently unlucky to-hit), **Witch Bolt**, hand crossbow.
- **Level 3: Artillerist.** Had also floated experimental elixirs. *"This class is kind of boring"* (mid-fight, level 2) — the turret should fix that.

## Episode 1 — played (Awakening choice)
> [!note] Sessions 1–2 — full account in [[Episode 1 — The Dare — Play Log]]
> **"The Basement: the Box and the Rig."** He **turned the dial on the sealed Black Box** (the instrument that *works*), dissolving the open rig and the voice that might have been [[Dominique Ferraro|Dom]]; the screen showed the ember and he retrieved it — **under his futon** (the design's air-vent detail changed at the table). **Read:** the on-flaw / misaligned branch — "glowing beats talking," data over the person; he took exactly the bait the post-it nudged him toward. Ember taken.
> **Ep-1 exit:** one of the last two standing in the [[The Buzzsaw Room|Buzzsaw]]; he went through the far **destabilizing archway** (the NW portal), [[Gern]] following. Resolved as the free-willed exit — see the play log.

## Episode 2 — played (Session 3)
> [!note] Full account in [[Episode 2 — The Hush — Play Log]]
> Cast **Sanctuary** on Gern the instant the accusation turned physical. Gave [[Dominique Ferraro|Dominique]] the **entire dungeon account** — including a live spell demonstration — which she filed as a very good magic trick and a wild story; landed the session's turning line, *"For the first time in my life, I can take care of myself."* Told Tank, once he learned the truth about the prank, exactly what he thought of it. Later stood as **Gern's one ally** when the Tome of Conviction was dismissed by the others. Tested Mage Hand's carry limit on gym equipment; devised the decoy-Ricky plan.

## Episode 3 — played (Sessions 5–8)
> [!note] Full accounts in [[Session 7 — Second Shift]] and [[Session 8 — Second Shift]]
> **Session 7:** charmed round 1 and dropped by the Siren; told Dominique everything before she had to ask; **Mended [[Ruth Bielak|Ruth]]'s tape player** — *"I just signed her death warrant"* — and **built [[The Modded Talkboy]]**.
> **Session 8:** declined to carry the Talkboy (*"likes making them, not using them"*); put the crossbow bolt in the Siren that left her on her last legs; **noticed the decades of dust** in the cabin and found **the Debrief card**; pushed to read **Ren's notebook** (*"more of a suggestion"*); built the poisoned stew's bowl; laid the grease and ball bearings that covered the Pit Room retreat. Inferred from *TANK WUZ HEAR* that the dungeon moves **forward in time**, not on a loop. **Ruth's death warrant never came due** — the Siren's death ended the walking.
