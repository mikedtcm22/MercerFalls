# Patricia Vance

Up: [[Cast & NPCs]]

**Ricky's mother and Wade's wife — the frightened, wholly sincere adult at the center of [[Episode 2 — The Hush]]'s moral hinge. Both her husband and her son are missing, and she is the only person in Mercer Falls behaving like that is an emergency.**

## Profile
Mid-to-late thirties, in the Vance house with the porch light burning in the afternoon. Frazzled, frightened, and **not covering** — an Insight read gets exactly that, which is what makes her different from every other adult the PCs talk to. She isn't Filter-resistant like [[Russ]] or [[Wade Vance|Wade]]; she simply has a concrete, undeniable fact (two empty beds) that the fog can't smooth over. She has been ringing every shop on the strip asking after her boy, which is how [[Jerry]] knows to send the kids her way.

## Function in Episode 2
She is the **Vance-house unlock's payoff** and the source of the episode's most load-bearing item.

- **The bandana.** On a genuine connection (a sincere, scared kid — [[Tank]]'s presence is what opens her up), she presses **Ricky's blaze-orange hunting bandana** on them: *"If you see him, give him this and tell him to come home."* It does two jobs at the climax — it's the **Wade key** (he clocks his son's blaze orange from cover and breaks cover as an ally) and a **spore mask** (advantage on saves vs. the lair's spores, resistance to the poison). See [[Episode 2 — The Hush]].
- **F2.** From her, the rich version of "Wade is out *hunting,* not shipping Ricky to military school."

## The Patricia fork (post-disguise)
If the party's **"Ricky was seen in town" disguise gambit** succeeds, the rumor reaches her within a day and **she comes looking for them,** lit up with the first good news in a week. Everything downstream hangs on whether they correct her:

- **Path 1 — they tell her the truth.** Brutal to do, and it must be the *better* path or the game punishes honesty. She becomes the one adult who knows and believes. She gives the bandana freely, tells them where Wade went in, and adds **where his truck is parked and the sector he was working — a free success on the lair search.**
- **Path 2 — they let her believe.** She becomes their **quest-giver with a false quest:** bring Ricky home, and *get word to Wade that he's searching the wrong place.* They still get the bandana, under false pretenses — which sets up a hell of a moment when they meet Wade in the woods. **If they deliver the message, Wade breaks off a correct search to check town: ~a day lost, and he drops one condition band** (Sharp→Worn, Worn→Spent).
- **Path 3 — they never come, or never engage the rescue.** See below.

> [!danger] DM-only — the Bystander branch
> If the party **never engages the rescue** (they fight the [[The Hush|Hush]] purely because it's stalking [[Tank]]), Patricia runs out of options and **goes into the woods after Wade herself.** She gets close enough to the lair to be **in the spore haze** and collapses — poisoned by the same mechanic the PCs would have faced, and horribly apt: a mother walking into poison looking for her son.
>
> **Wade has to choose** — he can carry his son or his wife, not both. He gets Ricky out and comes back for her. Ricky comes home to a **hospitalized mother**, and his read is *"my mom got hurt while you were playing monster hunters"* → **Ricky 2 → 1 (Wary)**, or **0 (Hostile)** if she's badly hurt. A clean accelerant into [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]]. ⚠ Open: how badly she's hurt, and whether she recovers.
