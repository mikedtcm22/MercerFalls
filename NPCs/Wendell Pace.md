# Wendell Pace

Up: [[Cast & NPCs]]

**The janitor [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]] dragged into the dungeon as a hollowing victim — the man the PCs met alive under the juvenile [[The Hush|Hush]] in the [[The Pillared Reach|Pillared Reach]] and watched hollow on the [[The Buzzsaw Room|Buzzsaw]] catwalk. The campaign's first proof, seen on-screen, that "death in the dungeon isn't exactly death" — and, as of [[Session 7 — Second Shift|Session 7]], its first demonstration that recovery is real and comes with a permanent mark.**

## Profile
Middle-aged **school janitor** who also **busses tables at [[Marco's Diner]]** — the identity the party already knew him by (Bash recognized him on sight). Unremarkable; the kind of man a town doesn't miss quickly. Wesley brought him down as labor (and a hollowing seed). The blood trail across the [[The Spiked Corridor|Spiked Corridor]] and into the [[The Buzzsaw Room|Buzzsaw Room]] was his.

> [!note] As played (Episode 1 — Sessions 1–2)
> The party came on him in the [[The Pillared Reach|Pillared Reach]] as the juvenile Hush killed him, **intervened, and saved him**. He tagged along, barely verbal — then, crossing the [[The Buzzsaw Room|Buzzsaw]] catwalk during the boss wave, he was struck and **hollowed on-screen**: dissolved to motes, leaving a nullprint. [[Gern]] recognized the shape from his Certaintist workbooks.

## The arc, in three states

| When | State |
|---|---|
| **Ep 1–3** | **Hollowed.** Glassy at Marco's, wiping the same section of counter. An Insight check catches the wrongness — lights on, nobody home |
| **Ep 3, mid** | **Recovered.** ~2 weeks. Cheerful, refilling waters, no memory |
| **Ep 3, close** | **Walking.** Notched, therefore susceptible |

> [!danger] The turn — [[Session 7 — Second Shift|Session 7]]
> The party found him **recovered** at Marco's and interviewed him. Friendly, undefended, useless:
>
> *"That's odd you say that. I do have the distinct impression that I was there recently. But I've been real out of it the past couple days... I do sort of have some recollection of making my way up there."* — then he trails off, changes the subject, and asks whether they're enjoying summer vacation.
>
> **Insight 13: he isn't lying.** He is genuinely grasping at something, failing to reach it, and giving up mentally. [[Bash Whitlock|Bash]] hummed the Siren's song at him and got nothing. **[[The Reality Filter|The Filter]], demonstrated on a man they watched die.**

> [!danger] He becomes a walker — and it's a payoff, not a contradiction
> The party explicitly puzzled over why Wendell **wasn't** among the sleepwalkers. The answer, which is sitting in their own conversation: **currently-hollowed people don't walk, because there's nothing in there to call.**
>
> **He recovered. That makes him notched. That makes him susceptible.** From the Session 8 cold open he is out at the gate with the rest.
>
> **He is one of two people the party has personally put on that road** — [[Ruth Bielak|Ruth]] is the other, and she's there for the opposite reason. Wendell walks because he *got better.*

## Function in the design

Wendell was the **"death below isn't final"** seed. He is now also the **control group** for [[Nullprints|the notch]]: the one person the party could name who was visibly wrong and *not* walking, until he stopped being visibly wrong.

If they ever assemble it themselves — *hollowed people don't walk; recovered people do* — that's the susceptibility rule derived unaided, off an NPC they've been eating lunch next to since June.

> [!danger] DM-only
> Wendell is **Wesley's** victim — a link the players cannot make yet and shouldn't. Hold it for the [[Wesley Crane]] recognition arc. His hollowing also feeds [[Jerry]]'s "who's been acting strange" whisper-count.
>
> **His second hollowing, whenever it comes, runs longer** — see the curve in [[Hollowing and Agency-Loss]].
