# Dominique Ferraro

> [!danger] DM-only
> To the players, Dominique is [[Bash Whitlock|Bash]]'s cool older cousin and the party's trusted aboveground "heart." She is secretly [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]]'s apprentice/heir and the [[The Equinox Ritual|ritual]]'s bloodline anchor. Seed every step as *good news;* the reread is what hurts.

**[[Bash Whitlock|Bash]]'s older cousin (~18) — bookish but well-liked, the bridge between the nerds and the popular crowd — established early as the PCs' aboveground operator, and revealed late as [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]]'s chosen heir and the ritual's [[Elias Mercer|Mercer]]-bloodline anchor.**

**Tonal direction: Mercer Falls' own Anakin Skywalker.** Not a mole and not a victim — a bright, loved, genuinely good person *walked* into it one defensible step at a time, in full view, agency intact.

## Who she is (the players' view)

~18, a Ferraro (Bash's maternal cousin). Bookish and scholarly — she shares Bash's curiosity, which is why he idolizes her — and genuinely *liked:* easy in any room, welcome among nerds and popular kids alike. She got Bash into computers; she's [[Tank]]'s crush. Drives. In and out of Bash's kitchen constantly, on good terms with [[Beth Whitlock]].

> [!note] ⭐ She works part-time at the [[Mercer Falls Historical Society|Historical Society]]
> Established [[Session 7 — Second Shift|Session 7]]. Filing, assistant-librarian work.
>
> **This is load-bearing.** Her design has always said she's bookish enough to follow the [[The Mercer Mansion|Mercer papers]] herself, and that it's partly how Wesley spots his heir. **That now has an address and a job title**, and nobody will ever think twice about her being in that building at odd hours.
>
> It also gives her town-wide soft access: she knew [[Ruth Bielak|Ruth]] because Ruth comes in.

## Current state — [[Session 7 — Second Shift|Session 7]]

**She sat with Bash through an entire hollowing.** Came by, found him glassy, and **stayed the night.** *"I tried everything to get through to you yesterday. You barely knew who I was. You were cordial, and if I said things you would respond, but it was clear he didn't really know what was going on. It was really scary."*

> [!danger] The designed confrontation never fired
> The grievance — *"you didn't tell me you were going"* — **never came up**, because Bash volunteered everything before she had to ask. She was frightened, then supportive, then useful.
>
> **Don't force it retroactively.** If it ever surfaces it should be much later, and quieter: she has now watched her cousin die twice and been told about it after the fact both times.

She reported multiple people at the diner losing sleep, and confirmed **she sleeps fine** — correct; she has no notch.

**She is Filter-porous, and it's Bash's fault.** Belief is what makes [[The Reality Filter|the Filter]] fail. He told her the story, then conjured a torch, then mended a pencil, then cast Guidance on her driving. She's most of the way to belief and still reaching for "magic trick" as a handrail.

## The pressure-point — an absence, not a wound

Dominique isn't broken, so Wesley's cure-the-broken-leg pitch doesn't work on her. Her crack is **rootlessness:** the universal bridge belongs *everywhere and nowhere*. [[The Community Center|The Center]] is the first place that feels like a **family with her near its centre**, and Wesley offers the one thing no one else can: **not healing, but partnership.**

## Generating the fractures

**Grievances major from her side and nearly invisible from theirs.** The unifying axis is **welcome vs. membership.**

1. **She's left at the threshold, every time.** Structurally unavoidable, therefore endlessly repeatable.
2. **She is handed errands and calls it trust.**
3. **She's told the truth and cannot verify a word of it.**
4. **Her wins become their wins.**
5. **She is talked *about* more than talked *to*.** Especially by Bash.

> [!danger] The Window — armed
> Bash's precognition arc has him seeing **"something terrible happening to Dominique."** The reading is *accurate* (her at the ritual, aligned with Wesley); his interpretation is wrong. **She knows the Window exists.** Do not spend this early.

# The mechanics

> [!danger] The design lock
> The lesson the party learns — *"Dominique gets us answers we can't get"* — **is the mechanism that loses her.** Make the useful option **cheap** and the including option **cost something**, then track honestly which they pick.

## The Adult Access ladder

| Who asks | Effect | TP |
|---|---|---|
| **PCs alone** | **DC +5.** Some doors don't open at all | — |
| **PCs with Dominique fronting** | **Base DC**, and she can Help for advantage | **1** |
| **Dominique alone** | **Advantage, +2** — and some things become possible that otherwise aren't | **0** |

**Triggers the +5:** grief, health, money, family trouble, drinking, why somebody lost a job or was "off." Anything after dark. Anything where the honest answer is embarrassing.

**Worked exactly as designed in S7:** four teenagers on a widow's porch is a closed door; *"She might open up a little bit more if I'm there too"* is tea in the kitchen.

## Limits

- **One lead per day.** She has her own life and says so pleasantly.
- **She won't go anywhere dangerous.**
- **She won't lie to an adult she actually knows.** She'll shade and omit; she will not lie to [[Beth Whitlock]]'s face.

> [!note] Two watch-items
> **The party has not yet discovered the free option.** Nobody has sent her anywhere alone, so the −1 engine has not engaged. The first time they say *"can you go ask her about it?"* the drift starts.
>
> **[[Old Man]] is openly flirting with her** (Tank objected on Bash's behalf). **Score flirting at zero.** Attention is not membership, and reading it as such gives a false positive that will corrupt the whole ledger.

**Read at [[The Equinox Arc|the Equinox]]:**

| Score | Outcome |
|---|---|
| ≤ −6 | Lieutenant, fully committed |
| −5 to 0 | Lieutenant, but reachable at Interest 4+ |
| **+1 to +5** | **Drifting but conflicted. Reachable in one scene** ← *currently here* |
| ≥ +6 | **She never becomes the heir.** Wesley needs someone else |

> The math is deliberately steep. Five or six episodes at two-to-three deployments each is a −10 to −18 baseline. **The +4 is fragile and early.** It will erode the moment the party optimizes.

## Her hurt looks like extra grace

Wounded, she doesn't escalate — she gets *more gracious*, smooths it over, moves to being useful. **She always offers an off-ramp**, and taking it is free and feels kind. Declining it is the only unambiguous way a PC shows her she's a member rather than a resource.

## If the party actually wins her

Her two roles **come apart:**

- **Bloodline anchor** — the only Mercer-line participant available. If she refuses, **the ritual cannot complete.**
- **Apprentice/heir** — a role somebody else could fill.

**The fix: Wesley needs another Mercer.** Losing his heir doesn't stop him — it sends him looking, and now the party has a **different** person to protect who they've never met.

**And the natural replacement heir is [[Ren Sołtys]]**, who is about to arrive at his door asking for method. **If they save Dominique by including her, the seat is filled by the girl they also got hollowed.**

> [!note] The inclusive path has its own way of losing her
> A Dominique who feels like a **member** follows them in. A courier waits by the phone. **High affinity makes it more likely she eventually goes into the dungeon after them**, and she has no ember. Planned: she finds crucial intel *after* the party has gone down, follows to deliver it, succeeds, and is **hollowed** for a long stretch — long enough that the party may forget her across sessions. **The tracker never becomes a "solved it" button.**

## The fall

She **becomes**, by degrees, in plain sight: belonging → status → rationalization. **Not hollowed and not a victim** in Act 1 — she keeps full agency, which is the horror. **Every step of her drift must look, in the moment, like good news.**

> [!danger] The woman-in-the-van betrayal
> The party hands her the town-side follow-ups — including checking on the Rivals. They think she's helping the Rivals warm toward the party. She's walking each one **further into Wesley's family.** They aren't failing to protect the Rivals; they're *delivering* them, through the person they trust most.

## Mechanical role

- **Bloodline anchor:** the ritual cannot complete without a Mercer-line participant. **Exempt from the hollowing qualifier** — it needs her *blood*, and her lack of the lost-a-week look is a quiet tell.
- **Continuation-regardless:** if Wesley dies pre-equinox, **Dominique completes the ritual** — unless pulled back.
- **Reachable — but only just.** Not won by Time-Points disposition. Reachable only if a PC refuses the misdirection and **sees her.** If the ritual succeeds, she's **corrupted, not sacrificed.**
