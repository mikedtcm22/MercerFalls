# Stan Prokop

Up: [[Hollowed Townspeople]]

**58, ex-mill, Ellis Street — first ring, closest to the mill. A [[The 1991 Mill Incident|1991]] survivor who "lost some time" and never quite came back. He has been walking to that gate since night one, longer than anybody, and in the middle of [[Episode 3 — Second Shift]] he is the one who finally gets through the fence.**

## Why him

**The man who has been walking longest is the one who finds a way in.** Causally satisfying rather than arbitrary, and it starts a clock the party can feel: **every night, somebody might get through.** That's the pressure that justifies going back down without a long rest.

- **Ellis Street** — inside the innermost ring. He was in the first three.
- **1991 survivor**, so he's been notched five years and is one of the deepest cases in town.
- **A [[Marco's Diner|Marco's]] counter regular**, so the party has plausibly seen him for two episodes without noticing.

## The mid-episode hollowing

He gets through **on the night the party is out of it after the [[The Siren|Siren]] wipe** — which means it happened *because* they lost, and nobody was watching.

**Reported by [[Dominique Ferraro|Dominique]]**, who is the only adult-adjacent person in town who knows what a hollowing looks like up close, because she spent the previous day sitting with [[Bash Whitlock|Bash]]:

> *"And Stan Prokop's at Marco's this morning and he's not right. Marco's got him wiping the same section of counter."*
>
> *A beat. She's careful with this part.*
>
> *"He looks like you did yesterday."*

**Then there are two glassy bussers at Marco's** — Prokop and [[Wendell Pace|Wendell]] — and Marco doesn't connect them. The party eats there every week.

> [!note] Why not Tomek
> [[Tomek Sołtys|Tomek]] is deliberately held back. His getting through is the trigger for **[[Ren Sołtys|Ren]] following him in** at the episode's climax. Prokop takes the mid-episode slot so Tomek is available for the ending.

## Duration

**First recorded event: 1991, extended.** This is his second on the curve, so per [[Hollowing and Agency-Loss]] it runs **longer than the first** — weeks at minimum, possibly the start of a terminal slide. He is a warning about where the roster ends up if the party is slow.

He does **not** recover inside this episode.

## As the cold open (retired)

He was the original cold-open subject before [[Tomek Sołtys|Tomek]] took the slot — a fifty-eight-year-old hearing his mother calling him in off the porch on Ellis Street, thirty years dead, and being **relieved**, because for five years he has not been certain of anything. That prose survives in the run-of-show drafts and can be reused if you ever need a second cold open in this key.

## Open dials

- His household — wife, per the retired cold open. Unwritten.
- Whether he recovers at all, or becomes the campaign's second [[Henry Voss]].
- Whether the party ever works out that he got in on the night they were hollowed.
