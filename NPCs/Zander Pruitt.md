# Zander Pruitt

Up: [[Cast & NPCs]]

**The fourth of [[Wesley's Recruits]] — debuting in [[Episode 5 — The Mirror]] as a rival investigator. The town liar with one true thing: a lovable scoundrel the audience roots for, built as the foil to [[Bash Whitlock|Bash]]. Eventually runs mechanically as a Wild Magic Sorcerer.**

## Portrait (stable)

- **A lovable scoundrel, not an amoral opportunist.** If the table were an audience, they'd be rooting for him.
- **Fake names by reflex.** Hustles everybody, adults included; fences odds and ends to [[Jerry]]; gives away more than he keeps.
- **The liar with one true thing.** Nobody believes him, because about anything else nobody should.
- Small, underestimated, Chaos-coded improviser.
- **Friendly rival from day one** — not a victim, not a gate, not an interview (shape-variety rule, [[Episodes]]).

---

## Design thesis (stable)

**The Bash foil.** [[Bash Whitlock|Bash]]'s creed is *it's all readable — you just need the right instrument.* Zander gets results with no instruments and no theory — nerve, a VHS tape, and whatever he can lift — and won't hold still to be measured. He is a walking refutation who doesn't care that he is. **In Ep5 the foil lands as a choice for Bash:** a person's word against an instrument that can't read it.

> [!danger] DM-only
> Bash's gear is the Entity's antenna. Zander's luck may be the same signal with no instrument at all. **Two antennas — one tuned, one not.**

**The agency thesis.** The campaign's argument is that agency is the answer. Most of Zander's life runs on outcomes he *didn't choose* — luck, hustle, improvisation — which makes him the recruit [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]] barely has to work on: the pitch is *hand me the wheel*, and Zander handed most of it to luck years ago. **The flashes of frightening competence are load-bearing, not a gag** — they're the moments he takes the wheel back.

**Disposition hinge:** the party wins him by **catching him being deliberate and naming it out loud.** Pitfall: praising his luck. **In Ep5 the deliberate thing is his one-kid war for Cole, so the hinge becomes *believe the liar*** — pitfall: any version of *"prove it."*

**Wild Magic as chassis:** a caster whose power is *outcomes without choice.* Keep on-screen magic to one unexplained step in his method. No more than that in Act 1.

## Ep5 role — rival investigator

Full treatment in [[Episode 5 — The Mirror]]. In brief:
- **He's been hunting the vampire alone on a con artist's budget** — a Super Soaker of holy water lifted from the Catholic church's font, garlic in Cole's dinner, NyQuil in his Mountain Dew, sleeping across his brother's door. He fences what he can to Jerry for stakes and a pawn-shop crossbow.
- **He lies to the party to protect Cole from them** — they're hunting pale kids, and his brother is one of the things they're hunting.
- **The confession beat:** he watches Cole try to follow the gang into the Panels glass, and the glass won't take him. *Not yet.* He can't follow (he casts a reflection), so the only way down is the party's — and to get their help, the liar has to tell the truth.
- **His method:** cover the Panels mirror while the thing is in town and let dawn do it. Faster than the party's dungeon route, and far more dangerous.
- **The rupture:** if Cole comes back, Zander is still the kid who cried vampire and the party can't back him up without exposing the dungeon — and Wesley tells him *"I believe you."* If Cole turns, the party may have to choose whether to kill Zander's brother.
