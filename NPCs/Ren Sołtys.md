# Ren Sołtys

Up: [[Wesley's Recruits]]

**Fifteen. Born here. Has a paper route in the Flats and four years of a composition notebook recording every person in Mercer Falls who went strange and came back. She is the only person in town who has noticed the pattern, because she is the only person who wrote it down before she could forget. Rival, [[Old Man]]'s foil, debuted [[Session 5 — Second Shift|Session 5]]. Hollowed at the end of [[Episode 3 — Second Shift]] ([[Session 8 — Second Shift|Session 8]]), alongside her brother.**

## Who she is

Rising sophomore. A **lifer** — people have looked through her since she was small. The route is 4:30–6:00 for eleven dollars a week, and because it's the only two hours of the day nobody talks to her.

**She reads people in order to disappear. [[Old Man]] reads people in order to manage them.** That's the whole foil, and it's why every scene with her is his arena.

**Voice:** short sentences. Answers the question asked and not a word past it. When uncomfortable she gets *more* precise, not less. Openly sceptical of claims she can't check, and writes them down anyway.

**Hobby: gymnastics** *(established [[Session 7 — Second Shift|Session 7]])*. Shy about it. Uncomfortable discussing anything she likes. *(Session 9: her mother says she has a medal from two years ago and is proud of it.)*

## Why the notebook exists

**October 1992.** Her brother [[Tomek Sołtys|Tomek]] was seventeen. Some kids dared him to stay the night at the mill and he did. He came back and *"it wasn't the same person — he would eat if you put food in front of him, other than that he wasn't there."* **Two weeks** later he was fine.

Then the whole town closed over it like water. **Her mother does not remember it happened.** *(Session 9, Reading Glass vision: November 1992, her mother in the kitchen remembering it as a fever.)*

She was eleven. She started writing things down **because she watched everyone around her stop noticing, and she was terrified it would happen to her too.** What she says, near-verbatim, clutching the book:

> *"I started writing this stuff down because everybody else forgot. And I don't want to forget."*

**This makes her the party's mirror.** PCs aren't [[The Reality Filter|Filtered]]. Ren is Filter-*porous* and knows she's leaking.

## The notebook

Player-facing handout exists: three pages — **WHAT I THINK** (nine theories, four struck through and dated), **THE LIST** (four years of durations), and the walker roster. Her theory is a genuine third position against [[Certaintism]] and Mercer both: no cosmology at all, and **the forgetting treated as the central phenomenon.**

**Her fatal error is item 5: she believes writing it down is what protects her.** Half-right — attention is what makes the Filter fail — but the book protects her *memory*, not her *person*, and she has quietly concluded she's safe.

## What she did in [[Session 7 — Second Shift|Session 7]]

> [!danger] She solved the episode at a diner counter
> Unprompted, off her own two pages, she explained the **radius model** and then found the anomaly herself:
>
> *"Wait a minute — [[Ruth Bielak|Ruth]] lives on Ellis Street. She should be walking. If the closest people get called first she should be up there. I don't understand why it's not affecting her."*
>
> This was designed as [[Dominique Ferraro|Dominique]]'s beat. **Giving it to Ren is better** — it gives the party a reason to value her that isn't sentiment. She isn't a source they extract from; she solved it in front of them.

**Her line to the party:** *"I don't understand how it's so short for you."*

**Old Man asked to meet up and learn more, and she said yes** — *"It'd be nice for somebody to not forget all the time. It's no fun being the only one."* He gave her his number. **She already knew where he lived.** *(The meet-up never happened. **The diner was her last conversation with the party as herself.**)*

One sour beat: the party mentioned having *"killed three or four people"* down there and **she flinched and physically shifted away.**

## Disposition — high, and that changes the ending

**She has not been burned.** No careless handling, no third party, no exposure. She has been treated as a competent source by people who took her seriously and gave her a phone number.

> [!danger] Her hollowing now lands as loss, not betrayal
> The original design had her resentful or at least ambivalent. **That's off the table.** She goes in liking them.
>
> Which is worse, and correct: **the last thing she does before she stops being a person is agree to be somebody's friend.**

**Reachable through:** being treated as a competent source · being allowed not to explain herself · **"You were right and nobody asked you."**

**Pitfalls:** being thanked where anyone can hear · being asked how she knows · being described to a third party · [[Gern]] trying to save her · anyone calling it a *hobby* · **casual talk about killing.**

## Her hollowing — as played, [[Session 8 — Second Shift|Session 8]]

**Circumstance-driven, not [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]]-driven** — as designed.

- **[[Tomek Sołtys|Tomek]] was at the gate** at ~3 a.m. with the other walkers, testing the fence. [[Old Man]] broke him out of it for six seconds with the Talkboy and said one word to him: ***"Remember."***
- Sometime after the party went down, **Tomek got in and Ren went in after him.**
- **After the Siren was already dead**, the party heard **a young person's scream** — *a horror-movie scream* — from the direction they'd come in. **They chose to explore the unexplored passage north instead.**
- On the way back they found **two fresh, dark nullprints in [[The Octagonal Vestibule]]**, the smaller one Ren's, with **the marble composition book on the floor next to it.** Both "outside" and "inside" closing shots then fired: at dawn the party watched **Ren and Tomek walk home together, glazed.**
- **The permission is still the party.** She watched four kids go in and come back in a day, twice, and said so at the diner. Her model updated the only way it ever has. **Ren is hollowed by her own methodology.**

> [!warning] Open (held by the DM)
> - **What took them.** The Siren was dead; the cause is undecided. **Now needed** before the search-and-rescue can run (options: a Dogmen patrol from the Pit Room; something the Siren's death let in; the Vestibule itself at night).
> - Whether the party ever learns **they heard her and went the other way.**

**Nine days** (back around Saturday 20 July) — **unless the party carries her in.**

## The threshold rule — search and rescue (decided 2026-10-17)

> [!important] She can come back early, and it's dangerous
> Per [[Nullprints]]: a hollowed person who sets foot across the archway into [[The Cataloguing Office]] — **carried counts** — vanishes and reconstitutes at their live print, themselves. If the party carries Ren in, **she wakes alone on her Vestibule print, beside Tomek's**, with no bag, no notebook, and no idea what day it is.
>
> - **She goes deeper, looking for Tomek**, following Wesley's tally marks (someone who counts is someone who gets out), scratching **R →** on door frames. The party has to find her before something takes her down again.
> - **If she falls again,** her own rule holds: *always longer the second time.* Weeks, not days.
> - **If they get her out,** she's restored topside. Ep5 changes: no nine blank days; she knows the dungeon is real, knows they carried her in without asking, and knows they have her notebook. **Her road to Wesley changes shape:** she now knows how to wake Tomek, and needs someone to help her do it.
> - **Carrying her in is taking her wheel** — Wesley's move, made by the heroes for good reasons.
> - Pages she writes **below** come up **blank topside**.
> - Scene prep: [Ep4 · B-SR — Search and Rescue](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/ef3eb4b8-d9e4-4594-8ae3-4f8b99aabbd4).

## Session 9 — the house, the bag, the envelope

- **Her mother, seen at last:** about fifty, housecoat and slacks, reading glasses pushed up on her head, radio low, ceiling fan, Center Pyrex on the counter. She **mistook Tank for "Zach"** — Ren had written the name by the phone. She **glitched visibly** when asked whether Ren had been like this before. Tank let slip he'd been "up by the mill" that night.
- **Ren at home, hollow:** eating cereal; acknowledged hello. **Tomek was not shown** — show him next visit.
- **The Center got in first, as designed:** [[Eddie Park]] delivers its meals to the porch.
- **The bag:** Ren came home without it. Paper-route collection money and **her gymnastics medal**. Her mother asked Tank to find it and offered a reward. *(Proposed: it's in the [[The Tollgate|Tollgate]] tribute pile, $31.50 and the medal, "1994 · Regional · 2nd"; reward $20 and a standing welcome.)* **Return it discreetly** — her mother telling a boy about the medal is already a third-party exposure.
- **The envelope for Old Man:** Ren had left a list of questions addressed to Zach — including *why is it only one day for you*, *Lowell Brandt went strange three times and then he died — is 3 the limit?*, *do you forget things too, or is it just everyone else?*, and a struck-out last line, ***Can I come next time?*** Old Man has it. **The Ep5 reunion, prebuilt:** answering her questions is "you were right and nobody asked you." Question 7 confirms she went in because of them.
- **Old Man refused to pay the Reading Glass with her:** offered the price "you agreed to meet Ren," he declined — *"I cannot abandon Ren."*
- **The notebook:** the party still holds it (used it at the library on Friday). **The notebook-to-mother burn has not fired.**

> [!note] Unfaced-thing watch ([[Nullprints]] filter-break ritual)
> Her medal and notebook **don't** fit (pride she carries; her defence). **The question list with *Can I come next time?* struck out is the strongest fit in the campaign** — she's running from needing anyone. Burned on her black print, it would catch, and she'd come back seeing. Never explain it if it happens.

### What sends her to Wesley

**Not resentment.** When she comes back she opens her own notebook and finds **nine days with nothing in them.** She became the thing she spent four years insuring against, and she needs someone who can tell her how to stop it happening again.

> [!danger] Sharpened by Session 8 — Tomek
> **Tomek went in a second time.** Her own rule — *always longer the second time* — says he'll still be gone when she wakes. **She comes back, and her brother doesn't.** Her need stops being *teach me to stop this* and becomes **stop it happening to him** — which is a far stronger reason to say yes to the only adult who claims he can.

Wesley is the only adult in Mercer Falls who will say *"I can teach you that,"* and mean it. **He has also known her since she was ten.** It won't be a cold approach.

> [!note] How the Center gets in first
> Somebody has to look after her for nine days. Her mother will, badly. And [[The Community Center|the Center]] brings meals to families in trouble. **Nine days of quiet, genuine kindness to the Sołtys house, before Ren is awake to see it** — now to a house with **both** children gone. *(Confirmed in play, Session 9: Eddie on the porch with the Pyrex.)*
