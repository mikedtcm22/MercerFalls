# Ren Sołtys

Up: [[Wesley's Recruits]]

**Fifteen. Born here. Has a paper route in the Flats and four years of a composition notebook recording every person in Mercer Falls who went strange and came back. She is the only person in town who has noticed the pattern, because she is the only person in town who wrote it down before she could forget. Rival, [[Old Man]]'s foil, debuted [[Session 5 — Second Shift|Session 5]].**

## Who she is

Rising sophomore. A **lifer** — people have looked through her since she was small, and that's the town's doing, not a circumstance of arrival. The route is 4:30–6:00 for eleven dollars a week, and because it's the only two hours of the day nobody talks to her.

**She reads people in order to disappear. [[Old Man]] reads people in order to manage them.** That's the whole foil and it's why every scene with her is his arena.

**Voice:** short sentences. Answers the question asked and not a word past it. When uncomfortable she gets *more* precise, not less — she retreats into detail. Openly sceptical of claims she can't check; when Old Man told her he'd died and come back she said *"that's a bit of a stretch,"* and wrote it down anyway.

## Why the notebook exists

**October 1992.** Her brother [[Tomek Sołtys|Tomek]] was seventeen. Some kids dared him to stay the night at the mill and he did. He came back and *"it wasn't the same person — he would eat if you put food in front of him, other than that he wasn't there."* **Two weeks** later he was fine.

And then the whole town closed over it like water. Nobody talked about it. **Her mother does not remember it happened.**

She was eleven. She started writing things down **because she watched everyone around her stop noticing, and she was terrified it would happen to her too.** She can't articulate it that way. What she says, near-verbatim, as a mantra, clutching the book:

> *"I started writing this stuff down because everybody else forgot. And I don't want to forget."*

**This makes her the party's mirror.** PCs aren't [[The Reality Filter|Filtered]]. Ren is Filter-*porous* and knows she's leaking.

## The notebook

Four years. ~50 "off" entries, most with an end date, plus a new column that opens **the week before last** for the night-walking, which she has never seen before.

| | |
|---|---|
| Durations | 6 days · 9 days · three weeks · a month |
| [[Tomek Sołtys\|Tomek]] | Oct '92 — **two weeks** |
| Repeat entries (2–3 people) | first: 5 days → second: **three weeks** |
| [[Henry Voss]] | first logged: **two months** → second: **no end date** |
| The party | **one day each** |

**Four deductions available, none of which she has made** — she thinks in entries, not aggregates. **She has never read the book. She has only ever written it.**

1. The second entry is always longer than the first. **It compounds.**
2. Voss's *first logged* entry is longer than anyone else's second — **his beginning isn't in this book.**
3. Something changed a week and a half ago. *(A piece of chalk.)*
4. **It doesn't compound for the PCs.** The one that matters.

## Disposition

**Starts at 2 (Neutral).** As of Session 5 she has met Tank and Old Man, been treated decently, and been told a great deal.

**Reachable through:** being treated as a competent source · being allowed not to explain herself · anyone who takes her warning seriously *even while ignoring it* · **"You were right and nobody asked you."**

**Pitfalls:** being thanked where anyone can hear · being asked how she knows · being described to a third party in front of her · [[Gern]] trying to save her · anyone calling it a *hobby*.

**The burn is carelessness, not strategy.** Word getting back to her that someone showed her book around. There is no clever play that avoids it, and adults can't act on the data anyway — they're Filtered, and they'll find a reason.

## What Session 5 changed

> [!danger] Old Man told her almost everything
> The dungeon, "shadow dogs," "time archivists," symbolic traps — and **"I definitely died at least once and now I'm not."**
>
> Her notebook now contains **the party's own testimony that the mill is survivable.** She isn't just holding observational evidence that four kids came back in a day. She has them saying so. **Old Man handed her the permission.**

## Her hollowing — end of [[Episode 3 — Second Shift]]

Every Rival needs one pre-[[The Equinox Arc|Equinox]] hollowing. Hers is **circumstance-driven, not [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]]-driven**, and it is the party's fault without being their doing.

**The trigger is Tomek.** Someone got through the fence and she is out of nights.

**The permission is the party.** She watched four kids go into that mill and come back **in one day.** Her brother took two weeks; four years of data say weeks. So her model updates the only way she has ever updated a model — **from observation.** *Maybe it isn't as bad as I thought.*

She's wrong, because of an ember she has no way of knowing exists. **Ren is hollowed by her own methodology.** Nobody lied to her, nobody invited her, nobody was careless.

**Nine days.** Gone for the whole of [[Episode 4 — The Crossing]]; back in Ep5.

### The closing shot

Not a rescue. She should be un-preventable and un-savable. The party clears the sigil, the walking stops, and on the mill road in the small hours:

> *Canvas bag. Composition book still in her hand — the way you'd still be holding something you were holding when the lights went out.*
>
> *"Ren?"*
>
> *She goes right past you.*
>
> *"Ren."*
>
> *She does not turn her head.*

**The cold open, inverted, with the same two lines.** Then:

> *You take the notebook out of her hand. She lets you.*

Four years of the most carefully guarded object in Mercer Falls, handed over without a flicker, by a girl who built it specifically so she would never become someone who stopped noticing.

### What sends her to Wesley

**Not resentment, and not the party burning her.** When she comes back she opens her own notebook and finds **nine days with nothing in them.** She became the thing she spent four years insuring against, and she needs someone who can tell her how to stop it happening again.

Wesley is the only adult in Mercer Falls who will say *"I can teach you that,"* and he'll mean it. **He has also known her since she was ten** — a strange kid at [[The Community Center|the Center]] that a decent man was never unkind to. It won't be a cold approach. It'll be that same man finally being told what she's been writing down.

**Her recruitment is airtight, fully earned, and not Wesley-caused at any point.**

> [!note] How the Center gets in the door first
> Somebody has to look after her for nine days. Her mother will, badly. And the Center brings meals to families in trouble. **That's Wesley's entry — nine days of quiet, genuine kindness to the Sołtys house, before Ren is awake to see it.**

## What the party does about it

The problem isn't logistics. **They made a promise to someone who doesn't exist for nine days.** They can use the book, copy it, hand it to an adult, give it back — and she cannot object to any of it, and they know exactly what she would have said.

- **Bash will want to photocopy it.** Let him. It's correct, it's useful, and it's taking a permanent copy of a private document from a person who can't consent. **Don't editorialize.**

## Open dials

- Her **saving-grace line** isn't locked. Currently around *"you were right and nobody asked you."* The Act 1 warning is the obvious anchor; the moment needs picking.
- Her and Tomek's **mother** — referenced repeatedly, never seen. Worth one line of who she is.
- Whether the party ever learns she went in because of them.
