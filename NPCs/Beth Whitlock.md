# Beth Whitlock

Up: [[Cast & NPCs]]

**[[Bash Whitlock|Bash]]'s mother. Works nights, gets home in the early hours, frazzled and kind. Established in play, [[Session 5 — Second Shift|Session 5]] — she came home to find four teenagers in her kitchen at four in the morning and fed them cereal.**

## Who she is

Single mother, working nights. **Honey Nut Cheerios and Frosted Flakes** in the cupboard. Tired in the specific way of somebody holding a household together on an inverted schedule, and warm anyway.

**[[Dominique Ferraro|Dominique]] is her niece by marriage** and is in and out of the house constantly, on good terms.

## Why she matters

**1. She is the reason Bash can operate at all.** Bash lives in the basement; the Bilco doors are unlocked; she is at work most of the night. **He has functionally unlimited unsupervised time**, and it's nobody's fault — she's working. That's the household arrangement that makes a fifteen-year-old's dungeon habit possible, and it should stay quietly sad rather than negligent.

**2. She is [[Dominique Ferraro|Dominique]]'s hard limit.** Per Dominique's operating rules, **she will not lie to an adult she actually knows** — she'll shade and omit, but she will not lie to Beth's face. If the party ever needs Dominique to deceive Beth specifically, that's the one place she stops, and the refusal will surprise them.

**3. She's a Filter test case, unfired.** She has a son who has been hollowed once and comes home at strange hours with strange injuries. **How much she has noticed, and what she's told herself, is undecided** — and it's a good scene waiting whenever the campaign needs a parent to almost see something.

## Open dials

- Where she works nights. Unnamed so far.
- Whether Bash's father is anywhere in the picture.
- **How much she's noticed.** The most valuable unfired thing about her.
- Whether she's on any of the lists — [[Ren Sołtys|Ren]]'s roster, [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]]'s notebook, or the walker map. *(Recommended: no. Bash needs one person who is simply safe.)*
