# Timothy

Up: [[Cast & NPCs]]

**The Mercer Falls librarian — competent, a little lonely, and visibly pleased whenever anyone asks him for something. The town's memory has a keeper, and nobody uses him.**

## Profile
Runs the desk at [[The Library]]. Nobody requests local history in July, so a kid who walks in with a real research question gets a genuinely warm reception and better service than they expected. He's good at his job in a town that has no particular use for it.

## The rule that defines him
> **Timothy can pull *subjects* on request. He cannot notice a thing no one in this town has ever noticed.**

Delegated research yields **folders, not findings.** Ask him for the Mercers and he'll have a folder ready — free, no roll, and he'll be pleased to have made it. Ask him what happened to the missing kids and he'll pull the papers, but seeing the **disappearance pattern** requires laying two unrelated clippings side by side and connecting them, which is exactly what this town cannot do. That still costs the PCs their own eyes and a roll.

Design-wise this is the point: it rewards a party that thinks to *ask* without letting asking replace *investigating.* It also makes the Library **[[Old Man]]'s node** — he opened the relationship in Session 3 — the way [[Jerry's Pawn Shop]] became his through the guitars. Two nodes, one PC, both earned socially rather than by stat.

## In play
- **The Mercer folder** (free, or 1 TP, no roll) — eighty years of Mercer funeral coverage from the *Ledger*'s society pages, including the family's article-burning custom and the graves that face [[The Mill|the mill]]. See [[The Library]] for the full text and the Episode-3 seed it carries.
- **The microfilm** — he'll set up the reader and pull reels, but the pattern is theirs to find (1 pt DC 12 · 2 pt DC 10 · 3 pt DC 8).
- He is **not** Filter-resistant and not a mystery. He's a nice man with a good filing system — which is worth more to the party than most of the adults in town.

> [!note] DM
> Keep him uncomplicated. The episode already carries [[Reverend Hollis]] (a red herring), [[Russ]] and [[Wade Vance]] (the two Filter-resistant adults), and the gym coach (a deliberate near-miss). Timothy's job is to be **genuinely, plainly helpful** — a small kindness in an episode where every other adult is useless, hostile, or absent. Don't give him a secret.
