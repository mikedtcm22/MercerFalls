# Henry Voss

Up: [[Cast & NPCs]]

**A hollowed 1991 mill survivor who shrugs through his days behind the [[The Sinclair Station]] counter — and whose house, fed by dungeon-stone he doesn't remember carrying home, is becoming [[The Hungry House]].**

## Profile
A survivor of [[The 1991 Mill Incident]], hollowed five years ago and never recovered — **topside.** Works the [[The Sinclair Station]] counter — the campaign's walking proof of agency-loss. The embers' fire vocabulary, reversed, describes him: *no warmth, the ember gone out, ash behind his eyes, a stove banked too long.* He isn't lying when he says he doesn't remember things — he genuinely doesn't. See [[Hollowing and Agency-Loss]].

## The cycle (direction, 2026-10-17)

> [!danger] DM-only — Voss is a cycler, not a terminal case
> Under the threshold rule ([[Nullprints]]), a hollowed person who sets foot across the archway into [[The Cataloguing Office]] wakes on their live print, themselves. **Voss keeps doing exactly that.** Something in his routine walks him back into the mill; he wakes on his print, himself, deep underground — and fails to get out, and falls again. Each fall leaves a **deeper print and a longer hollowing.** Topside he has never once come back, so every record of him — [[Ren Sołtys|Ren]]'s notebook included — reads *no end date.*
>
> - **He is only ever himself underground.** A party that meets him down there may meet a lucid, frightened, very tired man who has no idea how long it's been.
> - **He leaves fresh prints, not old ones.** The "old black print" forensic signal belongs to someone who never walks back; Voss's prints are always new, and always deeper.
> - **Open:** what walks him back in. Candidate: the 1991 shift routine — the Sinclair sits on the mill road, and his body still keeps a shift he no longer works. Also open: whether he's emptying toward nothing, or just cycling slower each time.

## Arc / function ([[Episode — The Hungry House]])
After the 1991 incident, Voss walked home with a piece of dungeon-stone in his lunchbox — an artifact he doesn't remember picking up. The stone has slowly made his house an annex of the dungeon; a small chamber has formed in his basement and is growing. That house is **[[The Hungry House]]** on Pine Street: pets vanish near the front yard, then a kid on a bike, then the mailman (who turns up confused two days later). PCs resolve it by destroying or extracting the dungeon-stone (the basement chamber holds a small Floor-1-style native or two).

[[Marcy Hauer]] still checks on Voss — her brother Tommy was his friend before 1991 — which is the disposition-setting encounter for her episode. [[Jerry]] ([[Jerry's Pawn Shop]] owner) can note Voss is one of dozens of locals on a slow pawn-drip, the first whisper of the hollowed *pattern*.

> [!danger] DM-only
> **Wesley fingerprint: none** — Voss is independent of [[Wesley Crane]]'s operations; the episode plants the 1991-survivor thread. Voss is hard to Filter-pierce at Level 1 and probably won't be reached here; if Filter-broken later he'd say something Order-flavored. PCs may notice his slow drawl matches other townspeople — the first hint that 1991 wasn't an isolated accident. (One of the 2–3 hollowed mill workers named per bible §20 item 18; Voss at the Sinclair is locked.)
