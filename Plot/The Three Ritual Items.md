# The Three Ritual Items

Up: [[Plot & Structure]]

**The three dungeon-extracted materials [[Wesley Crane]] needs for [[The Equinox Ritual]], each retrieval a cornerstone of [[Wesley's Summer Calendar]].**

> [!danger] DM-only
> These items are the first of the ritual's three lock elements (the others are [[Dominique Ferraro]] as bloodline anchor and the four witness-recruits). Their summer retrieval — and the PCs' chance to deny them — directly sets the [[The Equinox Ritual|Ritual Progress Track]] starting count. See [[Aftermath Branches]].

> [!warning] Item #1 changed (2026-10-17)
> **The Tally Stone is retired** — it never appeared in play. **Item #1 is now the dungeon twin of [[The Mercer Mirror]]**, taken by Wesley at the end of [[Episode 5 — The Mirror]]. Its retrieval is the party's first legible loss and **kicks off the item race**: who is doing this, what he's after, and stopping the rest. Wesley's pre-Session-1 solo delve was his first push toward the mirror, not a Tally Stone retrieval. *(The "Tally Stone" alias is kept on this note so old links still land here.)*
>
> **Knock-on:** the old "one item from each upper floor" pattern breaks — Floor 1 now holds none and Floor 2 holds two (the mirror and Gathered Dust). Accept, or move one later.

## The Triad Theme

**Provisional** (bible §20, retheme proposed 2026-10-17): the dungeon's **door** / the dungeon's **substance** / the dungeon's **lock** — fitting a ritual whose purpose is opening [[The Mercer Gate]]. *(Previously record / substance / lock.)* Names remain provisional. The **Weight specifically interacts with the Mercer Gate's locking spell as the "lock" component** — it's the dungeon-side material the spell needs to release. The other two contribute to the ritual's accounting in roles still TBD.

## The Items

### 1. The Mercer twin — Floor 2, [[The Long Gallery]] (end of Ep5, late July)
The dungeon half of a twinned pair of Mercer looking-glasses — the dungeon's **door**. The pair passes anything that casts no reflection, and the dungeon set a guard on it: a vampire that rests in the glass. **While the guard lives, the mirror cannot leave the dungeon.** Wesley woke the guard trying to take it (Ep5 cold open) and was killed; the relic sent him home whole and he chose not to go back. **When the party kills the guard, the lock opens and Wesley takes the mirror**, leaving a fresh exit sigil where it hung. Full detail in [[Episode 5 — The Mirror]] and [[The Mercer Mirror]].

> *Retired:* **Tally Stone — Floor 1.** A flat stone marked with cuts, extracted ~a week before Session 1, with planted evidence of a fresh recess and a live sigil. Never surfaced to players; superseded by the Mercer twin.

### 2. Gathered Dust — Floor 2 (late July → proposed early August)
The residue of agency-loss, harvested fresh from a chamber where a civilian has just been hollowed — the dungeon's **substance**. [[Mike and Doug Tinsmith|Mike Tinsmith]]'s hollowing *is* the harvest; Wesley collects the residue while it's fresh. Downstream MOTW: [[Episode — Storm-That-Doesn't-Move]] (residue spillover at Mike's death-site).

> [!warning] Timing collision (open)
> Scheduled for late July on Floor 2 — the same week and floor as Ep5. **Proposed:** push the harvest to early August, so it's the first item the party can actually contest after the race begins. Wesley's fresh Floor-2 sigil at the end of Ep5 is the natural staging point.

### 3. The Weight — Floor 3 (early September, after a failed mid-August attempt)
A small iron disc with a **lock-shape** carved in — the dungeon's **lock**. The first attempt (mid-August) **fails**: Doug Tinsmith is hollowed, Wesley aborts via sigil, no retrieval. The second attempt (early September) **succeeds** with [[Phyllis Harrow]] as the hollowed collateral. See [[The Keystone Theft]] for the Floor 3 keystone Wesley steals around the same window — a separate object from the Weight.

## PCs Can Deny Them

Each item the PCs prevent Wesley from acquiring during the summer lowers the equinox [[The Equinox Ritual|Ritual Progress Track]]'s starting count (3 intact items = track starts 3/5; 0 items ≈ impossible for Wesley to finish in time). **The race now opens at the end of Ep5** with the mirror lost — unless the party hauls it out themselves, which denies item #1 outright. Gathered Dust and the Weight remain contestable. Reaching the Weight chamber on Floor 3 *before* Wesley becomes plausible for an aggressive Level-3 party with the longer summer. See [[Where the PCs Can Disrupt]].
