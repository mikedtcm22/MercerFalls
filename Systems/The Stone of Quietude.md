# The Stone of Quietude

Up: [[Gear and Items]]

**A dungeon-forged magic stone that swallows sound — a hand-held pocket of *Silence* the kids can carry, drop, or throw. Designed as the optional prize for the [[The Hush|Hush]] climax; never found in time for it. Found at last in [[Session 8 — Second Shift|Session 8]], behind a loose brick in [[The Spiral Stair Room]], by [[Gern]] — who matched the room to the photograph he'd been carrying since Session 1.**

> [!note] Status — found, held by Gern
> **Location resolved:** Floor 1, Room 8 — a loose brick with a secret compartment near the spiral stair, exactly where the Marv's Spot photo shows a man putting it back (*"a quiet rock… more trouble than it was worth"*).
> **Holder:** [[Gern]]. **Charges:** all spent in play the night he found it (see below); recharging.

## What it looks like
About the size of a softball — an oblate, river-smoothed lump that sits heavy in one palm, denser than a rock that size has any business being. Its surface is a matte, lightless charcoal-black — the same not-quite-stone the Hush's tooth is made of, the stuff that lines the dungeon's deepest architecture; light seems to *sink* into it rather than glance off. Worked into the face are faint concentric rings, like the still-water rings after a dropped pebble frozen mid-spread, tightening to a smooth central dimple. *(Described at the table as "a round stone with weird markings, and a depression almost like a button you could press.")*

It is cool to the touch and stays cool. Even dormant it has a *tell:* hold it and the room feels a half-second quieter, as if it's always faintly muffling the air at arm's length. Three of the incised rings hold a dim inner glow — **one lit ring per stored charge** — guttering out as the stone is spent and rekindling on a long rest.

## How it works
> [!info] Player-facing mechanics
> **Effect — *Silence*.** On activation, for the duration no sound can be created within or pass through a **20-foot-radius sphere** centered on the stone. Anything entirely inside is **immune to thunder damage**; creatures entirely inside are **deafened**; casting a spell with a verbal component inside is impossible.
> - **The stone is always the center.** The sphere follows the stone. Held, it's a moving bubble of silence that travels with you; set down or **thrown**, it anchors the bubble where the stone lands.
> - **Throwing it** is how you put the silence somewhere other than yourself — a chokepoint, or over a monster. But a thrown stone is a loose stone: **anyone can pick it up, kick it, or carry it off,** and nothing stops the bubble walking away with it.
> - **Activation:** an action — **press the central dimple.** *(As played in Session 8; replaces the earlier "tap it against another stone.")*
> - **Charges:** **3**, starts full. Each activation spends **1**. Regains **1d4** on a long rest (up to the max of 3).
> - **Duration:** 10 minutes, **no concentration** — the stone sustains it. Whoever activated it can end it early (no action) *while holding it* — pressing the dimple again; a thrown or dropped stone runs its full duration, or until someone else picks it up and dismisses it.
> - **No attunement.** Any hand that presses it can use it — which is exactly the risk the moment you throw it.

## Found — Session 8

Gern pulled it out and **pressed the dimple immediately**, over Old Man's warning. The party watched his friends come down the clanging iron stair in total silence, and then Gern did the Ace Ventura bit — press, release, press, release, voice cutting in and out. **That burned every charge.** He also tried it again at [[The Pit Room]] when Old Man asked for it; it was dead.

*"I told you guys this is a treasure spot. This will come in handy if we fight any more of these big boobie sirens!"* — Gern, correctly, about ten minutes too late.

## Against the Hush — now moot
This was the tool the Session-1 Marv's-Spot photo pointed toward. The [[The Hush|Hush]] hunts by sound, so it is **blinded by the absence of sound *or* by an overwhelming excess of it** — and the stone owns the silence end. **The Hush was resolved in [[Episode 2 — The Hush]] without it.**

**Going forward it's a general-purpose tool**, and a strong one: a portable, throwable, concentration-free 20-ft dead zone that shuts down verbal casters, voice-driven effects, and anything that hunts or communicates by sound. It cuts both ways — inside the sphere **the party is silenced too** — and rewards a table that plans hand-signals and picks its moment. **Worth checking against any sound-keyed encounter on [[Floor 2 — The Works|Floor 2]]** before the party walks in with it.

> [!danger] DM-only — what it actually is
> **Dungeon-Order tech** — a crystallized scrap of the containment machine's *suppressive* function (silence as the muting of chaos, of agency's noise). Same light-and-sound-swallowing not-bone material as the Hush's tooth: a recurring dungeon-stone signature to call back in [[Cosmology Seeds]] / [[The Pattern Recognition Arc]] / [[Episode — The Click]].
>
> **It now sits in the hands of an Order Domain cleric** (Gern, level 3). A suppressive Order artifact carried by the kid whose arc is inherited Order — not something to underline, but available.
