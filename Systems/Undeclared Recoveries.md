# Undeclared Recoveries

Up: [[Gear and Items]]

**Running register of things the party has pulled out of the dungeon that don't yet have a declared function. One line each until they matter. Anything here that gets resolved graduates to its own note and comes off this list.**

> [!note] Why this exists
> Per [[The Recall]], **anything carried out persists** — items are the one category the dungeon never reclaims. So loot accumulates permanently, and undeclared loot is a standing pile of unfired guns. This is the pile.

## Open

### The ornate silver hand mirror
**Recovered [[Session 5 — Second Shift|Session 5]]**, from the footlocker in [[The Buzzsaw Room]] (unopened on the first delve). Ornate, silver, hand-sized. **Nobody at the table remarked on it.**

> [!danger] Free connection — do not waste it
> [[The Mercer Mirror]] is a banked monster and [[Episode — The Mirror]] is a banked episode anchored on it at [[Panels]]. **The party has been carrying an ornate silver hand mirror since Session 5 without blinking.**
>
> Cleanest version: it is **not** the Mercer Mirror — it's a lesser thing of the same make, and the moment [[Russ]] sees it he goes quiet, because he has seen one before. That converts a dungeon trinket into the hook for a whole banked episode and costs nothing now.
>
> **Do not connect it for them until Panels.**

### The shimmering flask
**Recovered [[Session 5 — Second Shift|Session 5]]**, from the 6-o'clock strongbox in [[The Octagonal Vestibule]]. Unlabelled; the liquid shimmers. **Arcana 16: not ordinary, something unique about it — no further read.**

Undeclared. Worth deciding *before* someone drinks it, because someone will. Options worth weighing: a single-use ember-adjacent effect; something that interacts with [[Nullprints|prints]] or [[The Recall|the recall]]; or a genuinely mundane-but-strange industrial residue that means Elias Mercer's people were refining something down there.

### The Polaroid of dogman-Old Man
**Taken [[Session 5 — Second Shift|Session 5]].** [[Old Man]] disguised as a dogman, holding a bell. Roughly **one shot left** on the camera.

Not a mystery — a resource, and a **dwindling** one. Per [[The Recall]], photographs outlive their subjects, so film is the party's only durable proof of anything. Track the count.

### The tinkered scrap bell
Bash's improvised bell from Session 5, pocketed by Old Man. **Ambiguous duration** — Bash's Tinker items were ruled to dissipate at the end of a long rest, but the scrap-bell rule was 1 minute. **Needs a ruling; it may simply not exist any more.**
