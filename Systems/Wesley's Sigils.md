# Wesley's Sigils

Up: [[Game Systems]]

**[[Wesley Crane|Wesley]]'s anti-ember relic grants a class of inscribed sigil abilities that scale as he integrates further with [[The Dungeon|the dungeon]] — i.e., as he is consumed by it. To the PCs these read as Wesley *mastering* the dungeon; the truth is the boundary between Wesley and dungeon thinning.** Everything here is DM-only.

Each sigil requires **inscription** (drawn with dungeon-extracted chalk; takes time; visible once placed). **PCs cannot use sigils** — the relic is the prerequisite, and even capturing Wesley's notebook leaves the inscriptions inert in PC hands.

> [!danger] Locked rule — one active exit sigil at a time
> **Wesley can only have one live exit sigil.** Drawing a new one **renders the previous one inert.** He does not maintain a network; he maintains a *position*, and moving it is a real decision with real cost.
>
> **The Act-1 chain:**
> - Session 1 — the party watched him trigger his exit sigil in [[The Buzzsaw Room|the Millrace]] and vanish.
> - **Within a day** he re-entered through it, pushed deeper, and drew a new one in [[The Spiral Stair Room|Room 8]]. **The Millrace sigil went inert at that moment.**
> - The toll for the new one is [[The Siren]] and her retinue — which starts the sleepwalking in [[Episode 3 — Second Shift]].
>
> **Open question:** did he move it *because* the party saw it? If yes, the causal chain is real — they went down, he relocated, the toll landed in the shaft, the town started walking. Their guilt in Ep3 would be **warranted but misattributed.**

> [!note] ✅ ANSWERED — what an inert sigil looks like
> **[[Session 6 — Second Shift]]:** the party returned to the Millrace and found **white chalk, still clearly there on the floor, no longer glowing.** Locked.
>
> **What this hands them, unaided:**
> - The chalk **survived a reset**, so it isn't a dungeon feature — it's a *person's* mark ([[The Recall]]).
> - It was live in Session 1 and is dead now. **Something changed down here inside a week.**
> - And the thing through the archway is **lit green in exactly the same way.**
>
> Same hand, one dead, one live, a week apart. That's takeaway #3 sitting on the floor. **Do not connect it for them.**
>
> When they clear the shaft sigil, *"the chalk goes to ordinary dust"* should read as **the same white chalk they already found** — the rhyme that tells them what they just did to somebody.

## Level 1 — Real Sigils (active from Session 1)

- **Exit:** Wesley walks through any door in the room and wakes at home, fully restored. The exit-side stays active **1–2 minutes** — long enough for an astute PC to ride the same door out.
- **Cost:** monsters spawn in the room and **persist** — bound to the sigil until defeated, at which point the sigil dissipates.
- **Re-entry:** he can return to the sigil's location **once**, from a paired private site (a back room at [[The Community Center|the community center]]). Once used, the sigil is spent and can be redrawn.

**Cat-and-mouse.** Clearing a real sigil forces him to redraw, costing significant delve progress. **Given the one-at-a-time rule, this is worse for him than it looks** — it doesn't cost him a node in a network, it costs him *his only foothold*, and he has to delve from the entrance to re-establish one.

> [!note] The chalk survives resets
> Per [[The Recall]], the dungeon restores its own inventory and does not touch marks left by visitors. **Wesley's chalk, tallies and cartography persist across resets** — which is why the party could follow them in Session 1.
>
> Corollary: **the party is leaving marks too**, and Wesley will eventually walk past a hole in a wall that says TANK WUZ HEAR.

## Level 2 — Decoy Sigils (mid-Act 1)

Visually similar, subtly distinct on close reading.

- **Proximity-triggered**; summons monsters as a real sigil does.
- **No exit, no re-entry.** Baits PCs into burning resources for nothing.
- **Decoys do not count against the one-active-exit limit.** He can maintain several.

The first decoy-clear tells the PCs Wesley is *gaming the meta*, not just defending positions.

> [!note] Detection just got easier, and he doesn't know it
> The party has now seen **live green** and **dead white** in the same dungeon. A decoy is a third state. Their sample size is growing fast, and their [[The Recall|graffiti experiment]] has already taught them that chalk is evidence.

## Detection Paths

- **Visual pattern recognition** over many encounters
- **Glyph-language decoding** (see [[Forensic Delving]])
- **Forensic deduction** — his chalk-cartography around real sigils differs from around decoys
- **A captured notebook** resolves it instantly — highly-prized loot

## Anti-Embers as an Item Type

Sigils flow from the **anti-ember relic** — the Order-side mirror of the PCs' embers, ~100% Order (see [[The Embers]] / [[Order and Chaos]]). The equinox produces anti-embers for the [[Wesley's Recruits|Rivals]].

## Banked Abilities (Acts 2–3, not committed)

Pattern: early = *drawing* → middle = *mapping/shaping* → late = *summoning* → endgame = *being*.

- **Trapdoor Sigil** — exit lands in a different room than inscription.
- **Shared Sigil** — other anti-ember bearers can use his sigils.
- **Silent Sigil** — exit doesn't summon monsters.
- **Second Anchor** — a second simultaneous exit sigil. **The obvious first upgrade**, and it lands hardest right after the party has finally learned to hunt them.
- **Bound Sentinel** — binds one specific monster as a guard instead of a wave.
- **Floor Sculpting** — rearrange rooms on cleared floors.
- **Outflow** — extend dungeon effects into specific places in town.

## The Surfeit Counter

> [!danger] DM-only — the hidden cost of saving the PCs
> Every ember-bearer hollowing ticks a counter (**Surfeit**). [[The Entity|The entity]] pays the dungeon from its own substance to spare the PCs, and that payment accrues. **At thresholds the dungeon becomes incrementally stronger** — Wesley unlocks new sigil-class abilities, the [[The Reality Filter|Filter]] strengthens, the entity's interventions strain.
>
> **Running total:** Ep1 hollowed [[Tank]] and [[Old Man]]. Ep3's Siren fight is likely to add more. **Second Anchor is a natural unlock off that tick.**
