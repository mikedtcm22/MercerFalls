# The Reality Filter

Up: [[Lore & Cosmology]]

**Normies in Mercer Falls perceive the wrongness — they just can't hold onto it or muster the will to follow up. The Filter is not a perceptual veil. It is two things: a rationalization that arrives whole and satisfies, and a memory that drains like a dream.**

## The two halves

**They are separate effects with separate triggers**, and the distinction matters for running them.

| | **Rationalization** | **The decay clock** |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Witnessing anything impossible | Exposure to the dungeon, or to what it causes |
| Who | **Everyone.** No dungeon contact needed | Anyone who was below, or saw its effects |
| Speed | **Instant** | ~30 minutes for an adult |
| Feels like | Having an explanation | Nothing at all |

### 1. Rationalization — the half the party lives inside

People *see* it. Then an explanation **arrives in them whole**, satisfies them completely, and they go about their day. A Fireball in the town square is a gas main, and nobody calls the gas company.

> [!note] The diagnostic — and the party can run it
> **It is never the same answer twice.** Two witnesses to the same event will give two confident, incompatible explanations, standing within earshot of one another, each with perfect conviction.
>
> Mercer had the same object explained to him four ways in one afternoon. See **[[The Debrief Station|the Yard Trials]]**.

**Interrogation doesn't crack it, it just dissolves.** Press a witness and the answers get thinner and stranger until they lose the thread entirely and change the subject. *"What was under the tarpaulin?" "A tarpaulin"* — and they aren't troubled at having said it. **They are not lying.**

**At high strain it costs them.** Something bad enough produces vague illness, a day off work, and no memory of having been ill.

**The two tactical consequences, both of which the party can act on:**
- **They can cast in public and never be caught.**
- **They can never be corroborated.** No adult ally will ever confirm what they saw. **[[Wesley Crane|Wesley]]'s entire pitch runs on isolation, and the isolation is structural.**

> [!note] What is *not* affected
> **A Filtered person's written record of ordinary things is reliable.** They write down what they sincerely believe happened — so the Ledger's silences are **follow-through failures, not corrupted text.** A reporter who never connects a disappearance to an "animal attack" seven days later simply never thought to.
>
> This also means an interviewer at [[The Debrief Station]] *can* accurately transcribe a delver's report, which is the whole reason that station worked.

### 2. The decay clock

> [!danger] Established canon — see [[The Debrief Station]]
> **Memory of the dungeon, and of the fantastical things it causes in this town, fades the way a dream fades on waking.** Fast, on a clock, and nobody notices it happening.
>
> **Not suppression, not a lie.** The person genuinely had it. They reach, find a shape where it ought to be, and stop reaching — untroubled, because nothing feels missing. An hour later they'll say they had a fever, or slept badly, and **they will mean it.**

| Who | Roughly how long |
|---|---|
| **Ordinary adult** | **About half an hour.** Mercer's margin, measured on his own men |
| **Children** | **A day or more.** One of Mercer's sweeper boys still had the whole of it a week on |
| **Ember-bearers** | **It does not fade. At all.** Inside or out |

**The countermeasure is a dream journal.** Write it fast — your own experience, or dictated from someone just up from below — and you keep most of it.

> [!danger] Written thoughts do not leave the dungeon
> A magical rule; it wants no explanation at the table.
>
> **Marks stay** — tallies, route glyphs, chalk, a hammer-hole, *TANK WUZ HEAR*. **Accounts don't** — anything constituting a person's *report* of the place. Paper comes up empty.
>
> So the operation below could keep ledgers, maps and marks that worked perfectly **in situ**, and export none of it. The only channel out is a human head. See [[The Recall]].

> [!note] Why both halves matter for running the game
> The Filter is the hardest thing in the campaign to convey, because **players naturally read it as adults lying or stonewalling.** [[Wendell Pace|Wendell]] grasping and giving up looks evasive. [[Tomek Sołtys|Tomek]]'s *"I probably had a fever"* looks like a cover story.
>
> **[[The Debrief Station]] ends that reading permanently** — a hundred years of men *trying* to pass it on and failing, and a hundred years of men sincerely calling a two-rowed jaw a hog's.
>
> **The age gradient also explains [[Ren Sołtys|Ren]].** She held Tomek's hollowing after every adult let it go because she was eleven — and she would have lost it too, eventually, if she hadn't started writing it down.

> [!danger] DM-only — what the Filter *is*
> The dungeon's effect on the town, generalized. A century of agency-eating (see [[Hollowing and Agency-Loss]], [[Order and Chaos]]) has left Mercer Falls with a citywide deficit of follow-through and a population that cannot retain the evidence. The stuckness is mechanical — the containment system working as designed.

## Spell Rationalization

| Spell | Observer perceives |
|---|---|
| Burning Hands | Hairspray + lighter / gas-stove flare |
| Sleep | Long day, dozed off |
| Healing Word | Headache just went, weird |
| Mage Hand | The wind, probably |
| Charm Person | I had been thinking about that anyway |
| Fireball | Gas main blew (no follow-up call) |

**Two witnesses should give two different answers.** Make a habit of it — it's the tell.

## Item Rationalization (Visibility Gradient)

| Tier | Item | Rationalization |
|---|---|---|
| Invisible | Pocket knife, walking stick, hunting bow | None — normal |
| Easy | Hunting rifle, machete, hatchet, hockey gear | None, in rural Ohio |
| Moderate | Sword, mace, crossbow, leather armor, biker jacket | "Replica," "costume," "movie prop" |
| Strained | Greataxe, halberd, chain shirt, breastplate | "LARPing," "Renaissance Faire" — holds, but witnesses feel off |
| Maximal | Full plate, exotic dungeon weapons, anything visibly inhuman | Filter working hard; **vague unease, possible mild illness afterward**, can't name what they saw |

A tone-and-pacing dial. Smart play is the gear stash (see [[Gear and Items]]).

## Who Is / Isn't Subject

| Who | Filter applies? |
|---|---|
| Ordinary adults | Yes — full Filter, ~30-minute retention |
| Young children | Yes, but weakly — **and they hold the memory far longer** |
| Authority figures | Yes |
| The PCs' parents | Yes, but more strained — stronger follow-through pressure, weaker results |
| **Other PCs** | **NO** — weapons are weapons, and **nothing fades** |
| **[[Wesley Crane]]** | **NO** — ember-equivalent bearer |
| The rival party (post-equinox) | NO |
| Hollowed adherents | NO — broken in both directions |
| Filter-broken NPCs (mid-game) | NO |

**Parent edge case:** Filtered, but with stronger social follow-through toward their own kid. Flagrant gear-flaunting at home produces *"is everything okay at school?"* — permeable enough for concern, not for action.

### Filter-porous NPCs — belief is what makes it fail

| Who | Why |
|---|---|
| [[Ren Sołtys]] | Perceives and refuses to let go. **Four years of a notebook** is a deliberate countermeasure to the decay clock, invented at eleven |
| [[Russ]] | Grief. Half-aware his sister's death didn't add up |
| [[Dominique Ferraro]] | **[[Bash Whitlock\|Bash]] told her the whole story, repeatedly, then showed her.** She sat through an entire hollowing and it didn't slide off |

## Why the PCs Are Immune

[[The Entity]] has touched them — the [[The Embers|embers]] reinforce the bearer's capacity to act, **and stop the memory decay outright.**

> [!note] The proof the party can run on themselves
> They have been taking notes and arguing about the reset for weeks, **and none of it has faded.** Nobody has managed that since 1889. **Deliver it as a realization, not a fact** — have somebody check their own pockets.

## Filter-Breaking

Mid-game (Levels 4–10), PCs may learn to **break the Filter for individual NPCs** — a major story-power recruiting allies who can act.

**The two halves come apart.** Ending the rationalization and stopping the decay are not obviously the same operation. **A partial break — an ally who can *act* but still can't *retain*** — is a legitimately horrible outcome worth keeping in reserve.

> [!danger] DM-only — a cosmology breadcrumb
> Filter-breaking is a late clue about [[The Entity]]'s nature: hollowed mill workers, once broken, may say things that *don't fit* the "benevolent patron" frame. [[Russ]] is the strong candidate for the first significant break.

## The 1991 Historical Implication

[[The 1991 Mill Incident]] was a **mass hollowing, not a mass death**. Workers stumbled in, all hollowed, all reconstituted at the entrance and walked out past coworkers who saw it and don't remember. The "seven died" are the ones whose hollowed selves walked into machinery or off heights; the rest had safer jobs and are still in town (see [[Henry Voss]]).
