# The Siren

Up: [[Bestiary]]

**Bound to [[Wesley's Sigils|Wesley's sigil]] in [[The Spiral Stair Room]] since he drew it, about a week before [[Episode 3 — Second Shift]] opens. She has no face of her own. She wears the one that listener would come home for, and what she offers is permission to stop.**

> [!note] Stat block
> **DM holds the finished block separately** (locked 2026-08-29). The design constraints below are what the numbers have to preserve — check any block against them before running.

## The register — the whole monster in one rule

> **An invitation you don't have to earn.**
>
> No urgency, no debt, no deadline, nobody disappointed in you. The feeling is being called in off the street at dusk when you were a kid: the day is over, you're allowed to stop, and somebody is glad it's you.

That's why nine adults walk unhurried in the dark and none of them talk. **They aren't rushing toward anything. They're being welcomed.**

**Avoid entirely:** lateness, being expected, having failed to arrive, someone waiting on them, panic. *That register is reserved for [[Episode 4 — The Crossing]] and [[Lowell Brandt]] — do not bleed them together.*

**Vocabulary that works:** come in · come here · it's alright · you can stop · nothing you have to fix first · **"I'm coming."**

**She is the dungeon's most honest monster.** She doesn't steal agency — she asks for it, and people say yes. That's the machine's whole thesis wearing a face.

> [!danger] Reskin note — hold this
> Run as a **Siren**, never as a succubus. The chassis is borrowed; the framing is not. Nothing about her should read as seduction — the party are 15–17 and the horror is *domestic*. She sounds like a mother on a back porch, and that is far worse.

## Reach — two independent conditions

**The notch decides *whether*. Distance decides *when*.**

She isn't reaching down a wire into town. She is **calling, and the only ears that can hear her are ears that have been bitten** ([[Nullprints|the notch]]). Everyone else sleeps through it, at any range.

**Her reach is a radius from the mill, and it grows every night.** Full table in [[Episode 3 — Second Shift]]; by night 7–8 it has reached [[Tank]]'s and [[Old Man]]'s street, which is why two ember-bearers who have been notched all week only started walking now.

**Telepathic Bond gives reach; the notch gives purchase.** Once bonded she can whisper at any range. No notch, it's an unpleasant voice you shake off.

## Non-negotiable modifications

Whatever block you run, these must hold:

| | Why |
|---|---|
| **All damage resistances stripped except poison** | RAW the chassis resists nonmagical B/P/S plus cold/fire/lightning/poison — i.e. essentially everything this party can do |
| **No Etherealness** | She is bound to the sigil and cannot leave the shaft. **The only reason this is solvable** |
| **Tethered ~40 ft from the sigil** | Caps her retreat ladder so a party without ranged options can still finish her; and **fleeing works** — anyone who breaks contact is clear once out of the shaft mouth |
| **Shapechanger, narrated per-PC** | At initiative she appears **differently to each player.** Pass notes if you can |
| **Two kill routes, both shown** | The kiss, and **the drop** — she grapples a charmed creature *automatically, because it goes willingly*, flies up, and lets go. Better still, hands off to a harpy, which has no tether |

**The drop is the one they'll remember**, because the horror isn't being grabbed. It's that they went willingly.

## The countermeasure — occupancy, not blocking

**[[Ruth Bielak]] is the answer**, and she doesn't know it. Her notch is already full — seven years of a stretched cassette belt playing a dead husband's voice at the wrong speed, every night, in exactly this frequency. Nothing else can get in.

**Content is irrelevant. Only speed matters, because speed is the frequency.** [[Bash Whitlock|Bash]]'s deduction, and he should get to make it out loud. Converts into the **Talkboy** — 3 charges, 30-ft immunity bubble, see [[Episode 3 — Second Shift]].

> [!note] The wax ruling — outstanding
> The party landed on **wax in the ears** at the end of [[Session 5 — Second Shift]]. Recommended: let it fail informatively (muffles the sound; the pull isn't acoustic where it matters), so that Gern's *other* idea — ringing the [[The Bell Junction|Bell Junction]] bell to drown her out — makes Ruth's discovery a refinement of something they already guessed.

## Retinue

**2 harpies**, ~25 HP each, fly 40, Luring Song. **Not bound to the sigil** — they're her retinue, not her toll, so **killing her clears the sigil** and the survivors scatter. They own the upper landings and **can carry a charmed creature above her tether.** One at initiative; the second arrives end of round 2, so pressure rises instead of front-loading.

**House ruling, both songs:** they **override** the RAW "won't move into obviously damaging terrain" safeguard. A charmed creature doesn't perceive the drop — it perceives *the way home.* **This is the entire reason this monster is in this room.**

## Clearing her

*The green goes out. That's all. No sound. The chalk on the floor is just chalk now.*

**This costs [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]] significant delve progress** — he has to redraw elsewhere. The party will never know.

**No [[The Recall|recall]] on screen.** The fight is inside, so the bodies persist through the rest of the delve and go with the reset. The party does not witness a recall this episode.

## Open dials

- **What [[Russ]] calls her**, badly, off a VHS box, when they finally describe her to him. The party will adopt it permanently — pick well.
- Whether the Act-4 fight needs a third harpy given the Talkboy plus a party who now knows the room.
- Whether she leaves a corpse at all *(recommended: yes, and it looks like nobody — every player describes her differently)*.
