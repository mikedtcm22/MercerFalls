# Session 8 — Second Shift

Up: [[Episode 3 — Second Shift]]

**Played 2026-09-30, *The Siren's Song Falls Silent*. The episode closer. The party went straight back down at 3 a.m., killed [[The Siren]] with the [[The Modded Talkboy|Talkboy]] running every round, cleared [[Wesley's Sigils|Wesley's sigil]], found [[The Stone of Quietude]], climbed out through the top hatch into [[The Debrief Station|Mercer's cabin]] — and in between, heard a scream they chose not to chase. [[Ren Sołtys|Ren]] and [[Tomek Sołtys|Tomek]] were hollowed while the party was in the Pit Room. [[Old Man]] was hollowed there too. Episode 3 complete; party is level 3.**

> [!note] Numbering and date
> Vault order: S5 (08-28) → S6 (09-09) → S7 (09-25) → **S8 (09-30)**. The transcript file is dated 2026-10-01; the session was run the evening before.

## Cold open — the lawns again

All four PCs **sleepwalked to their front lawns** and woke there, ~2–3 a.m. — so nobody "snuck out"; their beds have been empty since the walk. They regrouped and went to the mill fence to field-test the Talkboy.

- **Among the walkers:** [[Wendell Pace|Wendell]] and [[Ruth Bielak|Ruth]] (her protection gone since [[Session 7 — Second Shift|Session 7]]), and **[[Tomek Sołtys|Tomek]]** — confirmed at the gate when Old Man asked.
- **Old Man and Tomek.** In the six-second window the Talkboy buys, Old Man took Tomek by the shoulders and said one word: ***"Remember."*** Something registered; then he went back to pacing the gate. No roll.
- **The walkers are getting smarter.** No one is inside, but they've started testing the fence in fits and starts. Tank: *do it now, before they get in.*
- **The Talkboy at the gate** wakes walkers briefly, then they relapse — the range test from Session 7, repeated.
- Party entered through a **bush-covered gap in the fence** (DC 10 Stealth, everyone passed — no walker saw the route).

**Bash swapped a replicated item.** Ruling: the Artificer's replicated-item cap is the Magic Items column; **making a new one destroys the oldest.** The **Bag of Holding became a Rope of Climbing**; contents were handed out first (Tank got the [[The Expedition Post|Room-11 key]]; Old Man got his record back, Mended). Tank keeps his +1 warhammer.

## The delve

**Route:** Entrance → [[The Cataloguing Office]] → [[The Octagonal Vestibule]] → [[The Bell Junction]] → [[The Spiral Stair Room]]. The party now has a map and picked its door directly.

**[[The Recall|Persistence, confirmed again]]:** *TANK WUZ HEAR* (backwards R) is still under the desk. Bash's read: *"we seem to be going forward in time in here instead of repeating."* Their Groundhog-Day theory is retired.

**The Cataloguing Office.**
- The ledgers record **objects, not names** — *"odd skull,"* a date, and a number that seems to mean where it was found.
- A **fountain pen with ink still dripping**, as if just set down. Bash: *"somebody's always just been in here writing something."*
- **⭐ Old Man left a note: *"To the Archivist — call me,"* with his home phone number.** The party now refers to the unseen writer as **the Archivist.**

> [!danger] DM ruling — the note stays
> Written material left in the Cataloguing Office **persists.** If the office wiped itself, nothing in those ledgers would survive either. See [[The Cataloguing Office]].
>
> **The Archivist** is now a live possibility as a real entity — see that note's DM section. Nothing decided.

**Gern's prep:** Goodberry, flavoured as **Capri Suns and Fruit Roll-Ups**, five each to Tank and Old Man.

**New rule — hurrying.** See [[Dungeon Mechanics]]. Used Office → Bell Junction.

**Wandering: three wolves** caught the party from behind at the Bell Junction. **Bash's caltrops** at the entry hallway — all three failed the DC 15 Dex save — **earned a surprise round.** [[Gern]] took the **Talkboy-holder job** voluntarily (Healing Word is a bonus action; he's tanky; "if she targets the holder, fine"). Wolves killed without loss; Tank took 3. The song was audible from here, damped by the Talkboy.

## The Siren — killed

**She was alone.** The harpies stayed dead, as ruled in Session 7. Same room, same green glow from the sigil.

**Gern pressed play at the top of every round** and kept the bubble on the party. The fight, briefly:
- **Tank** raged, closed, hit for 10 and **pushed her into the stairs** (+1d4 bludgeoning, ruled). Later **pushed her off a perch** — she has a fly speed but no hover, so she **fell 20 ft (2d6)** onto the rafters below.
- **Old Man**: **Dissonant Whispers** drove her up into the rafters, ~60 ft; Vicious Mockery twice; thrown daggers. *"You have one chance to give yourself up."* She spat blood at him.
- **Bash**: Poison Spray (repeatedly missing), then a **crossbow bolt** that left her on her last legs.
- **Gern** walked up beside her and **held the Talkboy to her ear** (ruled: roll it like Toll the Dead, Wis 14 — she saved, no damage).
- She fled upward each time she was hurt; Tank took attacks of opportunity. **On the last song Tank was ~40 ft from Gern, outside the bubble — DC 17, failed, charmed.** She had used her action to charm him and could do nothing else.
- **Gern dropped the Talkboy and cast Toll the Dead** — a bell bearing the **Certaintist emblem** formed beside her head — *"By the power of certainty!"* She failed. Necrosis took her apart; **she dissolved completely. No corpse.**

**The sigil went out.** The green glow died with her; the figure on the floor is now **plain white chalk** — *"like graffiti, or a hopscotch."*

> [!note] DM post-mortem, said at the table
> She was built so that **every damaging option requires a charmed target.** With the Talkboy running she had nothing but flight. That's the design working — and a lesson for any future charm-gated monster.

## Spoils in the stair room — [[The Stone of Quietude]]

Gern matched the room to **his photograph** (the Marv's Spot snapshot: a man putting *"a quiet rock"* back where he found it, *"more trouble than it was worth"*). **Loose brick, secret compartment, a round stone with markings and a button-like dimple.** He pressed it immediately. Ace Ventura ensued. **It casts *Silence* centered on the stone; Gern burned all its charges playing with it.** It recharges.

## The hatch and the cabin — [[The Debrief Station]]

At the top of the stair: a locked manhole-style hatch, **skeleton-key keyhole** (circle over a bell shape). **The Room-11 key opened it** — Tank had it from Bash's bag.

**Old Man stayed below to keep watch** and hold the hatch open (*"we've been killing things and dying sort of routinely"*). Tank and Gern went up; Bash joined late.

- **One-way.** The cabin side of the hatch is smooth and flush — no purchase. If it closes, it stays closed.
- **The room:** a single-room cabin in the woods, daylight starting in the windows. **One plush lounge chair in the middle of the room, facing the hatch**, with a small round end table beside it. A **writing desk** with scattered papers to one side; a **curio cabinet** of oddities to the other — **a strange-shaped skull, a lantern, gadgets.** Decades of dust. A door out to the woods.
- **Curio cabinet (Gern, Investigation 18 with Tank helping):** **[[The Reading Glass]]** — the two-handled lens. **Gern has it.** The DM sent Kenny the item text privately as player knowledge; **in character Gern says he doesn't know what it does yet.**
- **Desk (Tank, as Old Man's proxy, Investigation 1):** one paper only — **Mercer's III, *On the Inattention of Witnesses*.** Read aloud in full; PDF to follow. **Tank is carrying it.** Papers I, II, IV and the log are still on the desk.
- **End table (Bash):** the **laminated card** with the six rules, read aloud and posted to chat.

Party read: *"So Mercer was a reluctant caretaker of some sort of cursed thing."* Old Man: *"I guess we're going to hit the Historical Society sometime."*

## The scream — and the choice

Back at the foot of the stair, the party heard **a young person's shriek** from the direction they'd come in — *a horror-movie scream.* They peeked at the Bell Junction, saw nothing, and **chose the unexplored north passage instead** (*"I'd rather go where we haven't been"*).

## The Pit Room, first pass

[[The Pit Room]] as played: a ~8 ft pit of **sleeping giant hyenas** (two), rope ladders on two sides, a narrow lip on the left, the broad right side ending at the **Dogman Chief** asleep on a bearskin rug with circlet, pelts and a string of teeth. Stairs down at the far end. **The hyenas' ramp is a long board, currently pulled up, beside the Chief.**

**Old Man cast Disguise Self as a twin of the Chief** and tried a Pied Piper play on the pit. Stealth 12 (with Guidance) got him to the board and woke the Chief. Gern signalled the others back into the corridor (Survival 18) so only "a dogman" would be seen.

The Chief, half asleep: *"What are you doing here? Get back to your post."* Old Man improvised **kobold stew in the mess.** The Chief told him to fetch some, with grog — *"Get out of here, grunt."*

## The notebook

Going back toward the scream, in the **circular room (the Octagonal Vestibule)**: **two fresh, dark nullprints**, one larger, one smaller and slender. **Next to the smaller one: a marble composition book** — *PROPERTY OF REN SOŁTYS — DO NOT READ!!!*

> [!danger] They read it
> **Tank argued against** (*"It says do not read. Why would we read it?"*). **Bash pushed** (*"more of a suggestion"*). **Gern opened it** once the others leaned in. Read aloud below: the **WHAT I THINK** theory list, the **first entry** (Tomek in October 1992; Mama remembering it as a third-grade flu) and the **latest** (Wendell at Marco's, fourteen on the list, *"I didn't sleep last night and that's the other thing."*). Gern then used it investigatively — crossings-out, any sign she'd been in before, who she might have been with.
>
> **Who is holding it now: not confirmed — open.**

GM, out of character: **ember-bearer prints are faint; these two are much darker.** Gern: *"We failed to save the screaming woman."* Plan: find her tomorrow and see whether she can say who she was with.

Old Man, aside: *"Maybe we have the dogmen wrong. Maybe we're the real villains."*

## The Pit Room, second pass — the stew

**Recipe:** a dog bowl conjured by Bash's **Tinker's Magic** (Performance 13: not fancy, but a dog bowl), a Capri Sun, Dunkaroo frosting, and **a great deal of Poison Spray** condensed into it.

Old Man delivered it. **Deception 16 vs. the Chief's Wis 2** — he ate half, gagged, **took 11 poison.** He failed to see through the disguise (Int 11 vs. DC 13) but **knew he'd been poisoned by one of his own** — *"This is a mutiny and I won't stand for it."*

**The fight.** The Chief lowered the board for the hyenas and stepped back to shoot.
- Old Man's **Tasha's Hideous Laughter** failed.
- **Hyena bites hit hard** (11, 13, 13). The Chief used **Incite Rampage**, and **a hyena's reaction bite hollowed Old Man** — a faint nullprint and rising motes, *"warmth in your chest, everything going cold."*
- **Tank dropped to 0 and stayed at 1 on Relentless Endurance**; two Adrenaline Rush dashes.
- **Bash** ran, dropping **grease** and then **Tinker's Magic ball bearings**; both hyenas went prone in the grease.
- **Gern**: Healing Word on Tank (+11), **Infestation** on the Chief (5 poison, shoved a square toward him), then dashed.
- **The three survivors retreated up the spiral stair and out the hatch.** It dropped shut behind the third. The hyenas didn't take the stairs.

## Dawn

~**5 a.m.**, sun coming up. They walked out the cabin door, picked up trails through the woods, and came out by the mill. **Looking back, the cabin reads as an abandoned shack.**

**At the gate the walkers were waking up confused** — and each made up a reason to be going home: *"I've got to get coffee ready for my husband," "the kids are going to wake up soon."* Different reason every time.

**Among them, walking home with the glazed look: Ren, and Tomek.** End of Episode 3.
