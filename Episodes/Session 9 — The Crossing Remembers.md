# Session 9 — The Crossing Remembers

Played 2026-10-15. In-world Thursday 11 – Friday 12 July 1996. Part of [[Episode 4 — The Crossing]]. Next in-world beat: the vigil, **Saturday 13 July**. Ren wakes ~Saturday 20 July unless rescued.

This note has four layers. **Table canon** happened at the table. **Decided** was settled by the DM in the post-session prep chat (2026-10-15 to 10-17). **Proposed** is a recommendation awaiting a pick — not canon. **Open** is undecided.

## Headline

The party solved *who the ghost is* in one afternoon, using [[The Reading Glass]] four ways plus the library. They know: Lowell Brandt, killed at the same crossing in July 1995, Grace Tabernacle, a bedridden son, three entries in Ren's notebook, and that Ray Kubiak re-enacted Lowell's death. They know nothing yet about the *how*: white prints, release, the shoebox or the vial.

## Decided in prep

### The Reading Glass
- **Once per person per long rest** (four charges a day for the party). Endorsed.
- **Passing it round to shop for a cheaper price is the intended use.** Prices are fixed per holder per question, not per question.
- **Say-it-then-pay** is allowed: only the payer loses the fact.
- Pricing therefore targets what can't be shared — promises, kindnesses, private feelings — and uses the falter.

### Nullprints — see [[Nullprints]] for the full rules
- **The threshold rule.** A hollowed person who sets foot across the archway into [[The Cataloguing Office]] is removed and reconstitutes at their live print, themselves — a corpse run. **"Set foot" is literal: carried counts.**
- **Falling again** leaves a deeper print and a longer next hollowing. Anyone who fell deep is in a perpetual agency trap. **[[Henry Voss]] keeps cycling.**
- **Two rituals, two objects.** White print (dead): burn proof of a finished errand → release. Black print (alive): burn something representing what the person is avoiding or running from → **Filter-break**. The same object never works on both. **The wrong object won't catch.** Nothing about burning is lethal.
- The black-print object (DM shorthand: the **unfaced thing**) stays hidden from players for a long time. Accidental Filter-breaks are allowed and left unexplained.
- **Search and rescue.** If the party carries hollowed Ren (or Tomek) across the archway, she reconstitutes alone at her Vestibule print and wanders. The party must find her before something takes her down again.

### Bash's photocopies — three pages
- **A — a plate from Elias's notes.** Two figures on stone; the dark one "drawn down by degrees as the man returns to himself"; the pale one fixed — "The pale do not diminish. I have kept one under observation eleven years." The colour tell, and Gern's fourth strike.
- **B — a family letter on the funeral custom.** It fails as often as it holds: where it held, the thing burned was a thing he had *finished* ("Kowalczyk's last ledger, closed and balanced"); where it failed, a keepsake.
- **C — Constance Mercer to her brother, 1891** (draft text; first seed of Mercer as a past *user*). "Father goes down every Sunday after service, as other men go to their clubs. He says the works below will not keep without a hand on them… He comes up brighter each time, and kinder to no one." Pencil five-bar tallies in the margin. *Dial:* the same hand as the tallies underground, or only the same habit. *Watch:* Wesley may have seen this page go through the copier.

- **Gern** falters if he tries to compare the sigil; and if Dominique raises the Ruth visit.
