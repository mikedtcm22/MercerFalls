# Session 5 — Second Shift

Up: [[Episode 3 — Second Shift]]

**Played 2026-08-28. Episode 3, Acts 1–2. Cold open through the first delve — and they never reached the Siren.** The party rang the [[The Bell Junction|Bell Junction]] bell, found the portal, and took it *away* from the singing. Session ended on a wandering-monster wolf fight in the [[The Buzzsaw Room|Buzzsaw]] spike corridor.

> [!danger] Where Session 6 opens
> **Mid-delve, in Room 14, no long rest.** They have to find their way back to the Bell Junction archway. Their only known route is the long way — decide before play whether the [[The Octagonal Vestibule|Vestibule]]'s sealed spokes have re-opened.

## What actually happened

**Cold open — [[Tomek Sołtys]].** Ran as written. The voice was [[Ren Sołtys|Ren]], calling him to come look at something; delighted; *"it has been a long while since anybody was delighted about him."* Wrong shoe, wrong foot, past his sister twice without turning his head, fingers through the wire, crying without knowing why. Ren writing down the time.

**The lawns.** Tank and Old Man woke nine houses out. Both passed the first DC 13 Wis save at the gate. They let the walkers take point and followed.

**The gate, not the fence.** Run as the **front gate with the chain and master lock** — the same spot the whole campaign started. Nine walkers standing at it, fingers through the wire, nobody climbing. *(Supersedes the "fence dip" staging in the episode note.)*

**[[Ren Sołtys|Ren]].** Neither PC recognised her (History 9 and 10). She opened with *"You're not asleep."* What she gave:

- Her brother's in the group. He went in **on a dare at seventeen.** Came back and *"it wasn't the same person. He would eat if you put food in front of him. Other than that, he wasn't there."*
- **Two weeks** later he was fine. *(Note: earlier design said eleven days. **Two weeks is now canon.**)*
- Nobody remembers it happened. *"Hell, my mom doesn't even remember that happened."*
- **"I started writing this stuff down because everybody else forgot. And I don't want to forget."** Repeated near-verbatim as a mantra, clutching the notebook.
- It's escalating: *"There weren't this many."*
- **"You would have made eleven."**
- Tomek is completely normal in daylight. This only happens at night.
- Asked about his experience: *"He says he probably had a fever."*
- She warned them off: **"I wouldn't go in there. Not after what happened to Tomek."**

**Second Wis save: Old Man failed.** Walked to the fence mid-sentence and came to with his fingers through the wire. Tank shook him out of it. His voice was **his old guitar teacher, who moved away.** Tank passed.

> [!danger] Old Man told her almost everything
> The dungeon, "shadow dogs," "time archivists," symbolic traps — and **"I definitely died at least once, and now I'm not."** She was openly sceptical (*"that's a bit of a stretch"*), but she writes things down.
>
> **Her notebook now contains the party's own testimony that the mill is survivable.** This materially changes her hollowing setup — she no longer just has observational evidence that four kids came back in a day. She has them saying so. **Old Man handed her the permission.**

**The night tour.** They woke Bash by hammering on his Bilco doors (he came out the front with a crossbow), then went up to the Heights and pebbled Gern's window. Gern refused to sneak out and agreed to meet in the morning.

**Morning.** Tank worked out — **failed the DC 13 Athletics** (tired from sleepwalking), so **no advantage on Wisdom saves.** Then the [[Ricky Vance|Ricky]] beat landed: light weight, couldn't finish his first set, *"maybe I caught something out in the woods,"* **"my arms just quit."** Tank suggested a rest day and **Ricky visibly winced**, then went to do treadmill work.

**No Day-1 Time Points spent.** Straight from the gym to the mill. Wendell never observed, no library, no Panels.

**Delve in broad daylight**, after you clarified the dungeon isn't night-gated — which surprised all four players.

## The delve

Route: Entrance → [[The Cataloguing Office]] → [[The Octagonal Vestibule]] → [[The Bell Junction]] → **portal** → [[The Buzzsaw Room]] → spike corridor.

- **Cataloguing Office:** identical. Coffee still steaming, ink still wet on the pen. Bash rolled **26** investigation — *"as if you walked into the next room and came back."* Bash out loud: **"we might be looping through the exact same situation we were in before — that probably includes all the bad guys."**
- **The Vestibule had eight doors**, not the single door and smooth walls of the first visit. Dogman corpses gone. Investigation 26 mapped it: drafts at 12/10/7, snoring at 9, arrow traps at 2 and 4:30 (identical circular indentations — a repeatable tell), loot at 6.
- **Two dogmen alive and asleep** in the 9 o'clock closet. Old Man stealthed the door open and shut again without waking them.
- **6 o'clock strongbox:** $25 petty cash, a box of caltrops, and a **flask of unidentified shimmering liquid** (Arcana 16 — not ordinary, something unique about it).
- Tank turned the 2 o'clock knob twice, dodged two arrows (Dex 13, 15), and worked out it's a **fake door** (Perception 15).
- Opening the 7 o'clock door **dropped stone over every other spoke.** They noted, too late, that they should have marked the dogman door.
- **Bell Junction:** bell 15 ft up, out of melee reach. Bash's tinkered scrap bell did nothing. **Mage Hand on the striker** produced a low drone → a handle appeared on the bell-marked door → **portal.** The drone also triggered a Wis save and made **the Siren's song audible through the archway.** All passed; both notched PCs felt the pull toward the archway.
- **They took the portal instead.** Bash first, Old Man after him, Tank shoved Gern through, then followed. Caltrops left on the Bell Junction floor.
- **Buzzsaw Room:** the journal they looted is **still gone.** Crate creature lowered and confirmed identical. Footlocker (unopened last time): **ornate silver hand mirror, pair of signal whistles, box of ball bearings.**
- Spike corridor: **re-armed.** Gern pulled the torch to drop them.
- **Wandering monster:** dire wolf + 2 wolves. Old Man, disguised as a dogman, tried to befriend them, **rolled a natural 1**, and took 10 damage. Won on Bane + Tank's new hammer + Sanctuary + Bash re-arming the spikes as a held action + poison spray finishing the dire wolf.

## The reset experiments — the party's own methodology

Deliberate, and it deserves a canon answer. Rules now written up in **[[The Recall]]**.

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Set dressing | Identical |
| Dogman corpses | Gone — and the dogmen are back, asleep |
| Journal they looted | **Still gone** |
| Solved spike trap | **Re-armed** |
| Crate creature | Same |
| Brightness (daytime) | Unchanged |
| **Tank punched a hole in the office wall** | **Pending** |
| **Bash inked *TANK WUZ HEAR*** (backwards N, "HEAR") under the desk | **Pending** |

**Ruling:** the dungeon restores its own inventory, doesn't touch yours, and doesn't repair what you break. **Both markers persist.** Which lets the party derive, unaided, that Wesley's chalk is a *person's* marks surviving multiple resets — and starts an accumulating scar trail he will eventually walk past.

## New canon established at the table

- **Beth Whitlock** — Bash's mother. Works nights, home early morning, frazzled and kind. Honey Nut Cheerios and Frosted Flakes.
- Bash lives in the basement; the Bilco doors are unlocked. Gern lives in the Heights, parents home, one creaky step.
- **Gern's kit:** shoulder pads, knee pads, his dad's old rugby helmet, and the **family Certaintist crest turned into a shield**, thumbs-up-with-a-ring painted on badly.
- **Goodberries are flavoured as 90s snacks** — Dunkaroos, Ecto Coolers, shark fruit snacks. Gern hands them out.
- Gern has **Resourceful** (heroic inspiration on long rest).
- **Bash crafted a +1 Warhammer engraved TANK** and gave it to him, plus the satchel ("purse") — a running gag now. His Tinker cot dissipates at end of long rest.
- **Old Man is fey — advantage on saves vs. spells and magical effects.** Declared at table. *Note: does **not** stack with the ember's charm advantage, but does cover Reproach and other magical effects.*
- Signal whistles are **human-frequency**, confirmed by Tank testing them on dogman-Old Man.
- **Dungeoneering procedure in use:** one action per room; wandering-monster rolls; 3 TP flat per delve regardless of duration.

## Threads to pay off

- **The silver hand mirror.** [[Episode — The Mirror]] is banked with the Mercer Mirror at [[Panels]] as its anchor, and they just pulled an ornate silver hand mirror out of a dungeon footlocker without blinking. Free connection.
- **The unidentified flask.** Undeclared.
- **Old Man is still a dogman**, said so for the record, and wants a **clipboard** to pose as "the inspector." He's tried the disguise twice. A dogman with a clipboard is good enough to work once.
- **The surveillance project.** Old Man wants multi-night watch on the gate; Gern floated Credo as sentry; **Bash asked about building an alarm and you said it could be built over time with TP.** Live crafting project, no cost assigned.
- **Credo as standard scout** — and Gern's own suggestion that something could **intercept or falsify the familiar link.** You said you'd work on it. Bank it; devastating if it fires once, late.
- **They landed on wax in the ears.** Explicitly, at session end. The [[Ruth Bielak|Ruth]]/Talkboy design was built so the Odysseus solution *wouldn't* work — **needs a ruling.** Recommended: let it fail informatively (muffles the sound; the pull isn't acoustic where it matters), so that Gern's *other* idea — ringing the bell continuously to drown her out — makes Ruth's discovery a **refinement of something they already guessed** rather than a handout.
- **Ricky winced at "rest day."** He is not going to rest.
- Two sleeping dogmen behind a spoke that's now sealed.
