# Session 6 — Second Shift

Up: [[Episode 3 — Second Shift]]

**Played 2026-09-09. Almost entirely dungeon. The party worked a loop through Floor 1, recovered both the [[The Map-Stone|map-stone]] and the [[The Compass-Stone|compass-stone]], confirmed [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]]'s old sigil is dead, and came back around to the [[The Bell Junction|Bell Junction]] — ending the night in almost exactly the position Session 5 ended in, with the Siren's song through the archway and fewer spell slots.**

> [!note] No transcript
> Reconstructed from DM notes. Lighter on dialogue than [[Session 5 — Second Shift]]; treat quotes as absent rather than omitted.

> [!danger] Correction to the Session 5 log
> The Bell Junction portal dumped them into **[[The Expedition Post]]**, not the Buzzsaw Room. The wolf fight in the spike corridor followed from there, and **the footlocker loot** (ornate silver hand mirror, signal whistles, ball bearings) belongs to the Expedition Post. `Session 5 — Second Shift` still says Buzzsaw and needs correcting.

## The route

**[[The Expedition Post]]** *(start, from the portal)* → spike corridor → **[[The Pillared Reach]]** → **[[The Buzzsaw Room|The Millrace]]** → **[[The Stutter Stairs]]** → switchback room → checkerboard room → secret corridor → **[[The Bell Junction]]**.

## What happened

**[[The Pillared Reach]] — the map-stone.** Called as an audible: **the [[The Map-Stone|map-stone]] was hidden in a wall panel here** rather than at its planned location. Recovered.

**[[The Buzzsaw Room|The Millrace]] — wandering monster: 2 hyenas.** Both went into the water. [[Gern]] caused one to flee in a random direction and it ran off the ledge; [[Tank]] knocked the other off the catwalk with the killing blow. *(Second fight running that the room's geography has resolved — worth noticing as a pattern the players may start exploiting.)*

- **Loot: [[Gloves of Missile Snaring]].**
- **⭐ The sigil is dead.** White chalk still visible on the floor where it was, **no longer glowing.** See below.

**[[The Stutter Stairs]].** Puzzled over it a while, solved it. **The secret room holding the compass-stone was removed from here** and relocated to the map-stone's originally planned position. Exited by the far door.

**Switchback room.** Sparse.

> [!danger] New rule established — the reset fires on exit, not on a clock
> The [[The Octagonal Vestibule|Octagonal Vestibule]] **had not reset**, because the party never left the dungeon — they came back around via the portal. The spoke they chose in Session 5 was still the only open one; **every other doorway was still walled off.** They had to go the long way.
>
> **This is a significant and clean mechanic.** A party that stays down indefinitely faces a **persistent, degrading** dungeon: consumed resources stay consumed, collapsed routes stay collapsed, and cleared rooms stay cleared. Repeated short delves and one long delve are now strategically different things.
>
> Needs adding to [[The Recall]] and [[Floor Reset and the Intelligence Gradient]].

**Checkerboard room.** Solved the puzzle and opened the door — but **the table did not grasp the mechanism.** They didn't register that **the door's position was signalling which things in the room to activate.** [[Tank]] found one of the pit traps by accident and made a strong Dex save. Through to the next room and **claimed the [[The Compass-Stone|compass-stone]].**

**Secret door and corridor** back into the Bell Junction.

**[[Gern]] failed his Wisdom save** and started down the archway toward the singing. **[[Tank]] restrained him.**

## Where Session 7 opens

**[[The Bell Junction]], facing the archway** — the same position Session 5 ended in, minus the portal escape and minus a meaningful chunk of spell slots.

**They are one room from [[The Siren]] and they are depleted.** Leaving to regroup is a live, legitimate option and they know the way out. If they take it, run the [[Dominique Ferraro|Dominique]] **all-retreat** branch.

## Encounter retune — one harpy

Given party resources, **[[The Siren]] runs with a single harpy** rather than two.

Under the Chorus rule the DC is driven by singer count, so this is **two rounds of grace, not a softer fight**:

| Round | Siren + 2 harpies | **Siren + 1 harpy** |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 17 | **15** |
| 3 | 19 | **17** |
| 5 | 21 | **19** |
| 7 | 21 | **21** |

Same wall, reached later. It also sharpens the tactic: **one singer to kill**, AC 11, 25 HP, and **the DC drops 2 the moment it dies** — plausibly a one-round job for Tank with the `TANK` hammer.

**Gern's failed save at the bell was a free rehearsal** for restraining a charmed ally. In the shaft, make it a **contested check** — a charmed PC does not want to be saved — so the move that worked in a corridor becomes a real cost in a room with a drop.

## What the dead sigil gives them

> [!danger] The open question from Session 5 is now answered
> **White chalk, still there, no longer glowing.** Per the one-active-sigil rule in [[Wesley's Sigils]], it went inert the moment Wesley drew the new one in [[The Spiral Stair Room|Room 8]].
>
> **What the party can derive, unaided:**
> - The chalk **survived a reset** — so it isn't a dungeon feature, it's a *person's* mark ([[The Recall]]).
> - It was live in Session 1 and is dead now. **Something changed down here in the intervening week.**
> - And the thing they're about to walk into is **lit green in exactly the same way.**
>
> That's takeaway #3 — *someone else is active down there, and it started about a week ago* — sitting on the floor in front of them, requiring no exposition. **Do not connect it for them.**
