# Episode 3 — Second Shift

Up: [[Episodes]]

**The Flats is sleepwalking to the mill. The party goes down to stop it, gets taken apart by a [[The Siren|Siren]] bound to [[Wesley's Sigils|Wesley's sigil]], and spends the rest of the episode working out how to fight something that kills you by making you love it. Featured Rival: [[Ren Sołtys]] (debut, and hollowed in the last thirty seconds). Wesley fingerprint: present, unattributed.**

> [!note] Status — in play
> **[[Session 5 — Second Shift]] played 2026-08-28.** Cold open through the first delve. **The party never reached the Siren** — they rang the [[The Bell Junction|Bell Junction]] bell, found the portal, and took it *away* from the singing. Session 6 opens mid-delve in [[The Buzzsaw Room]], no long rest.

## The mechanism — two independent conditions

> [!danger] Susceptibility is a scar; reach is a radius
> **[[Nullprints]] fade to nothing on recovery**, so the connection isn't a live file — it's **[[Nullprints|the notch]]**. Once the machine has taken a bite of you there's a place in you shaped like its teeth, and it never closes. **The Siren isn't reaching down a wire. She's calling, and the only ears that can hear her are ears that have been bitten.**
>
> **But the notch decides *whether*. Distance decides *when*.**
>
> Her reach is a **radius from the mill, and it grows every night.** Everyone inside it who has ever been hollowed walks. Everyone outside it sleeps through it, notch or no notch.
>
> **The party can only observe one of the two conditions**, which is exactly why they need both [[Dominique Ferraro|Dominique]] (who can map the circle) and [[Ren Sołtys|Ren]] (who has the only record of who's been bitten).

| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| Never hollowed | Nothing, at any distance. No notch |
| **Hollowed, recovered, inside the radius** | **Walks. No save.** [[Tomek Sołtys\|Tomek]] and most of the roster |
| Hollowed, recovered, **outside** the radius | Nothing yet. **They're just next** |
| Hollowed, **ember-bearer**, inside | **Walks, then wakes.** Wis save with advantage — the ember spends itself, and it hurts |
| **Currently hollowed** | **Doesn't walk.** Nothing in there to call. **[[Wendell Pace\|Wendell]] is the control group** |
| Terminal ([[Henry Voss]]) | Doesn't walk. Already gone |

### The radius

Keep it in **streets**, not miles, so the table can draw it.

| Night | Reach | Walkers |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Ellis, Delaney — the mill-road blocks | 3 |
| 3–6 | Out through the near Flats | 9 |
| **7–8 (Session 5)** | **Reaches Tank's and Old Man's street** | **11** |
| Next | The far Flats, then the transitional blocks toward the Heights | ? |

**Why the PCs weren't walking earlier isn't severity — it's geography.** The circle reached their street on night seven. The ember doesn't decide whether she can reach them; it decides that **they wake up.**

> [!note] Locked ruling — ember-bearers and compulsion
> **Advantage on saves against charm, domination, and compulsion.** Narrate as the chest-warmth going *hot and painful* — saving you from this hurts.

## Act 1 — the night

**Cold open, 1:50 a.m.** Tomek doesn't wake up; he's already up. The voice is **Ren**, calling him to come and look at something, delighted — *"it has been a long while since anybody was delighted about him."* He walks past his sister twice. She writes down the time.

> [!note] The register — hold it for every walker
> **An invitation you don't have to earn.** No urgency, no debt, nobody disappointed in you. Being called in off the street at dusk when you were a kid: the day is over, you're allowed to stop, and somebody is glad it's you.
>
> **Avoid entirely:** lateness, being expected, having failed to arrive. *Reserved for [[Episode 4 — The Crossing]].*
>
> **Vocabulary:** come in · come here · it's alright · you can stop · nothing you have to fix first · **"I'm coming."**

**2:40 a.m., the lawns.** Tank and Old Man come to standing up, barefoot, nine houses out. **Each gets one bonus Night TP for this scene only.** Don't roll — they wake because they have embers.

**Everything topside fails.** Can't wake a walker; can't block one — they go around like water; restrained, they stand patient as a dog at a door. Asked a question, one answers without stopping or looking: ***"I'm coming."*** Wendell is asleep in his bed.

**The gate** *(played as the front gate with the chain and master lock, not a fence dip)*. Notched PCs hear it coming up out of the ground. **DC 13 Wis, advantage.**

**Ren, writing.** Full scene in [[Ren Sołtys]]. **She does not hand over the notebook in Act 1.** She warns them off and fails — her brother went in on a dare and she couldn't stop him; now four kids are about to do the same.

## Act 2 — the delve and the wipe

**3 TP.** Route: Entrance → [[The Cataloguing Office]] → [[The Octagonal Vestibule]] → [[The Bell Junction]] → [[The Spiral Stair Room]].

**The reset.** See **[[The Recall]]** for the full persistence table — the party ran their own experiments in Session 5 and the answers are canon.

**The shaft.** Geometry and stat block in [[The Spiral Stair Room]] and [[The Siren]]. Run her **at full strength**; show **both kill routes** at least once. The drop is the one they'll remember, because the horror isn't being grabbed — it's that they went willingly.

**Fleeing is real and it works.** She's tethered; the harpies won't leave her. **Anyone who gets out under their own power takes no notch** — [[Bash Whitlock|Bash]] and [[Gern]] currently have zero.

**Three branches:** full wipe (intended) · partial · **they win — let them.** **Do not railroad the loss.**

## Act 3 — town

**The lost day.** Everyone who dropped loses **exactly one day. All of them. Same as last time.** Don't comment. It detonates when the notebook comes out.

### [[Dominique Ferraro|Dominique]] — the confrontation

> [!danger] The design lock
> The lesson the party learns here — *"Dominique gets us answers we can't get"* — **is the thing that loses her.** You don't need a second mechanism. Make the useful option cheap and the including option cost something, and track honestly which they pick. Tracker and prices live in [[Dominique Ferraro]].

**Staging: Bash's kitchen. She's already there when he comes back to himself, and she's been there since yesterday.** She came by, found her cousin glassy and unresponsive, and stayed.

**She's Filter-porous, and it's Bash's fault.** She watched a fifteen-year-old not be a person for a day and **it didn't slide off her**, because he'd already told her the whole story and she half-believes it. **Belief is what makes the Filter fail.** His Session-3 overshare protected her perception — a good thing that is going to cost.

**What started her:** nobody asked, and she didn't go looking. **The town brought it to her**, because that's what happens around Dominique. Somebody's mother mentioned it in a checkout line — *my husband keeps waking up on the porch with his shoes off* — the way people tell her things. Then somebody else said something similar. She filed it as a funny coincidence. Then she found Bash glassy and stayed the day, and it stopped being funny. **That night she drove up the mill road and saw the row at the gate, and did not get out of the car.**

**Her hurt looks like extra grace.** She's a bridge person; managing rooms is what she does. Wounded, she doesn't escalate — she gets *more gracious*, smooths it over, and moves on to being useful. **That smoothing is the wound**, and it's the trap for the players.

**Beats, in order:**

1. **She's not angry that they went.** Establish early — she knows she can't go. That was never the complaint.
2. **The complaint is that she found out by watching.** *"I sat here yesterday and asked if you wanted a sandwich and you looked right at me."*
3. **One hard line, once.** *"I'm not asking to come. I know I can't come. I'm asking you to tell me when you're going, so that when it happens I'm not sitting here trying to work out whether to call an ambulance on my own cousin."*
4. **Then she takes it back.** *"Okay — I've been up all night, that came out worse than I meant."* A laugh. A hand wave. **The off-ramp.**
5. **Then, unasked, she produces what she's been doing.**

> [!danger] Step 4 is the whole scene
> If someone **declines the off-ramp** — doesn't accept the apology, says *"no, you're right"* — that's the single most valuable thing any PC does for her all summer (**+2**). If everyone takes it gratefully and moves to the intel, the drift starts and nobody at the table feels a thing.

### What she brings

Her unique asset is something she earned in this scene: **she spent a full day sitting with a hollowed person.** She is the only adult-adjacent person in town who knows what it looks like up close, and she learned it from Bash.

> *"There were eleven up there last night. I counted twice. It was nine the night before — I know, because I counted then too."*
>
> *"And [[Stan Prokop]]'s at Marco's this morning and he's not right. Marco's got him wiping the same section of counter."*
>
> *A beat. She's careful with this part.*
>
> *"He looks like you did yesterday."*

Then the reframe:

> *"The eleven aren't random. I drove it. The first ones are all up off the mill road — Ellis, Delaney, that stretch. The two new ones last night were further out. Further than the ones before them."*
>
> *"It's getting bigger. Like a circle."*
>
> *"You two are on the edge of it."*

**And the wall she genuinely cannot climb:**

> *"What I'd need is everybody it's ever happened to. Not just now — going back. So I could look at the next few streets out and know who to watch."*
>
> *"There isn't a list like that."*

**There is.** Four years, dated, with durations, in a marble composition book, kept by a fifteen-year-old because nobody else would remember. **Dominique defines the problem and hands them the reason they need Ren.** No misdirection required.

> [!note] The credit moment
> If anyone says *"you were right, you just couldn't check it"* — **out loud, to her** — that's **+2** and the best thing that happens to her this month. Most tables will say it to each other instead. **She'll wave it off. She always does.**

### Why the stakes changed

It stopped being *understand this* and became **who's next, and can we get to them first.** Somebody got through the fence last night while the party was out of it. **Every night, somebody might.** That's the pressure that justifies going back down without a long rest.

### Partial-wipe variant

No confrontation — no time, and the survivors are short-handed and scared. **She becomes the second body**, and they discover it experimentally: a lone PC can't get into a stranger's kitchen; Dominique can, and she'll be offered coffee.

**The confrontation still happens, deferred to when the hollowed wake** — and it's worse, because by then she's spent a day being useful and has more standing.

### [[Ren Sołtys|Ren]] finds them

She warned them, they went anyway, and she's spent a day and a half watching four more kids not be people. The notebook, finally — contents and the four deductions in [[Ren Sołtys]].

**Her book is the historical roster and the durations. Dominique's map is the radius. Neither is sufficient alone**, and the party is the only thing that can put them together.

### How Ruth pops

**Put the roster on the map and it's a picture, not a calculation.** Every previously-hollowed person inside the radius is walking — **except one dot, well inside the innermost ring, dark.**

**[[Ruth Bielak]], on Delaney.** Two blocks off the mill road, hollowed in '94, never once out there. **She should have been in the first three.**

The anomaly as a hole in a circle: legible to any player who looks at the map, no statistics, no misdirection. And it points straight at *why is that one house different* — which is exactly the question Dominique is best equipped to go answer.

### [[The Community Center]] — [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]]

Structurally the redundancy for Ren: if they skipped or burned her, he keeps the episode running.

> [!danger] The eccentricity: he keeps a notebook
> Small, cheap, back pocket, soft at the corners. Names and dates and a word or two. Unembarrassed: *"I can't hold it all. Never could. So I write it down."*
>
> **Reads as:** a man who runs a community center and remembers people. **Is:** four years of candidates.
>
> **The rhyme is the point.** Two people in this episode keep notebooks of hollowed townspeople — one out of fear, one out of appetite, physically indistinguishable. **If the party has Ren's book in a backpack while he opens his, draw no attention to it. Ever.**

His list overlaps Ren's almost exactly. If you want the sharp version, he thumbs past a page with **their four names on it**, dated mid-June: *"Somebody said you'd been out at the mill overnight and looked half dead for a couple days after. I keep an eye on that kind of thing. Nobody keeps an eye on kids in this town. Somebody should."* Every word true.

**The thing he doesn't do: he never asks a second question.** A normal adult digs. He doesn't — because he already knows why anyone in this town asks that.

**One warm beat that costs him and gains him nothing.** And on Ricky, if he comes up: *"That boy didn't get lost. People don't come back from getting lost looking like that."*

**Props:** chalk in the seams of his knuckles, once, never mentioned again. And the last shot — through the window, Ren at a table with Wesley, *talking*, and he is listening, and it looks like a good thing. Because it is one.

### [[Panels]]

**1 TP to ingratiate** and Russ recommends **MIDNIGHT SHIFT #11, "The Lorelei Waltz"** — the villain sings people off a bridge; the hero beats her with a dictaphone played back warped. He hands them the solution as a plot summary and nobody notices.

### [[Ruth Bielak]] and the Talkboy

Her husband died in '89; she sleeps with a cassette of his voice on the windowsill, seven years, every night. **The belt is stretched. It runs slow.** *"He sounds slower than he was. I keep meaning to get it fixed."*

**She isn't protected by anything clever. She's protected by a broken tape player.**

> [!danger] This cannot come from the party
> Four kids do not get told about a dead husband's voice on a cassette. **The party goes door to door and gets names. Dominique goes to one house and stays.** That's what she's for — and it's precisely the skill that will be used on the party in September.

**[[Bash Whitlock|Bash]] gets the mechanism.** Dominique gets the fact; Bash gets the why. The belt, the drag, playback dropped about an octave — **it isn't that the voice is comforting, it's that it's on her frequency.** Her notch is occupied; nothing else can get in. **Content is irrelevant. Any recording. Only speed matters.** He's right, and he should get to be right out loud.

> **THE TALKBOY** *([[Jerry]]'s, six dollars, bin of dead Christmas toys)*
> Any recording, played on SLOW. **3 charges** — the tape stretches further every pass.
> **Bonus action.** Until the start of your next turn, allies within 30 ft are **immune to the Siren's song and to Luring Song.**
> **First use only:** she hears a human voice at the wrong speed and it isn't hers. **DC 13 Wis or stunned until the end of her next turn.**
> After the third charge the tape is unreadable.

**Let the party decide who carries it.** Tank is the natural answer — free bonus action after round one, no ranged attack. **Don't steer.** Let whoever records it choose what goes on the tape, on screen, before they know what it's for.

> [!note] The wax problem — needs a ruling
> The party landed on **wax in the ears** at the end of Session 5. Recommended: let it fail informatively — it muffles the sound, but the pull isn't acoustic where it matters. Then Gern's *other* idea (ringing the Bell Junction bell to drown her out) makes Ruth's discovery a **refinement of something they already guessed** rather than a handout.

## Act 4 — the return

**3 TP.** Same route, fast. **Charm drops to DC 13.** **Draining Kiss and Take Me Home stay live.** Three Talkboy charges against a six-round fight means whoever holds it covers half and has to choose which half. **She is not weaker. They are equipped.**

**Clearing it.** *The green goes out. That's all. No sound. The chalk on the floor is just chalk now.* **This costs Wesley significant delve progress** — he has to redraw, and they will never know.

**What the room gives them:** the sigil was *drawn* — chalk, human hand, a compass-steady ten-foot circle. **Tally marks nearby, same as Session 2, with a fresh stroke added.** A spent torch. Adult boot prints, work sole, ~size 11.

## The closer — Ren goes in

Full treatment in [[Ren Sołtys]]. **Circumstance-driven, not Wesley-driven.** The trigger is **[[Tomek Sołtys|Tomek]]** — deliberately held back from the mid-episode hollowing, which is [[Stan Prokop]]'s, so that Tomek is available for this. **The permission is the party**: she watched four kids go in and come back in one day, and her model updated the only way she has ever updated a model. She's wrong because of an ember she can't know about. **Ren is hollowed by her own methodology.**

On the mill road in the small hours she comes down toward them with the notebook still in her hand. *"Ren?"* She goes right past. *"Ren."* She does not turn her head. **The cold open, inverted, same two lines.** Then: *you take the notebook out of her hand. She lets you.*

**Nine days.** Gone for all of [[Episode 4 — The Crossing]].

## Aftermath

- The walking stops that night. Nobody notices except the party.
- **Tomek is fine.** Completely. At the Sinclair Monday morning.
- **[[Stan Prokop]] is not.** He's at Marco's next to [[Wendell Pace|Wendell]] — two glassy bussers, and Marco doesn't connect it.
- **Ricky is still doubting**, and his hands still don't work.
- Ren's mother has nine days of a daughter who isn't there, and **the Center brings meals to families in trouble.**
- The Community Center is on their map, with a closed door at the back nobody mentioned.

## Open dials

- **Ren's saving-grace line** isn't locked.
- **The Act 4 fight may be too easy** with the Talkboy plus a party who knows the room.
- **Ruth's tape content** is unwritten. What Walt is saying matters more than it looks.
- **Marcy at the Center** — already a recruit in June, or later?
- What Russ calls the Siren off a VHS box.
- Whether the radius keeps growing after the sigil clears *(no — it stops that night)*.
