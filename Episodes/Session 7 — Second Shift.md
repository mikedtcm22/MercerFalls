# Session 7 — Second Shift

Up: [[Episode 3 — Second Shift]]

**Played 2026-09-25, *The Song That Calls Them*. Full TPK against [[The Siren]] — all four hollowed, one day lost each — followed by the best town half the campaign has run. [[Ren Sołtys|Ren]] found the [[Ruth Bielak|Ruth]] anomaly herself off her own notebook, [[Dominique Ferraro|Dominique]] got them through Ruth's front door, and [[Bash Whitlock|Bash]] built the [[The Modded Talkboy|Modded Talkboy]].**

> [!note] Numbering
> The session recorder missed the 2026-09-09 dungeon-loop night, so the group's own file numbering runs one behind. **Vault order is authoritative:** S5 (08-28) → S6 (09-09, DM notes only) → **S7 (09-25)**.

## The fight

**Going in: zero spell slots, zero rages.** The GM flagged it at the end — *"this is not going to go well"* — and it was the design working. The party walked into the boss on fumes because they'd spent two sessions looping Floor 1.

**Approach.** [[Tank]] threw the `TANK` warhammer at the bell 15 ft up, connected, and the ring **drowned out the singing locally** and lit the bell-door. They did **not** take the portal this time. [[Old Man]] sustained **Thaumaturgy** (vibraphone, middle C) as a counter-tone; [[Gern]] later plugged his ears.

> [!note] Precedents set — carry these forward
> **Sustained counter-sound (Thaumaturgy): +1 to the save.** **Fingers in ears: +1.** Acoustic muffling gives a real but small edge. It does not solve the song, which is exactly the ruling [[Ruth Bielak|Ruth]]'s discovery needed.

**The room.** Bell-shaped chamber, spiral staircase climbing out of sight, **catwalks roughly every 20 feet.** Siren perched with two harpies.

**Charm ran at DC 15, one target at a time — lowest roller gets charmed.** Clean, fast, and it worked; keep it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **[[Bash Whitlock\|Bash]]** | Charmed round 1. Dashed to her voluntarily. Grappled — *"for all intents and purposes this is a grapple, but since you're charmed it's just an embrace"* — flown up, **dropped. 27 fall damage.** Tank positioned to catch him and, at 9 HP, chose not to eat the damage. |
| **[[Gern]]** | Charmed on a natural 1. *"Give mommy a kiss."* **Draining Kiss.** Gone in one action. |
| **[[Old Man]]** | Charmed, lured up to a catwalk, shoved off. 10 damage. His comfort-voice was **a Vonnegut lecture on library cassette.** |
| **[[Tank]]** | Killed **both harpies**, never failed a charm save until the end, then ran for an unexplored archway, **failed a DC 13 at distance**, was lured two stories up and shoved off. |

**Full TPK.** All four woke at home. **One day each.** Long rest granted, slots restored.

**The sigil.** Gern rolled a natural 20 on Arcana, then a 14, and **clocked the green sigil** in the corner — recognising it as the same figure the man drew in Session 1. Critically he also noticed **there was no shimmering portal this time.** Old Man tried to deface it with calligraphy ink (no effect); Tank struck it with the warhammer (green flash, the hammer bounced, no effect).

**Ruling made in play: Spare the Dying does nothing in the dungeon.**

**New:** the party exchanged phone numbers.

## The town half

### [[Dominique Ferraro|Dominique]]

**She was in Bash's kitchen when he woke, and she'd stayed the night.** Not angry — frightened. *"I tried everything to get through to you yesterday. You barely knew who I was. You were cordial, and if I said things you would respond, but it was clear he didn't really know what was going on."*

> [!danger] The confrontation never fired
> The designed grievance — *"you didn't tell me you were going"* — **never came up**, because Bash volunteered everything before she had to ask. She was scared, then supportive, then useful.
>
> **Affinity ledger opens at +4:** told before asked (+1), brought along (+1), **brought to a scene they'd have passed** (+2, Gern: *"You want to join our crew?"*).
>
> **Two watch-items.** The party **has not yet discovered the free option** — nobody has sent her anywhere alone, so the −1 engine hasn't engaged. And **Old Man is openly flirting with her** (Tank objected on Bash's behalf). **Score flirting at zero** — attention is not membership, and reading it as such gives a false positive.

**⭐ She works part-time at the [[Mercer Falls Historical Society|Historical Society]]** — filing, assistant-librarian work. Biggest canon of the night. Her note has always said she's bookish enough to follow the [[The Mercer Mansion|Mercer papers]] herself; **that now has an address and a job title**, and nobody will ever think twice about her being in that building.

She also reported multiple people at the diner losing sleep, and confirmed **she sleeps fine** — correct, she has no notch.

### [[Wendell Pace]] — recovered

At [[Marco's Diner|Marco's]], cheerful, refilling waters, no recognition. *"That's odd you say that. I do have the distinct impression that I was there recently — but I've been real out of it the past couple days."* Then he trails off and changes the subject. **Insight 13: not lying — genuinely grasping at something, failing, and giving up mentally.**

Bash hummed the Siren's song at him. Nothing.

### [[Ren Sołtys|Ren]] — at the counter, with the notebook

She opened with *"You guys went back there,"* then: **"I don't understand how it's so short for you."**

**The handout did the work.** She showed both pages. Gern asked to be added and she was already mid-update. What came out of her, unprompted:

- **[[Lowell Brandt|L. Brandt]]** — Gern asked about the *died* entry. An accident, about a year ago, per the paper. **"I didn't see him come back to himself."**
- **The radius model, in her own words** — she pointed at the streets and explained that it's reaching further each night.
- **⭐ The [[Ruth Bielak|Ruth]] anomaly, found by her.** *"Wait a minute — Ruth lives on Ellis Street. She should be walking. If the closest people get called first she should be up there. I don't understand why it's not affecting her."*

> [!note] Designed as Dominique's beat, delivered by Ren — and it's better
> It costs nothing, because the prop carried it. And it gives Ren **a reason the party values her that isn't sentiment.** She isn't a source they extract from; she solved it in front of them. Dominique's irreplaceable contribution survived intact, just relocated to getting them through Ruth's door.

**Her hobby: gymnastics.** Shy about it, uncomfortable discussing anything she likes.

**Old Man asked to meet up later and learn more, and she said yes** — *"It'd be nice for somebody to not forget all the time. It's no fun being the only one."* He gave her his phone number. **She already knew where he lived.**

One sour note: the party mentioned having *"killed three or four people"* down there and **she flinched and physically shifted away.**

### [[Ruth Bielak|Ruth]] — and what they did to her

All four PCs **plus Dominique** went to her door. Tea, chocolate milk for Gern.

Walt died in **[[The 1991 Mill Incident|the 1991 incident]]**, as written. **New:** she visits a park bench by the waterfall with a view of the mill rising over it, and sits there thinking about him.

She played the tape. The GM described the warble exactly. **Old Man rolled a natural 27 Persuasion** to borrow the recorder overnight to "fix" it. **Bash cast Mending and fixed it.**

**Old Man's Performance 13** then put it together: human speech at that speed is why Ruth sleeps through the night.

**They returned it working.** She was delighted — *"I can finally hear my Walt the way he used to sound."* Bash earned inspiration for it, and said the quiet part out loud: ***"I just signed her death warrant."***

**That night, testing the Talkboy, Ruth was among the walkers.**

> [!danger] Cash this slowly
> They talked a grieving widow out of the only thing protecting her, out of kindness, for a good reason, and knew it as they did it. **Don't underline it.** Just have her out there again the next night, and the night after.

### The build

Bash had a **Talkboy** in the back of his closet. Arcana 14 to wire a powered speaker into the headphone jack. Full rules in **[[The Modded Talkboy]]**.

**Field-tested that night on the walkers:** they come to their senses inside the radius and relapse when the party moves away — which is how the party established the range themselves.

**They plan to record Hootie and the Blowfish.**

**Two TP into the day; the build consumed the rest.** No level-up until the episode ends.

## Where Session 8 opens

**All four wake mid-night, half-walking toward the mill.** They are all notched now.

Among the walkers: **[[Wendell Pace|Wendell]]** — a payoff, not a contradiction, since he's recovered and therefore susceptible, and the party explicitly puzzled over his absence — and **[[Ruth Bielak|Ruth]]**, because of them.

**[[Ren Sołtys|Ren]] and [[Tomek Sołtys|Tomek]] are not there.** Tomek got in; Ren went after him. Don't volunteer it; answer it if asked. If they phone Ren, her mother doesn't know where she is.

> [!danger] The notebook changes hands
> After the Siren dies, depending on how they exit: **either** they find Ren's and Tomek's nullprints on the way out with the **marble composition book lying on the floor between them**, **or** they pass the two of them walking home and **Ren absent-mindedly drops it.**
>
> Either way the party ends the episode holding it. **What they do with it while she is hollow sets her disposition going forward** — see [[Ren Sołtys]].
