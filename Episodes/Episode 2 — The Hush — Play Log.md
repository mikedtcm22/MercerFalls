# Session 3 — the reassembly and the first two days

## Cold open — Ricky, in third person
Run exactly as designed. [[Ricky Vance]] — starting wide receiver, the crew's designated evidence-man — waves off his buddies at the falls overlook, takes a **Polaroid camera** back up through the gap in the fence, and settles in outside the Blast Furnace to catch the two younger kids running out screaming. The yard goes *flat* quiet; crickets cut mid-note; something pads in a slow circle. A high window bursts. He grins, reads it as the prank working, bangs on the barred front doors for his own jump-scare — and nothing comes out the door. **Something comes out the window.** His thumb fires the shutter on instinct, the flash pops, the film whirs out gray on something the frame doesn't quite hold, and he drops the camera and runs for the gate. Cut.

The **dropped Polaroid** is in the weeds by the gate, unclaimed.

## Day B, morning — the two who fell wake up
- **[[Tank]]** wakes in his own bed at ~10:30–11am, hungover-groggy, with **all his dungeon gear leaning against his furniture** — the club/bat and the hammer, undeniably real. **[[Dominique Ferraro|Dominique]] is waiting on his stoop** and unloads: nobody has seen Ricky in a day-plus, Blake and Tyler are telling the whole town a mill kid ratted him out, somebody saw Ricky's dad throwing duffel bags into his pickup and speeding off, and the military-academy threat is presumed executed. Her ask is her cousin: *"I can't have Bash be the kid with a target on his back from the jocks for the rest of the school year."* Tank rolled **17** on a Charisma read and she came down off the boil — *"I'm sorry. I'm just trying to protect my baby cousin. I could really use your help."* **She formally enlists him.** (Vector Tank→Bash, live.)
- **[[Old Man]]** wakes with his instrument and daggers strewn on his desk and gets **Petey** on the sidewalk, carrying both designed payloads: *(1)* **the sighting** — out past the Kowalczyks, "a dog except it wasn't a dog," far too big, walking funny, and *the streetlight's light ran away from it and bounced around it,* with a **clicking** sound; and *(2)* **the lost day** — *"I came to get you yesterday but you were weird. You looked right at me and didn't say anything."* Old Man talked him down to a nap-dream and covered the blank day with his "grown-up walks," and sent him off reassured. He is privately unbothered — *"I'm not too concerned about lost time. It's happened before. It's part of the lifestyle."*

> [!note] The sighting is the adult Hush, and Petey described it correctly
> Every element is on-model — a glimpse **reads as a stray dog**, the **light-eating hide** rendered by a seven-year-old as light "running away from it," the **spin-and-click** search state, and the decoupled gait ("walked funny"). See [[The Hush]]. Old Man filed it as wild dogs, and the party's shorthand for it has become **"the dog creature."**

## Day A — flashback, the day before
- **[[Bash Whitlock|Bash]] & [[Gern]]** wake on the [[The Mill|mill]] grounds beside a smoldering campfire at ~8am, gear intact, bikes still chained to the fence. Both had told their parents they were sleeping over at the other's house — the [[Time Points|sleepover cover]], with its delayed fuse now live. Gern's immediate reaction is doctrinal jubilation (*"Everything I've been told has been coming true"*) and he recites a Certaintist affirmation on the spot — see [[Certaintism]].
- **[[Marco's Diner|Marco's]], with Bash's mom.** She spots them biking home off her night shift and offers breakfast (a station wagon, established at the table). GM gave Bash an insight check to confirm the offer was **genuinely optional** — a small, deliberate no-coercion beat. Cover story: *NeverEnding Story*, Gern got scared, they left the lights on and played with the new radio. **Deception 8 and 13 — she buys it warmly.**
  - **Hollowed [[Wendell Pace]] is bussing tables.** Bash went looking; GM rolled a **nat 20** insight for him: Wendell looks him dead in the eye with **zero registration**, treats every customer in the room as a stranger, moves sluggish and zombie-like — the lights on and nobody home. Gern nearly gave the game away. Prompting him about the school flickered *something*, and he pitched them a **folklore-and-legends exhibit at the [[The Mercer Mansion|Mercer Mansion]]** (Historical Society, all day). Bash's mom seconded it.
- **The accusation.** Back at Bash's house, **Blake and Tyler** are on the lawn berating Gern: there's no photo, Ricky's gone, one of the mill kids snitched. Bash immediately cast **Sanctuary** on Gern. **Gern's persuasion: 3.** They didn't believe a word, threatened him and the summer, and only backed off when they clocked Bash's mom watching from the window. *The named scapegoat took the hit alone and failed the roll, exactly as designed.*
- **Town-Time, first spend.** Gern rode home to show his face (parents out; he **stashed his mace and staff under the back porch** and left a note), then the two went looking for Tank: **the white pages at a phone booth** for the address (GM waived the roll for the period detail), and **Tank's father** answered — annoyed, unhelpful, *"He's out carousing with those second-stringers. Should be in the gym."*
- **They find hollowed Old Man.** Riding the neighborhood, they see Old Man cycle past, give a flat little wave, and turn into the woods. Following cost an extra point: **nature 14** found the bike against a tree and a trail; a survival check tracked him to a boulder by a bubbling stream, where he sat bolt upright staring straight ahead. He did not know them — placed Bash only as *"Dominique's cousin"* — said they were harshing his spot, and moseyed off the way he came. **The cost of falling, witnessed from outside.**
- **Evening — Bash tells Dominique everything.** Given her alone, Bash gave her the **entire account**: the dungeon, the torches, the radio, the dog creature, the janitor, Tank and Old Man dying — and then **demonstrated a spell,** conjuring an oil-drenched torch into his bare hand via his tinkering. She read the conjuration as **a very good magic trick** (*"I didn't see anything up your sleeve… magicians and their secrets"*) and the rest as a wild story she wasn't going to call a lie. Her practical note: it won't play as proof he didn't snitch. Bash: *"For the first time in my life, I can take care of myself"* — which landed on her, visibly.

> [!note] Dominique's position — settled, deliberately open-ended
> Bash handed her near-complete intelligence, and she's provisionally in — *by design, per DM ruling.* She's more useful to the party for knowing, and her eventual defection is a long-game payoff, not a Session-4 problem. See [[Dominique Ferraro]] for the fracture-generation framework this now feeds.

## Day B — the reconvene, and the first real Town-Time day
- **[[Tank]] → the school gym.** His stated first move: *"the only logical thing to do — go to the gym. Gotta talk to the football players. I need to get my pump on anyways."* Morning lift, alone as usual. On the way out he met two **practice-squad** kids fishing for status (*"I heard Ricky got ratted out by one of those mouth breathers"*). **Intimidation 21.** He shut it down cold — *"I'm the one who decides who gets vendettas pulled against them"* — and made them repeat back that they'd do nothing, and would redirect anyone with something to say to him. They're terrified and will spread the word. **First locked date on the clock:** they said Ricky's been gone *"going on two days now."*
- **[[Old Man]] → [[The Library|the Library]].** He asked whether a historical society existed; **history check 5**, so it didn't come to mind — library it is. Inside, one librarian (**Timothy**) and one other patron at the microfilm.
  - **[[Reverend Hollis]]** at the microfilm reader (the table said "Follett" — a mishearing; **Hollis is correct as spoken**) — kills the machine the moment Old Man comes up behind him, nods, gathers his things and goes. **Perception nat 1:** Old Man learned nothing about what he was reading. *The self-concealing staging worked perfectly.*
  - **Investigation nat 1** — he can't recall a single name from the 1887 ledger. He does hold onto the mill's name being the town's name.
  - **2 TP on the microfiche → the disappearance pattern:** newspaper reports of **fatal "animal attacks," not frequent but recurring every couple of years, usually young people.** The [[The Library|Library]]'s core Ep2 payload, delivered — though only half of it; see the Library's own note for what's still unspent.
  - **1 more TP cultivating Timothy** (persuasion 14) — he'll pull everything on the mill's founding, ready **tomorrow morning**; Old Man said he'd come by in the afternoon.
- **[[Old Man]] → [[Jerry's Pawn Shop|Jerry's]]** (Pine Street). Traded the dungeon **lyre** — covered as a probate inheritance from "Great Uncle Rudolph," pitched on the bone nut and saddle and abalone inlay — to clear his pawn debt and **redeem his Epiphone Thunderbird**. Pure re-flavoring — same recovered instrument, now thematically him. Jerry threw in strings for tuning the shop's guitar stock (extra TP; Old Man also gave Jerry honest advice on which to window). Then bought a **miner's helmet with a working lamp, $20 cash** — *"Tommy"* scrawled inside — deliberately at full price as goodwill, and left a **standing request** for flashlights, maglites, and helmet lamps.
  - **Jerry's lead, unprompted:** **[[Wade Vance]]** came in, bought *"way more shells than you'd need for deer,"* paid cash, threw it all in a duffel bag and bolted. Jerry's read: *"He's one of those hush-hush military badass dudes. Weird he came to me and not his unit."* Old Man clocked it as possibly deliberate paper-trail avoidance and filed it away. *(This does pre-spoil the* category *of Wade's reveal — accepted; the den scene will deliver scale instead. See [[Episode 2 — The Hush]].)*
- **The three-way, then the four-way.** Tank ran into Bash and Gern converging on the diner around 10am. Tank interrogated Gern hard about Ricky (Gern: *"I didn't see no Ricky"*), and learned from Bash that **he had lost an entire day** — his first real horror of the session was that he'd missed workouts (*"I was wondering why my bench wasn't as high as it should have been"*). They went to the pawn shop; **Old Man was there and remembered everything**, his memory resuming from the moment he cracked his head in the underground river.
- **The walk-and-talk.** Bash laid out the theory on the sidewalk: *you two died; you and Wendell both became empty shells; you didn't recognize me yesterday and you do today.* Old Man refused the inference on principle — **David Hume, the black swan, "the existence of this does not indicate its definition"** — and continues to hold that this may still be an elaborate prank. Gern's counter-theory: the Snarl took their bodies, they rematerialized, they were too full of doubt to see straight yesterday and have leveled out today.
- **Second gym trip — the protection play.** Tank hauled Bash back to the gym specifically so teammates would see them together (*"you, me, gym — people don't mess with you anymore"*), and used the walk to finally tell Bash **the truth about the prank**: he was taken to the mill to be scared into running out crying while Ricky photographed it. Bash took it standing — *"you and your friends are really fucking stupid"* — with real heat, which Tank respected. A couple of teammates clocked them together and walked away from Bash. Bash also privately **tested Mage Hand's carry limit** on the dumbbell rack. See [[The School Gym]].
  - **The defensive coordinator** stopped Tank on the way out: he'd run into **Patricia** (Ricky's mother), *"real worried,"* and hasn't seen Ricky all day, which is abnormal. Suggested Tank go check on him. **First of the two designed nudges toward her door** — see [[Patricia Vance]].
- **Dominique, again.** Tank walked Bash home; Dominique was in the kitchen with Bash's mom. Bash pressed the case, naming Wendell as **killed by a dogman.** She thought about it, took a breath, and gave him the line of the session: **"You say this happened. Until we find out otherwise — let's see this thing through."**
- **Evening reconvene at Bash's house.**
  - **Gern presents the Tome of Conviction**, open to the **nullprint** section, and pushes it at everyone. The illustration is **an exact match** for the outline Wendell left. **Figure 9, confirmed in front of the whole party** — and confirmed by the DM as legitimate, not something Gern is inventing.
  - **Tank refuses to even look at it,** and closes the door with *"Don't you hang out in a comic book shop where there's a dude who can control magnets? I'm certain that's in a book too. Doesn't make it real,"* then walks away. Old Man engages, but philosophically rather than sympathetically. **Bash is the only one who takes him seriously** — *"I think you're really onto something, Gern. Don't give up faith."*
  - **Gern's religion check (10) → new doctrinal fact:** susceptibility to nullprinting **is not uniform**, and *an aspect of one's conviction can dampen the effect* — it devastates ordinary people, but a person who steels themselves can mitigate it. **This is the seed for the Marco's Diner comparison** (Wendell still glassy vs. Tank & Old Man recovered) the next time they go back — see [[Certaintism]].
  - **The decoy-Ricky plan (entirely player-invented).** Bash had Tank bring his **yearbook** (retconned into the earlier walk home; GM allowed it free), picked out photos of Ricky, and asked Old Man whether he could become him. Old Man cast **Disguise Self** — **duration 1 hour** — and turned into **Ricky Vance**. The plan for the morning: appear at the diner where the team hangs out, be seen alive, vouch for Bash, and exit with *"my dad's taking me somewhere hush-hush, I won't be around for a while."* Tank's assessment: *"I've heard dumber plans."* **This plan has a real cost — see the Patricia branch in [[Episode 2 — The Hush]].**
- **Night points.** Tank and Gern were out (strict households, 1 Night point each) and both went home rather than eat a parental consequence; Tank explicitly weighed risking it and declined. **Old Man's household is lax** (+1 Night). **Bash's Time Points are unmodified** — he did not take the Lax Parents mechanic; treat his budget as the standard baseline.
  - **Old Man's night:** burpees and sprinting, noting he isn't normally this built — then pulled down *The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe* and the first two **Dark Tower** books looking for a rhyme. **Insight 17 → the twin-worlds read:** the dungeon is *a different place where different things are true;* time and death both work differently there. GM's landing: **"You died, but you didn't die — and that might not mean you're immortal."** The ember-warmth in his chest is still there and he can feel it.

## Where it ended — the stinger
**Tank, in bed.** He hears a voice calling his name. **It sounds like his own voice** — a distorted impression of it — and it's hard to place, thrown from no fixed point. Then **clicking**: faint, building until it feels like it's right outside the window or on the front lawn, then fading as whatever it is moves off.

*Mechanically: Thrown Voice plus the spin-and-click search state, at one dose — a rough bearing on his block, no fix on his window. **Tank was not re-dosed.** Still 1 dose.* Next session opens on the decoy-Ricky play at the diner.

## Where it stands, going into Session 4
- **Ricky's clock:** Day B is roughly **day 2** of dosing against a **6–7 day** window. Four to five days of runway.
- **Track A and Track B are not connected.** The party is treating Ricky's disappearance as a **social problem with a social fix** — the town believes he was shipped off, and the party's plan is to *fake him being alive*. Nobody has entertained the possibility that something took him. **This may resolve itself the hard way — see the Patricia branch in [[Episode 2 — The Hush]].**
- **The party is assembled but not bonded.** Tank has told Bash the truth about the prank; Bash is angry and standing his ground; Tank is running physical protection for him. Gern was left holding his book alone with only Bash taking him seriously. Old Man is a committed skeptic who still half-believes this is a prank.
- **Dominique is in, and that's the intended state for now** (see above). Her drift is a long-game thread — see [[Dominique Ferraro]].
- **Patricia has had one nudge sent Tank's way; a second is planned for Session 4** (likely via [[Jerry]]). See [[Patricia Vance]].
