# Episode — The Mirror

Up: [[Episodes]]

**⭐ Numbered 5 (2026-09-13).** A cursed Mercer-family mirror in the back room at [[Panels]] starts showing figures that aren't there — and the episode lands three anchors: [[Russ]]'s 1991-sister tragedy becomes load-bearing, [[Elias Mercer]] stops being a name in old papers and becomes a *previous user* of the dungeon, and the party crosses into [[Floor 2 — The Works|Floor 2]] proper. Wesley fingerprint level: **none** — the Mirror predates him by decades.

> [!warning] Featured Rival — open decision
> Written originally as [[Eddie Park]]'s **debut**. He now debuts in [[Episode 4 — The Crossing|Ep4]], so that framing is stale. Two live options, decided in [[Episodes]]:
> - **Eddie again (Eps 4–5).** Precedent exists ([[Ricky Vance|Ricky]] ran Eps 1–2), and the mansion library annex already routes through him at no cost. Downside: three of the first five episodes are witness work.
> - **[[Zander Pruitt]] debuts here.** The shape-variety rule wants his debut to be something other than a fourth interview, and this is a cursed-object episode with a research spine — rival investigator or competitor fits cleanly. Downside: the annex/Eddie thread needs re-routing.
>
> The dispositions table below assumes Eddie; rewrite it if Zander takes the slot.

> [!note] The party is already holding a mirror
> They have carried an **ornate silver hand mirror since Session 5** — see [[Undeclared Recoveries]]. [[Panels]] is still unvisited as of Session 5, so the episode can open either through Russ or through the object already in their pack.

## The monster
[[The Mercer Mirror]] — on display in [[The Mercer Mansion]] for decades, sold at an '83 [[Mercer Falls Historical Society|Society]] auction, cycled through estate sales, bought cheap by [[Russ]] in '89. Recently it shows figures not in the room and moves when unwatched. A kid who looked too long has nightmares of the figure walking *out* toward him.

> [!danger] DM-only
> The thing in the mirror is an **Order-touched echo from one of [[Elias Mercer|Mercer]]'s earliest experiments** — older than [[Wesley Crane]] entirely (see [[Order and Chaos]]). A [[Cosmology Seeds|cosmology seed]]: the first material connection between the PCs' weird problems and the Mercer family specifically. Mercer-as-previous-user is months from legibility, but the threads are weaving.

## The twin, and the dungeon half

**The mirror is one of a pair.** Its twin hangs in [[The Long Gallery]] — room 8 of [[Floor 2 — The Works]], on Branch A past the major puzzle. The gremlins have barricaded the approach with scrap and will not go near it.

**What the twin shows depends on whether the party has been to Panels yet:**

> [!danger] Before the bridge exists
> The Long Gallery is a fragment of [[The Mercer Mansion]] interior rendered in dungeon stone — wallpaper in bas-relief, furniture-shaped rock. The twin shows **the same room as the mansion drawing room**: furnished, papered, gas-lit, warm. The party appears in the reflection standing *in that room*, in their own clothes, movements matching. At the far end of the reflected room, past where the real room ends, **a figure stands with its back turned**, and it closes distance every time someone looks away and back. It never moves while watched.
>
> This leaks nothing about [[Panels]] — important, because a window onto Russ's back room would tell [[Wesley Crane|Wesley]] exactly where the twin is. What it *does* do is hand the party their first hard material link to [[Elias Mercer]], which should send them at the [[Mercer Falls Historical Society]] and the mansion annex.

> [!danger] After the bridge opens
> The reflection **changes** — the drawing room is gone and they are looking into Russ's back room. **The switch is the reveal.** A window that was always there would be scenery; a window that *becomes* something else is the moment the two halves of the episode snap together.

**The boss arena is [[The Black Rotunda]]** (room 9, immediately past the Long Gallery) — where the echo has been taking its victims. Translucent black floor, a reverse-gravity chamber beneath it, and flip panels that let combatants cross between two boards sharing one footprint. The victims stand *under* the glass and read as reflections until someone notices they're moving independently. Required rulings so the fight can't stall are in [[Floor 2 — The Works]].

**Route:** Floor 1 → Down-Stairs → [[The Tollgate]] → [[The Wash]] → ... → [[The Door Machine]] → Branch A. The party will have seen the first two rooms during Ep4, so this is their first real push into [[The Gremlins|gremlin]] territory. If they hold the Floor-2 brass tag by now, the [[The Spiral Stair Room]] hatch shortcut skips the Floor-1 ring entirely.

## Town investigation
Russ knows the mirror is weird but hasn't acted. Investigation surfaces the **auction provenance** (a [[Mercer Falls Historical Society]] thread to pull), the nightmare-kid, and the mirror's volatility — and naturally pulls the PCs to the **library annex** inside the old mansion to research Mercer-family materials, where they meet [[Eddie Park]]. The provenance thread is also where the **pair** surfaces: the Society's auction record should list two, and only one was sold.

## Russ's 1991-sister anchor lands here
This is where Russ becomes more than a friendly shopkeeper. Investigating the mirror's history opens conversations about loss and the town's past. **Russ's older sister Annie was one of the seven who died in the [[The 1991 Mill Incident|1991 mill incident]]** — she worked the line; she was 24, Russ 22. Her death is why he stayed in Mercer Falls. Not secret, just rarely discussed; a PC on the right thread can hear it. Russ is now bonded to the campaign's underlying tragedy — he half-knows what happened — which begins seeding the eventual [[The Reality Filter|Filter]]-break.

**Sharpened by Ep4.** By now the party knows the 1991 dead left **seven permanent white prints** in and around [[The Mill]], and that white means *dead, and there's a ghost*. Annie is one of those seven. Nobody has ever separated one out. Do not connect it for them.

## Eddie's disposition impact
*(Assumes Eddie holds the slot — see the open decision above. His starting disposition is no longer 2/Neutral; carry forward whatever Ep4 set.)*
A PC who finds Eddie at the library annex while researching Mercer materials:
- **Genuine peer treatment, sustained engagement** → up one step
- **Uses Eddie as a research-source without a bond** → no movement; Wesley's "they just needed the library access" line lands easily
- **Talks down to him / treats him as a kid** → down one step
- **Doesn't engage** → no movement

[[Wesley Crane]] counter-cultivates either way. See [[The Negotiation System]]. *(Note: Eddie is unknowingly adjacent to Mercer's sealed papers in the annex — a late-game artifact.)*

## Resolution
**Destroy** the mirror (Russ's preference, easiest), **seal** it (Eddie can research how), or **extract** what's inside back to the dungeon — careful handling lets the PCs keep it as a high-value, dangerous magic item ([[Gear and Items]]). **The twin complicates all three:** destroying the Panels mirror while its pair still hangs on Floor 2 is not obviously a solution, and the party should be allowed to discover that the hard way.

## Aftermath & seeds
- [[Ren Sołtys|Ren]] **wakes up in this episode** and finds nine days with nothing in them. That, not resentment, is what sends her to Wesley.
- Mercer becomes a *person who was here first*. The mansion, the annex, the sealed papers, and the register from Ep4 all start pointing the same direction.
- The gremlins' terror of rooms 8 and 9 is a standing tell: whatever is down there is older than them and not theirs.
